Abstract¶

Governing both mechanisms are Jost’s axiomatic CPT theorem (\(\Theta = CPT\) anti-unitary involution) [Jost, 1957] and Sakharov’s baryogenesis conditions [Sakharov, 1967]: because \(\Theta\) commutes with any local Lorentz-invariant Hamiltonian, particles and antiparticles must share exact equilibrium rest masses, \(CP\) violation is strictly equivalent to \(T\) violation, and any matter–antimatter asymmetry produced via CP-violating Higgs interactions must occur out of thermal equilibrium.

We formalize this entire chain in Lean 4 (Signals.YangMillsHiggsCPT), Rust/PyO3 (yang_mills_higgs_cpt.rs), and SymPy/45-digit mpmath, while conducting an explicit epistemic audit of Daniel Toupin’s 2026 PhilArchive preprint [Toupin, 2026] and monograph Chapter 11 [Toupin, 2025] alongside our Signals.SatoGrassmannian.RHGaps and RiemannSpectralBridge framework.

\[ \langle B\rangle_\beta = \sum_{k=1}^n \Bigl(B_k^+ e^{-\beta E_k^+} + (-B_k^+) e^{-\beta E_k^-}\Bigr) = 0, \]
