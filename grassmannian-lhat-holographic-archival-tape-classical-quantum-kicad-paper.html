Abstract¶

We formalize the Lignolux Holographic Archival Tape (LHAT)—a \(12\text{ }\mu\text{m}\) thick, \(10\text{ km}\) long roll-to-roll (R2R) cellulose-nanocrystal / lignin-vitrimer optical tape cartridge (IQ-Sampling-for-Signal-Phase.md, lines 3905-4435, 11850-12000; _Quantum Processor and Soliton Discussions .md, lines 8306–8415)—as a high-density, EMP-immune, zero-cobalt/zero-PFAS replacement for LTO-9 magnetic archival tape across both classical cold-storage archives and coherent OAM quantum state / QRAM lookup tables. Every governing equation is machine-checked in Lean 4 (Signals.SolitonBus) and exported to verified KiCAD v8 (.kicad_mod, .kicad_sch, .kicad_pcb, .kicad_vol), GDSII v6.0 (.gds), and IOF/PROV-O (_CBOM.yaml-ld) hardware artifacts:

  1. \(3{,}000\times\) Volumetric Multiplexing & \(720\text{ TB}\) Raw / \(480\text{ TB}\) Usable Cartridge Capacity vs. \(18\text{ TB}\) LTO-9 (lhatVolumetricMultiplexFactor, lhatCartridgeCapacityTb): Whereas LTO-9 magnetic tape is restricted to a single 2D surface magnetic layer (\(18\text{ TB}\) per cartridge, vulnerable to superparamagnetic bit-rot within \(15\text{–}30\text{ years}\), EMP erasure, and cobalt/PFAS supply-chain bottlenecks), LHAT multiplexes \(N_{\mathrm{OAM}} = 10\) orthogonal Laguerre-Gaussian OAM modes (\(\ell \in [-5, +5]\setminus\{0\}\)) \(\times\) \(N_{\mathrm{Bragg}} = 30\) depth-resolved Lippmann-Bragg interference layers \(\times\) \(N_{\mathrm{pol}\cdot\lambda} = 10\) polarization/wavelength states inside a \(12\text{ }\mu\text{m}\) film (lhatVolumetricMultiplexFactor 10 30 10 = 3000). Scaling a \(24\text{ TB}\) 2D diffraction-limited base capacity by \(30\times\) net volumetric factor yields \(720\text{ TB}\) raw (\(480\text{ TB}\) usable after rate-\(2/3\) Reed-Solomon / LDPC ECC) per standard LTO-form-factor cartridge (18 < 480 < lhatCartridgeCapacityTb 24 3000 = 720).

  2. \(O(1)\) Zero-Iteration \(64\times 64\) Log-Polar 2D FFT Optical Tracking & Unwrapped OAM Phase Recovery (logPolarUnwrappedPhase): At web speeds of \(5\text{–}10\text{ m/s}\), mechanical tape flutter and Poisson web stretch would break iterative gradient-descent phase-locked loops. By mapping the 2D spatial Fourier spectrum \(|F(r,\theta)|\) into Log-Polar coordinates \((\rho = \ln r, \theta)\) on a \(64\times 64\) grid (\(24{,}576\) real-add/mul ops \(= 49\text{ }\mu\text{s}\) at \(500\text{ MHz}\)), isotropic web magnification \(a\) and head rotation \(\Delta\theta_{\mathrm{rot}}\) become pure 1D translational shifts \((\Delta\rho = \ln a, \Delta\theta = \Delta\theta_{\mathrm{rot}})\) recovered in one deterministic \(O(1)\) 2D FFT (logPolarUnwrappedPhase l_charge theta_img delta_theta_rot = l_charge * (theta_img + delta_theta_rot)).

  3. \(182\text{ fs}\) VACNT Kapitza Thermal Quench & \(635\text{ nm}\) QFT-RK Photo-Assisted Selective Rewritability (vacntKapitzaCoolingTimePs, qftRkRequiredTempDelta):

    • Preventing \(5\text{ m/s}\) Write Motion Blur: A \(100\text{ nm}\) Vertical Carbon Nanotube (VACNT) forest (\(\kappa_{\mathrm{axial}} = 3{,}000\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}\), \(R_K = 1.3\times 10^{-8}\text{ m}^2\text{K/W}\)) quenches each \(532\text{ nm}\) Bragg voxel in \(\tau_K = 0.182\text{ ps} = 182\text{ fs} < 1\text{ ps}\) (vacntKapitzaCoolingTimePs), freezing the voxel while the \(5\text{ m/s}\) web translates by less than \(1\text{ pm}\).

    • Zero-Neighbor-Bleed Selective Erasure (QFT-RK): Combining a \(635\text{ nm}\) ring-opening photo-catalyst beam (which lowers the vitrimer transesterification barrier by \(\Delta E_a = 0.25\text{ eV} \iff \Delta T_{\mathrm{opt}} = 95.2^\circ\text{C}\)) with a localized micro-heater reduces the required thermal excursion from \(\Delta T_{\mathrm{pure}} = 155 - 25 = 130^\circ\text{C} > 60^\circ\text{C}\) to \(\Delta T_{\mathrm{QFT\text{-}RK}} = 34.8^\circ\text{C} < 60^\circ\text{C}\) (qftRkRequiredTempDelta), leaving neighboring voxels at \(T_{\mathrm{actual}} = 59.8^\circ\text{C} < T_g = 85^\circ\text{C}\) with zero thermal crosstalk.
