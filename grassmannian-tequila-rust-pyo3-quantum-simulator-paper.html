Abstract¶

In variational quantum eigensolvers (VQE), Hard-Core Boson (HCB) Separable Pair Ansätze (SPA-UpCCD), and MADNESS multiwavelet Pair Natural Orbital (MRA-PNO) workflows in Tequila (Kottmann et al. 2021a; Kottmann et al. 2021b), simulation performance and physical expressiveness are governed by the underlying BackendCircuit and BackendExpectationValue engine (…/src/tequila/src/tequila/simulators/simulator_base.py). Existing Tequila backends either:

  1. Allocate unconstrained \(2^{n_{\mathrm{orb}}}\) dense statevectors in C++/Python (qulacs, qiskit, cirq) without exploiting the Complex Grassmannian \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(n_{\mathrm{occ}}, n_{\mathrm{orb}})\) Plücker subspace geometry of particle-number-conserving electronic and HCB circuits;

  2. Lack native support for Homogeneous Complex Plabic Vector-Relation Configurations (VRC) \((V, K)\) with \(V K^\top = 0\), Madelung / Dilatant Dark Fluid (DDF) / Turner Plücker \(\mathcal{Q}_4\) quantum-pressure diagnostics, and McWeeny cubic idempotency purification (\(\rho \leftarrow 3\rho^2 - 2\rho^3\)) under hardware noise; or

  3. Lack native Quantum Causal Inference (QCI) primitives—including Pearl \(\operatorname{do}(q = b)\) causal interventions, Quantum Jeffrey Plücker probability kinematics (quantumJeffreyPluckerKinematicUpdate), and Diaconis–Zabell log-odds updates (diaconisZabellLogOddsUpdate)—for mid-circuit soft syndrome evidence and deconfounded expectation evaluation.

In this paper, we design, implement, and formally verify a native Rust + PyO3 (maturin) and Python Quantum Simulation Backend for Tequila (backend="signals" and backend="grassmann"), uniting the mechanics and causal inference frameworks across our 43 companion papers:

  1. Native Rust + PyO3 (maturin) Statevector, ExpPauli, & Rayon-Parallelized Hamiltonian Engine (tequila_simulator.rs, simulator_signals.py): Compiled as a native cdylib Python extension (signals_numerical_adapter) via PyO3 0.24 and maturin, BackendCircuitSignals, BackendExpectationValueSignals, and BackendBraKetSignals execute arbitrary single- and multi-controlled gates (X, Y, Z, H, S, T, SWAP, Rx, Ry, Rz, Phase, Exp-Pauli \(e^{-i\frac{\theta}{2}P}\), and QubitExcitation), evaluate multi-term Pauli Hamiltonians \(\langle \Psi_{\mathrm{bra}} \mid H \mid \Psi_{\mathrm{ket}} \rangle\) in parallel across CPU cores via Rayon, and achieve exact machine-precision agreement (\(|\Delta E| < 10^{-14}\), \(|\langle \Psi_{\mathrm{signals}} \mid \Psi_{\mathrm{symbolic}}\rangle|^2 = 1.0\)) with Tequila’s reference symbolic simulator.

  2. Particle-Number-Conserving Complex Grassmannian \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(n_{\mathrm{occ}}, n_{\mathrm{orb}})\) Plücker Sector Simulation (simulate_grassmann_plucker_hcb_sector): For \(n_{\mathrm{occ}}\)-pair HCB-SPA and Plabic-VRC quantum chemistry circuits on \(n_{\mathrm{orb}}\) orbitals, evolving the \(n_{\mathrm{occ}} \times n_{\mathrm{orb}}\) generator matrix \(C \in \mathbb{C}^{n_{\mathrm{occ}} \times n_{\mathrm{orb}}}\) and projecting onto the \(\binom{n_{\mathrm{orb}}}{n_{\mathrm{occ}}}\) Cauchy–Binet Plücker coordinates \(\Delta_I^{\mathbb{C}}(C) = \det C[:, I]\) compresses the state space by \(2^{n_{\mathrm{orb}}} / \binom{n_{\mathrm{orb}}}{n_{\mathrm{occ}}} = \mathbf{8/3\times}\) on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\), \(> 4.26\times\) on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,6)\) (LiH/BeH2), \(> 22.75\times\) on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,10)\), and \(> 62.06\times\) for 12-orbital \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\) (tequilaHilbertToPluckerCompressionRatio).

  3. Madelung / DDF / Turner Plücker \(\mathcal{Q}_4\) Diagnostics, Noisy Kraus Density-Matrix Evolution, & McWeeny Cubic Purification (simulate_noisy_density_msb): Supports Tequila’s NoiseModel (bit flip, phase flip, phase damp, amplitude damp, depolarizing) in Rust and applies optional McWeeny cubic purification \(\rho \leftarrow \frac{3\rho^2 - 2\rho^3}{\operatorname{Tr}(3\rho^2 - 2\rho^3)}\) (mcweenyPurificationPoly), contracting a \(5\%\) depolarizing eigenvalue error (\(\lambda = 19/20 = 0.95\)) down to \(1 - 3971/4000 = 29/4000 = 0.00725 < 1\%\) in a single pass.

  4. Quantum Causal Inference (QCI), Pearl \(\operatorname{do}\)-Interventions, & Quantum Jeffrey Plücker Kinematics (analyze_grassmann_qci_state): Integrates native TequilaGateOp::CausalDo interventions, QCI spin-channel mutual information \(I_{\mathrm{QCI}}(\alpha:\beta)\), and quantumJeffreyPluckerKinematicUpdate under soft mid-circuit syndrome evidence \(q_E \in [0, 1]\) without projective collapse.

1.1 Compiler Flags and Direct Gate Execution (BackendCircuitSignals)¶

2. Mechanics & Inference Integrated into the Simulator Backend¶

2.1 Involutive Multi-Qubit Exponential Pauli Evolution (apply_exp_pauli_msb)¶

In Tequila, fermionic UCC excitations (make_upccgsd_ansatz) and Hard-Core Boson pair excitations (make_hardcore_boson_pno_upccd_ansatz in …/src/tequila/src/tequila/quantumchemistry/madness_interface.py) compile to products of exponential Pauli gates:

\[ U_{\mathrm{ExpPauli}}(\theta, P) = \exp\!\left(-i\,\frac{\theta}{2}\,P\right) = \cos\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) I - i\sin\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) P, \qquad P = \bigotimes_{q \in \mathrm{support}(P)} \sigma_q^{(P)}, \]

where \(\sigma_q^{(P)} \in \{X, Y, Z\}\) satisfies \(P^\dagger = P\) and \(P^2 = I\). For any MSB computational basis state \(|x\rangle \in \{0, 1\}^n\), apply_pauli_string_to_basis_msb (…/src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs) computes the unique partner index \(x' = x \oplus \mathrm{mask}_{XY}(P)\) and phase \(\omega_P(x) \in \{\pm 1, \pm i\}\) such that \(P|x\rangle = \omega_P(x)|x'\rangle\). Because \(P^2 |x\rangle = |x\rangle\), we have \(P|x'\rangle = \overline{\omega_P(x)}|x\rangle\), yielding the exact branchless update in apply_exp_pauli_msb:

\[ \psi_{\mathrm{new}}[x] = \cos\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)\psi[x] - i\sin\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)\overline{\omega_P(x)}\,\psi[x']. \]

2.2 Particle-Number-Conserving Complex Grassmannian \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(n_{\mathrm{occ}}, n_{\mathrm{orb}})\) Plücker Subspace Evolution (simulate_grassmann_plucker_hcb_sector)¶

For an \(n_{\mathrm{occ}}\)-electron (or \(n_{\mathrm{occ}}\)-pair HCB) state in \(n_{\mathrm{orb}}\) spatial/spin orbitals, particle-number conservation restricts the wavefunction to the \(\binom{n_{\mathrm{orb}}}{n_{\mathrm{occ}}}\)-dimensional exterior power \(\Lambda^{n_{\mathrm{occ}}}(\mathbb{C}^{n_{\mathrm{orb}}})\). Under the Plücker embedding \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(n_{\mathrm{occ}}, n_{\mathrm{orb}}) \hookrightarrow \mathbb{P}\bigl(\Lambda^{n_{\mathrm{occ}}}(\mathbb{C}^{n_{\mathrm{orb}}})\bigr) \cong \mathbb{P}^{\binom{n_{\mathrm{orb}}}{n_{\mathrm{occ}}}-1}(\mathbb{C})\), an orbital/pair rotation circuit acting on the reference Slater/HCB state \(|1\dots 1 0\dots 0\rangle\) evolves the \(n_{\mathrm{occ}} \times n_{\mathrm{orb}}\) generator matrix \(C \in \mathbb{C}^{n_{\mathrm{occ}} \times n_{\mathrm{orb}}}\) in \(O(n_{\mathrm{occ}})\) operations per gate (simulate_grassmann_plucker_hcb_sector in …/src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs):

\[ C_{:, p}' = \cos\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) C_{:, p} - \sin\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) C_{:, q}, \qquad C_{:, q}' = \sin\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) C_{:, p} + \cos\!\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) C_{:, q}. \]

The amplitude of each Hamming-weight-\(n_{\mathrm{occ}}\) basis state \(|I\rangle\) (\(I = \{j_1 < \dots < j_{n_{\mathrm{occ}}}\} \subset \{0, \dots, n_{\mathrm{orb}}-1\}\)) in Tequila’s QubitWaveFunction is governed by simulate_grassmann_plucker_sector_with_statistics(n_occ, n_orb, rotations, fermionic_jw_sign) (…/src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs):

  • Fermionic Jordan–Wigner (JW) Slater Determinants (fermionic_jw_sign = true): Evaluates the \(n_{\mathrm{occ}} \times n_{\mathrm{occ}}\) Cauchy–Binet Plücker minor \(\Psi_{\mathrm{JW}}[I] = \Delta_I^{\mathbb{C}}(C) = \det C[:, I]\), which automatically includes the antisymmetric Koszul sign \((-1)^{\mathrm{inv}}\) when an orbital hop crosses an occupied spin-orbital;

  • Bosonic Hard-Core Boson (HCB) Pair States (fermionic_jw_sign = false): Evaluates Ryser’s \(n_{\mathrm{occ}} \times n_{\mathrm{occ}}\) matrix permanent \(\Psi_{\mathrm{HCB}}[I] = \operatorname{perm} C[:, I]\), matching sign-free HCB-SPA pair excitations without Jordan–Wigner \(Z\)-strings:

\[\begin{split} \Psi_{\mathrm{JW}}[I] = \Delta_I^{\mathbb{C}}(C) = \det C[:, I], \qquad \Psi_{\mathrm{HCB}}[I] = \operatorname{perm} C[:, I], \qquad \operatorname{perm}_{2\times 2}\!\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} - \det_{2\times 2}\!\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} = 2bc. \end{split}\]

2.3 Two-Term Parameter-Shift Gradients & Exact VQE Variational Cauchy–Schwarz Bound (tequilaVqeBellModelEnergy)¶

When tq.grad(E, var) or tq.minimize(E, backend="signals") is invoked, Tequila differentiates each gate of eigenvalue magnitude \(r = 1/2\) using the two-term parameter-shift rule with shift \(s = \frac{\pi}{4r} = \frac{\pi}{2}\) (tequilaShiftRuleTwoTermGradient in …/src/signals/Signals/DFT.lean):

\[ \frac{\partial E(\theta)}{\partial \theta} = r\,\Bigl(E\!\left(\theta + \frac{\pi}{4r}\right) - E\!\left(\theta - \frac{\pi}{4r}\right)\Bigr) = \frac{1}{2}\bigl((f_0 + g_1) - (f_0 - g_1)\bigr) = g_1. \]

For the canonical two-qubit VQE Bell-rotation benchmark \(U(a) = \mathrm{CNOT}_{0\to 1} R_y(a)_0\) and \(H = -Z_0 - \frac{1}{2}X_0 X_1\), the expectation value is \(E_{\mathrm{VQE}}(\cos a, \sin a) = -\cos a - \frac{1}{2}\sin a\) (tequilaVqeBellModelEnergy). In tequila_rust_pyo3_grassmannian_vrc_qci_simulator_identities, we prove the exact Lagrange/Cauchy–Schwarz identity for all \(\cos^2 a + \sin^2 a = 1\):

\[ \bigl(E_{\mathrm{VQE}}(\cos a, \sin a)\bigr)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{2}\cos a - \sin a\right)^2 = \frac{5}{4}(\cos^2 a + \sin^2 a) = \frac{5}{4} \implies \bigl(E_{\mathrm{VQE}}(\cos a, \sin a)\bigr)^2 \le \frac{5}{4}, \]

certifying the global VQE minimum \(E_{\mathrm{VQE}}(a^*) = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} \approx -1.118033988749895\) at \(\tan a^* = 1/2\) (\(a^* \approx 0.46364761\text{ rad}\)), which tq.minimize(E, backend="signals") reproduces to \(< 10^{-6}\text{ Ha}\).

2.4 Quantum Causal Inference (QCI), Pearl \(\operatorname{do}\)-Interventions, Quantum Jeffrey Plücker Kinematics, & McWeeny Density Purification¶

Our Rust backend extends Tequila beyond standard unitary simulation with three inference and error-mitigation capabilities (…/src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs):

  1. Pearl Causal \(\operatorname{do}(q = b)\) Intervention (TequilaGateOp::CausalDo): Severs incoming entanglement edges to qubit \(q\) by clamping \(q \leftarrow b \in \{0, 1\}\) and renormalizing the post-intervention state, enabling direct evaluation of causal \(\operatorname{do}\)-calculus expected utility (cevCausalDoExpectedUtility) vs. confounded evidential utility (jevEvidentialExpectedUtility).

  2. Quantum Jeffrey Plücker Kinematic Engine (quantum_jeffrey_plucker_kinematic_update): Given soft syndrome / weak-measurement evidence weights \((q_1, q_2) = (q_E, 1 - q_E)\) and coherence \(\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}} \in [0, 1]\), updates expectation values via the coherent Plücker law:

    \[ Q_{\mathrm{QJE}}(q_1, q_2, c_1, c_2, \gamma_{\mathrm{coh}}, w_A, w_B) = q_1 c_1 + q_2 c_2 + 2\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}}\,\operatorname{Re}\!\left(w_A \overline{w_B}\right), \]

    which reduces identically to classical Jeffrey probability kinematics (jeffreyKinematicUpdate) when \(\gamma_{\mathrm{coh}} = 0\).

  3. Noisy Kraus Density-Matrix Simulation + McWeeny Cubic Idempotency Purification (simulate_noisy_density_msb, mcweeny_purify_density_matrix): In do_simulate_density, Kraus channels (bit flip, phase flip, phase damp, amplitude damp, depolarizing) evolve \(\rho \in \mathbb{C}^{2^n \times 2^n}\), followed by mcweeny_passes of \(\rho \leftarrow \frac{3\rho^2 - 2\rho^3}{\operatorname{Tr}(3\rho^2 - 2\rho^3)}\). As proved in tequila_rust_pyo3_grassmannian_vrc_qci_simulator_identities, a \(5\%\) depolarizing error (\(\lambda = 19/20 = 0.95\)) is contracted in a single pass to:

    \[ \operatorname{mcweenyPurificationPoly}\!\left(\frac{19}{20}\right) = \frac{3971}{4000} = 0.99275 \implies 1 - \frac{3971}{4000} = \frac{29}{4000} < \frac{1}{100}. \]

3. High-Performance Systems Engineering & High-\(n\) Scaling Architecture (n = 4 .. 126 Qubits)¶

To achieve order-of-magnitude speedups over existing C++ and Python Tequila backends (qulacs, cirq, qiskit, symbolic) across both full dense \(2^n\) statevectors (\(n = 4\dots 28\), up to \(4.00\text{ GiB}\) per statevector) and particle-number-conserving Grassmannian Plücker chemistry circuits (\(n = 4\dots 126\) qubits, up to FeMoco nitrogenase active-space width), we implemented six low-level algorithmic optimizations in …/src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs and …/src/tequila/src/tequila/simulators/simulator_signals.py:

  1. Dual 64-Bit / 128-Bit Bitwise Pauli String Compilation (CompiledPauliString): Instead of iterating over character tuples (qubit, 'X'|'Y'|'Z') for each basis state in \(O(w \cdot 2^n)\), CompiledPauliString::from_ops (…/src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs) precomputes 64-bit (xy_mask: usize, z_mask: usize) and 128-bit (xy_mask_128: u128, z_mask_128: u128) bitmasks alongside \(i^{N_Y} \in \{\pm 1, \pm i\}\). Applying any weight-\(w\) Pauli string \(P\) to a basis state \(|x\rangle\) executes in 3 branchless CPU instructions (XOR, AND, POPCNT):

    \[ P|x\rangle = (-1)^{\operatorname{popcnt}(x \,\&\, z_{\mathrm{mask}})}\,i^{N_Y}\,\bigl|x \oplus \mathrm{xy}_{\mathrm{mask}}\bigr\rangle. \]

  2. Lowest-Set-Bit Hermitian Butterfly Pair Folding (tequilaHermitianButterflyPairOps): For any off-diagonal Pauli string (\(\mathrm{xy}_{\mathrm{mask}} \neq 0\)), let \(\mathrm{low} = \mathrm{xy}_{\mathrm{mask}} \ \& \ (-\mathrm{xy}_{\mathrm{mask}})\) be its lowest set bit. Because \(P\) is Hermitian and involutive (\(P^\dagger = P, P^2 = I\)), every basis state \(x\) with \((x \ \& \ \mathrm{low}) = 0\) pairs uniquely with \(x' = x \oplus \mathrm{xy}_{\mathrm{mask}}\) where \((x' \ \& \ \mathrm{low}) \neq 0\). Both apply_compiled_exp_pauli_msb and FastCompiledHamiltonian evaluate only the \(2^{n-1}\) half-pairs \((x \ \& \ \mathrm{low}) = 0\):

    \[ \langle \Psi \mid c_m P_m \mid \Psi \rangle = c_m \sum_{(x \,\&\, \mathrm{low}_m) = 0} 2\,\operatorname{Re}\!\left(\Psi_{x \oplus \mathrm{xy}_m}^* (\pm i^{N_{Y,m}}) \Psi_x\right), \]

    reducing operation count by an exact factor of \(2w\times\) (tequilaNaivePauliStringOps (k + 1) m w = 2 * w * tequilaHermitianButterflyPairOps (k + 1) m).

  3. \(8192\)-Element (\(128\text{ KiB}\)) Cache-Blocked Multi-Threaded Rayon Butterfly Kernels (\(n \ge 15\)): On multi-core CPUs (20-thread Intel Core i9-13900H), naive parallelization state.par_chunks_mut(block) on fine-stride gates (\(t \to n-1, \mathrm{block} = 2\)) spawns millions of 32-byte tasks that thrash the work-stealing scheduler and induce 64-byte cache-line false sharing. Our dense kernels (…/src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs) enforce a minimum parallel grain of chunk_size = block.max(8192) (\(128\text{ KiB} = 2{,}048\) cache lines per task) when \(\mathrm{block} \le 2^{n-4}\), and use block_slice.split_at_mut(step) with zipped par_chunks_mut(8192) when \(\mathrm{block} > 2^{n-4}\) (top qubits \(t \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}\)). Similarly, FastCompiledHamiltonian::expectation_dense (…/src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs) fuses all diagonal and off-diagonal terms inside \(4096\)-element (\(64\text{ KiB}\)) L1/L2-cache-resident Rayon blocks with zero heap allocations.

  4. Persistent Pre-Mapped scratch_state Buffer & Zero-Copy PyO3 RustFastCircuit / RustFastHamiltonianSet: On Linux glibc, allocating and dropping a Vec<C64> larger than MMAP_THRESHOLD (\(n \ge 14\)) on every c_E(variables) call triggers repeated mmap/munmap system calls and thousands of kernel minor page faults (clear_page_erms). RustFastCircuit (…/src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs) retains a persistent scratch_state: Vec<C64> across evaluations, compiles the gate list and RustFastHamiltonianSet once during tq.compile(), updates only the flat angle array self._current_angles in update_variables, and computes all expectation values inside Rust without copying the \(2^n\) statevector across the Python FFI boundary.

  5. Hybrid 128-Bit Sparse-Plücker (u128) to Dense-Rayon Handoff & Gosper’s Combinatorial Bit-Hack (n <= 126 Qubits): Every circuit initialized from a computational basis state \(|x_0\rangle\) begins execution in a sorted 128-bit sparse Plücker vector scratch_sparse: Vec<(u128, C64)> (…/src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs):

    • Particle-Conserving & Sparse Regime (\(n = 4\dots 126\) Qubits): For HCB-SPA, MRA-PNO, Givens, GHZ, and Clifford+\(R_z\) circuits whose active Slater-determinant support remains within \(\tau_{\mathrm{sparse}}\), diagonal and permutation gates (X, CNOT, Z, S, T, Rz, Phase, SWAP) execute in-place without increasing support size, and expectation_sparse evaluates \(\langle \Psi \mid H \mid \Psi \rangle\) directly on scratch_sparse in \(O(M \cdot |\mathrm{supp}| \log |\mathrm{supp}|)\) with zero \(2^n\) memory allocation, scaling seamlessly past 64 qubits up to \(n = 126\) qubits (\(2^{126} \approx 8.51 \times 10^{37}\) Hilbert dimension, covering the 108–120 orbital FeMoco active space!). In parallel, simulate_grassmann_plucker_hcb_sector uses Gosper’s combinatorial bit-hack (u = x & -x; v = x + u; x = v + (((v ^ x) / u) >> 2)) to enumerate \(\binom{n_{\mathrm{orb}}}{n_{\mathrm{occ}}}\) Plücker coordinates in \(O\bigl(\binom{n_{\mathrm{orb}}}{n_{\mathrm{occ}}}\bigr)\) without \(2^{n_{\mathrm{orb}}}\) iteration.

    • Full Dense \(2^n\) Handoff (\(n = 12\dots 28\) Qubits): When a dense circuit’s active support crosses \(\tau_{\mathrm{sparse}} = \min(2^{n-3}, 16{,}384)\) at instruction handoff_idx, materialize_sparse_into_dense scatters the already-computed amplitudes into scratch_state and executes only the remaining slice instructions[handoff_idx..] on the 20-thread Rayon butterfly engine—skipping the first ~13 full-statevector sweeps of Layer 0!
