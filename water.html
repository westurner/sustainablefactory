Water¶

Water composition¶

  • Water is H20. Two parts Hydrogen and One part Oxygen.

  • Water has a fingerprint

    • When and where it was formed

  • Water is a solution and a mixture

    • Nitrogen

  • Water is scarce

    • The groundwater level is dropping unsustainably in many places,

    • Aquifer levels are falling,

    • Sugared water fetches more than plain water, so sugar water vendors can afford to buy water rights.

  • Water is essential for earth ecology, life on earth, and for humans.

  • Water contains contaminants that are hazardous to humans:

    • heavy metals

      • lead

      • iron

      • calcium

    • biological hazards of water

      • fungii

      • bacteria

      • viruses

    • water treatment additives

      • Cl: Chlorine

      • F: Fluoride
