Abstract¶

We formalize and synthesize Application 4 of Semi-Dirac Fermions (Etch-Free 1D Topological Majorana \(\mathrm{MZM}\) and Parafermion Qudit Waveguides) together with the complete optical and volumetric-EDA theory of varying phase during lasing to synthesize carbon-based qudits across the repository’s research corpus (IQ-Sampling-for-Signal-Phase.md, lines 7801-7949, 8615-8740, 9513-9560, 9873-9914; _Laser-Engraving-circuits-on-Lignin-and-other-materials.md, lines 600-830; gemini_google-gemini_2026-01-25T19-38-50-0500.md, lines 2503-2585, 3228-3340; Bell-Correlations-in-Atomic-Momentum.md, lines 1073-1325; Google-Search_Density-Functional-Theory_20261105-1512.md, lines 2680-3120). Every identity is machine-checked in Lean 4 (Signals.Nanolithography, Signals.DFT), implemented in Rust/WASM (cannon_mask_cad.rs) and Python (SemiDiracAndSU4WittenUnificationEngine), and exported to verified KiCAD v8 (.kicad_mod, .kicad_sch, .kicad_pcb), volumetric (.kicad_vol), Calma GDSII v6.0 (.gds), and IOF/EMMO/PROV-O (_CBOM.yaml-ld) artifacts:

  1. Etch-Free 1D Semi-Dirac Topological Waveguide Confinement & \(100\times\) Sidewall \(D_{\mathrm{it}}\) Suppression (semiDiracTransverseGroupVelocityX, semiDiracEtchFreeCoherenceGain): In uniaxially strained rhombohedral trilayer (RTG on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,6)\)) or pentalayer (RPG on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,10)\)) graphene hosting intrinsic SC1/SC2 superconductivity, the transverse group velocity along the massive \(\hat{x}\) axis vanishes at the semi-Dirac node (\(v_{g,x}(0, m_x^*) = 0\)) while the massless \(\hat{y}\) width remains dispersionless (\(\sigma_y^2(t) = \sigma_{y,0}^2\)). This channels Bogoliubov quasiparticles into virtual 1D Kitaev/parafermion nanowires inside a pristine 2D sheet without reactive-ion etching (RIE), reducing sidewall dangling-bond interface trap density from \(D_{\mathrm{it,rie}} = 10^{12}\text{ cm}^{-2}\text{eV}^{-1}\) to \(D_{\mathrm{it,etchfree}} = 10^{10}\text{ cm}^{-2}\text{eV}^{-1}\) (\(\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{coh}} = 100\times\)) and preserving the \(\omega_0 = \Delta_0^2 / E_F \approx 20\text{ }\mu\text{eV}\) Caroli–de Gennes–Matricon (CdGM) vortex mini-gap.

2. Physics of Application 4: Etch-Free 1D Topological Majorana (\(\mathrm{MZM}\)) & Parafermion Waveguides¶

2.1 Why Reactive-Ion Etching (RIE) Degrades Conventional Topological Nanowires¶

In conventional 2D topological superconductor platforms (or lithographically etched graphene nanoribbons), confining Bogoliubov quasiparticles into a 1D Kitaev wire requires physical reactive-ion etching (RIE). As formalized in our Grassmannian Interfacial Process-to-Electrical (G-IEP) model (grassmannian-interfacial-electrical-properties-paper.md), RIE plasma bombardment creates a high density of dangling-bond and carbon-cluster interface traps \(D_{\mathrm{it,rie}} \sim 10^{12}\text{ cm}^{-2}\text{eV}^{-1}\) along the etched sidewalls. These sidewall traps:

  1. Degrade the gate subthreshold swing \(SS = SS_0\bigl(1 + \frac{q^2 D_{\mathrm{it}}}{C_{\mathrm{ox}}}\bigr)\), and

  2. Induce sub-gap Yu–Shiba–Rusinov / Andreev poisoning states that collapse the topological Caroli–de Gennes–Matricon (CdGM) vortex mini-gap \(\omega_0 = \frac{\Delta_0^2}{E_F} \approx 20\text{ }\mu\text{eV}\) (grassmannian-rhombohedral-graphene-superconductivity-qc-paper.md).

2.2 How Semi-Dirac Dispersion Creates Virtual 1D Nanowires Without Etching¶

When a pristine 2D rhombohedral trilayer (RTG, \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,6)\)) or pentalayer (RPG, \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,10)\)) graphene sheet is strain-locked to the semi-Dirac merging point (\(\Delta_m = 0\)) via \(532\text{ nm}\) LCS polyCBD Bragg clamps:

  1. Vanishing Transverse Group Velocity Along \(\hat{x}\) (semiDiracTransverseGroupVelocityX): Near the semi-Dirac node \(k_x \to 0\), the transverse group velocity

    \[ v_{g,x}(k_x, m_x^*) = \frac{\hbar k_x}{m_x^*} \xrightarrow{k_x \to 0} 0 \qquad \bigl(\operatorname{semiDiracTransverseGroupVelocityX}(0, m_x^*) = 0\bigr) \]

    vanishes identically, whereas longitudinal Bogoliubov modes propagate along \(\hat{y}\) at the full relativistic Dirac speed \(v_y > 0\).

  2. Dispersionless Soliton-in-\(\hat{y}\) Wavepacket Confinement (semiDiracAnisotropicSplatVarY): By Theorem semi_dirac_fermions_with_and_without_grc_and_ddf_identities (grassmannian-semi-dirac-fermions-ddf-paper.md), a 2D NormedTensorGaussianSplat2D wavepacket propagates along \(\hat{y}\) with constant variance \(\sigma_y^2(t) = \sigma_{y,0}^2\).

  3. \(100\times\) Sidewall Interface-Trap Suppression (semiDiracEtchFreeCoherenceGain): Because the 1D superconducting channel is defined purely by optical strain and electrostatic gating inside an unetched 2D crystal (\(D_{\mathrm{it,etchfree}} \le 10^{10}\text{ cm}^{-2}\text{eV}^{-1}\)), the sidewall trap coherence gain is:

    \[ \mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{coh}}\!\left(10^{12}, 10^{10}\right) = \frac{10^{12}}{10^{10}} = 100 > 1. \]

3.3 Critique, SPP Thermal-Drift Compensation (compensatedOamPhaseResidual), CGLMP Qudit Bell Witness, and Falsifiable Experimental Test Protocols¶

As noted in the chat corpus (gemini_google-gemini_2026-01-25T19-38-50-0500.md, line 2581; Bell-Correlations-in-Atomic-Momentum.md, lines 1669-1715), two critical hardware challenges must be closed and tested before claiming operational qudit superiority:

  1. Gap 32.1 — Thermal Warping of Vitrimer Spiral Phase Plates (compensatedOamPhaseResidual): A temperature excursion \(\Delta T\) across a polyCBD-SS-Lignin Spiral Phase Plate of thermal-expansion/thermo-optic coefficient \(\alpha_{\mathrm{CTE}}\) induces a parasitic azimuthal phase drift \(\delta\Phi_{\mathrm{thermal}}(\ell, \alpha_{\mathrm{CTE}}, \Delta T) = \ell\,\alpha_{\mathrm{CTE}}\,\Delta T\) that blurs integer OAM charge \(\ell\) into adjacent modes \(\ell \pm 1\). Sampling the \(532\text{ nm}\) / \(1550\text{ nm}\) pilot-tone homodyne phase at HOMODYNE_READ and applying the phase-conjugate SLM / rGO correction \(\delta\Phi_{\mathrm{corr}} = \ell\,\alpha_{\mathrm{CTE}}\,\Delta T\) cancels the thermal drift identically:

    \[ \mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{SPP}}\bigl(\ell, \alpha_{\mathrm{CTE}}, \Delta T, \ell\,\alpha_{\mathrm{CTE}}\,\Delta T\bigr) = 0. \]

    • How to Test Experimentally: Apply a controlled thermal gradient \(\Delta T \in [0, 20\text{ K}]\) across Stage 1 (1_SPP_PHASE_VARYING_LASER_IN) while imaging the output Laguerre–Gaussian ring on an InGaAs OAM mode sorter at QUDIT_BUS_OUT: verify that closing the HOMODYNE_READ phase-conjugate feedback loop suppresses inter-mode OAM crosstalk below \(-25\text{ dB}\).

  2. Gap 32.2 — Falsifiable CGLMP High-Dimensional Qudit Bell-Inequality Witness (cglmpClassicalBound, cglmpQutritQuantumBenchmark) & Etch-Free 1D \(\mathrm{MZM}\)/Parafermion Tunneling Protocol:
