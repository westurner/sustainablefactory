Abstract¶

We revise, refine, and formally verify the Lignolux / Signals.SolitonBus optical-quantum interconnect and QRAM architecture (soliton-bus.md; _Quantum Computing Hardware Architectures Review .md, lines 1–1810; Lignin-Vitrimer-Surface-Roughness-Limits.md, lines 890-1750) by integrating our newly formalized Semi-Dirac fermion components (Papers 29, 31, & 32) and Complex Grassmannian \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\) / \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,6)\) / Homogeneous Plücker \(\mathcal{Q}_4\) / Complex VRC mechanics (Papers 19 & 22). Specifically, we prove in Lean 4 (…/src/signals/Signals/SolitonBus.lean) and synthesize in Rust/WASM (cannon_mask_cad.rs) and KiCAD v8:

  1. Resolution of the 6 Classic SolitonBus Bottlenecks via Semi-Dirac & Grassmannian Mechanics:

    • Waveguide Dispersion & Sidewall Mode-Mixing: Replacing planar N-LIG ribbons with \(532\text{ nm}\) LCS strain-locked Semi-Dirac Ring-Core Waveguides and \(C_8\) Star-Shaped Vias enforces vanishing transverse spreading \(v_{g,x}(0, m_x^*) = 0\) and dispersionless longitudinal soliton width \(\sigma_y^2(t) = \sigma_{y,0}^2\) while suppressing RIE sidewall \(D_{\mathrm{it}}\) traps by \(100\times\).

    • kHz Thermo-Optic rGO Speed Bottleneck \(\to\) GHz Ballistic Semi-Dirac Klein Gates & Phase-Only \(\pi\)-Destructive SLMs: Replaces slow thermal switches with electrostatic Semi-Dirac Klein gates (\(T_y = 1\) vs. \(T_x = 1/101\), \(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{Klein}}(10) = 101\)) and phase-only \(\pi\)-destructive interference metasurfaces (zero absorbed laser heat).

    • \(\mathrm{CO}_2\)-Carbonate \(5\times\) \(\chi^{(3)}\) CPHASE / CNOT Gate Compression (co2CarbonateChi3CphaseLength): Introducing Direct-Air-Captured \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) carbonate bridges (\(-\text{O–CO–O}-\)) boosts \(n_2 \mapsto 5 n_2\), shrinking the XPM CPHASE/CNOT interaction length \(L_{\mathrm{CPHASE}} = \frac{\lambda}{2 n_2 I_{\mathrm{ctrl}}}\) to \(\frac{1}{5} L_{\mathrm{CPHASE}}\) (\(80\%\) power reduction).

  2. Slow-Light N-LIG CROW (Coupled Resonator Optical Waveguide) Delay Line (crowSlowLightDelayLength): During the \(N_{\mathrm{cycles}} = 12\) cycles (\(24\text{ ns}\) at \(f_{\mathrm{clk}} = 500\text{ MHz}\)) required for the RFSoC LUT-CORDIC decoder to compute a thermal-drift-corrected phase map, an unbuffered soliton in \(n_g = 1.5\) vitrimer travels \(L_0 = 24/5 = 4.8\text{ m}\). A Slow-Light N-LIG CROW buffer compresses the required physical delay length to \(L_{\mathrm{CROW}}(n_g = 5) = 36/25 = 1.44\text{ m}\) and \(L_{\mathrm{CROW}}(n_g = 300) = 6/250 = 0.024\text{ m} = 24\text{ mm}\) on-chip.

  3. Tiered 128-to-64-Bit QNIC RDMA Bus, LUT-Seeded CORDIC Thermal-Drift Cancellation, & \(37.5\%\) PWM 4K Cryo Budget (tieredQnicStrippedPayloadBits, lutPlusCordicOamPhase, nligInternalPumpAvgPowerMw): Stripping the 64-bit inter-rack QNIC header (32-bit Wafer/Rack ID + 16-bit Time-Bin + 16-bit Bell entanglement graph tag) at \(300\text{ K}\) leaves a 64-bit intra-QPU RDMA payload ([63:32] Spatial Via + [31:16] WDM Color + [15:0] OAM \(\ell\) & superposition) with exact LUT+CORDIC drift recovery \(\ell\theta_{\mathrm{LUT}} + \ell(\theta_{\mathrm{true}} - \theta_{\mathrm{LUT}}) = \ell\theta_{\mathrm{true}}\), while the NLIG_Power_Manager \(60\text{ }\mu\text{s} / 160\text{ }\mu\text{s} = 3/8\) (\(37.5\%\)) PWM duty cycle reduces 250-node \(1.5\text{ mA}, 500\text{ }\Omega\) internal-pump Joule dissipation from \(281.25\text{ mW} > 110\text{ mW}\) down to \(\frac{3375}{32} \approx 105.47\text{ mW} < 110\text{ mW}\).

2. Mathematical & Hardware Theory of the Revised Semi-Dirac Soliton Bus¶

2.1 Tiered 128-Bit Inter-Node QNIC to 64-Bit Intra-QPU RDMA Bus & LUT-Seeded CORDIC Decoder¶

As analyzed in Prompts 15–18 of _Quantum Computing Hardware Architectures Review .md, lines 1495–1760, a 128-bit RDMA address space (\(2^{128} \approx 3.4 \times 10^{38}\) addresses) enables global datacenter entanglement-graph routing, picosecond time-bin multiplexing, and multi-rack Wafer IDs, but routing a 128-bit CORDIC pipeline across the \(4\text{ K}\) cryostat boundary would exceed the \(110\text{ mW}\) thermal budget. The Tiered QNIC Architecture (tieredQnicStrippedPayloadBits 128 64 = 64) resolves this by partitioning the 128-bit address at the room-temperature (\(300\text{ K}\)) Quantum Network Interface Controller:

  • Upper 64 Bits (Stripped at \(300\text{ K}\) Edge QNIC):

    • Bits [127:96] (32 bits): Rack / Wafer ID for inter-node optical bypass,

    • Bits [95:80] (16 bits): Picosecond Time-Bin TDM schedule for the external Acousto-Optic Modulator,

    • Bits [79:64] (16 bits): Multi-partite Bell / surface-code entanglement graph pair tag.

  • Lower 64 Bits (Forwarded to \(4\text{ K}\) RFSoC LUT-CORDIC Decoder):

    • Bits [63:32] (32 bits): Spatial \((X, Y)\) coordinate selecting among \(2^{32} \approx 4.29 \times 10^9\) N-LIG \(C_8\) star-shaped vertical vias,

    • Bits [31:16] (16 bits): Wavelength / UCNP color channel (\(2^{16} = 65{,}536\) WDM sub-bands across \(475\text{–}1550\text{ nm}\)),

    • Bits [15:0] (16 bits): Topological OAM mode \(\ell \in [-24, +24]\) (48 modes, requiring only \(48\text{ KB}\) of BRAM for a \(32 \times 32 \times 8\text{-bit}\) base LUT) plus two-mode superposition weights.

When the \(20\text{ kHz}\) widefield CASR NMR camera detects a thermal drift \((\Delta x, \Delta y)\) of the waveguide center, the pipelined CORDIC engine computes the angular offset \(\Delta\theta_{\mathrm{CORDIC}} = \theta_{\mathrm{true}} - \theta_{\mathrm{LUT}}\), and the DSP slice outputs (lutPlusCordicOamPhase):

\[ \phi_{\mathrm{final}}(\ell, \theta_{\mathrm{LUT}}, \theta_{\mathrm{true}} - \theta_{\mathrm{LUT}}) = \ell\,\theta_{\mathrm{LUT}} + \ell\,(\theta_{\mathrm{true}} - \theta_{\mathrm{LUT}}) = \ell\,\theta_{\mathrm{true}}. \]

2.2 Slow-Light N-LIG CROW Delay Line (crowSlowLightDelayLength)¶

During the \(N_{\mathrm{cycles}} = 12\) clock cycles at \(f_{\mathrm{clk}} = 500\text{ MHz} = 0.5\text{ GHz}\) (\(t_{\mathrm{compute}} = \frac{12}{0.5} = 24\text{ ns}\)) required for the CORDIC pipeline to settle, the incoming soliton must be buffered on-chip (crowSlowLightDelayLength):

\[\begin{split} L_{\mathrm{CROW}}(N_{\mathrm{cycles}}, f_{\mathrm{clk}}, c_0, n_g) = \left(\frac{N_{\mathrm{cycles}}}{f_{\mathrm{clk}}}\right)\left(\frac{c_0}{n_g}\right) = \begin{cases} \frac{24}{5}\text{ m} = 4.80\text{ m} & (n_g = 1.5\text{ unbuffered vitrimer}), \\[4pt] \frac{36}{25}\text{ m} = 1.44\text{ m} & (n_g = 5.0\text{ moderate CROW}), \\[4pt] \frac{6}{250}\text{ m} = 0.024\text{ m} = 24\text{ mm} & (n_g = 300\text{ slow-light CROW}). \end{cases} \end{split}\]
