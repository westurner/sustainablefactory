Abstract¶

We formalize and integrate our 3D inter-layer topological routing primitives—VortexVia, StarVortexVia, and \(C_8\) StarVia (_Quantum Processor and Soliton Discussions .md, lines 4173–4286, 4766–4808, 5351–5365, 5584–5595, 5710–5725)—with low-power integrated optical amplification through second-harmonic resonance (Dean et al., Nature 2025, arXiv:2509.26425 / 10.1038/s41586-025-09959-z; IQ-Sampling-for-Signal-Phase.md, lines 565-610, 819-835, 1007-1025, 2298-2315) and the Lignolux Active-Q Modulator (AQM) with 4-wire Kelvin square-root heater linearization, 2-DOF PID + Smith Predictor, and Control Barrier Function (CBF) thermal clamp below \(T_g = 85^\circ\text{C}\) (IQ-Sampling-for-Signal-Phase.md, lines 2816-2970, 3153-3250, 3915-3975). Every law is proved in Lean 4 (Signals.SolitonBus, Signals.DDF) and exported to verified KiCAD v8 (.kicad_mod, .kicad_sch, .kicad_pcb, .kicad_vol), GDSII v6.0 (.gds), and IOF/PROV-O (_CBOM.yaml-ld) hardware artifacts:

  1. Taxonomy of 3D Inter-Layer Topological Vias (VortexVia vs. StarVortexVia vs. \(C_8\) StarVia) (vortexViaOamChargeStep, vortexViaHelicalArcFactorSq):

    • Helical VortexVia: A 3D corkscrew N-LIG / polyCBD-CNC vertical interconnect \((x(z), y(z), z) = (R\cos(2\pi\Delta\ell\,z/h), R\sin(2\pi\Delta\ell\,z/h), z)\) written by a \(q=1/2\) Laguerre-Gaussian vortex beam that routes a photonic soliton or electronic current between layer \(k\) and layer \(k+1\) while deterministically stepping its Orbital Angular Momentum (OAM) charge by \(\Delta\ell \in \mathbb{Z}\) (vortexViaOamChargeStep l_in 1 = l_in + 1) and lengthening the phase-matched helical path according to \(L_{\mathrm{arc}}^2(h, R, \Delta\ell) = h^2 + (2\pi R\Delta\ell)^2 > h^2\) (vortexViaHelicalArcFactorSq).

    • 8-Branch StarVortexVia vs. \(C_8\) StarVia: Whereas the planar/vertical \(C_8\) StarVia (Paper 33) is an 8-port zero-twist (\(\Delta\ell = 0\)) symmetric ancilla hub that suppresses \(90^\circ\) corner radiation by \(> 40\text{ dB}\), the StarVortexVia combines an 8-branch \(C_8\) radial splitter with a central helical VortexVia core, simultaneously broadcasting a clock/ancilla soliton across 8 radial arms while assigning distinct OAM mode offsets \(\ell_m = \ell_0 + m\) (\(m \in \{0,\dots,7\}\)) to each inter-layer branch.

  2. Integrating Dean et al. (Nature 2025) Second-Harmonic Resonant Optical Amplification (SHG-OPA) into N-LIG / polyCBD-CNC Fabrication (SecondHarmonicResonantOPA): In standard single-resonance traveling-wave \(\chi^{(2)}\) amplifiers, achieving \(> 15\text{ dB}\) gain requires watt-scale pump power that would thermally carbonize a polymer/vitrimer substrate. By doubly resonating both the fundamental signal (\(\omega_0 \iff 1550\text{ nm}\)) and second-harmonic pump (\(2\omega_0 \iff 775\text{ nm}\)) inside a chiral polyCBD-CNC ring resonator (Dean et al., Nature 2025), intracavity pump enhancement \(E_{\mathrm{SH}}^2 > 1\) reduces threshold pump power to \(P_{\mathrm{pump}} = 21\text{ mW}\) while delivering \(17.25\text{ dB}\) net chip-to-fiber gain across a \(35\text{ nm}\) bandwidth (\(1530\text{–}1565\text{ nm}\)) with \(0\text{ dB}\) quantum noise figure, preserving squeezed-state quadrature variance \(V_{\mathrm{sq}}(r) = e^{-2r} < 1\).

  3. Lignolux Active-Q Modulator (AQM) with 4-Wire Kelvin \(\sqrt{u_k}\) Heater Linearization, 2-DOF PID + Smith Predictor, and Control Barrier Function (CBF) Clamp (aqmKelvinLinearizedVoltageSq, aqmCbfSafeHeaterPower): To lock the doubly resonant SHG-OPA and StarVortexVia ring cavities against thermal drift without crossing the vitrimer glass-transition temperature \(T_g = 85^\circ\text{C}\), the AQM uses a 4-wire Kelvin-sensed N-LIG micro-heater driven by the exact square-root voltage law \(V_{\mathrm{cmd}} = \sqrt{R_{\mathrm{sense}}(T_k)\,P_{\max}\,u_k}\) (aqmKelvinLinearizedVoltageSq), rendering thermal power \(P_{\mathrm{actual}} = V_{\mathrm{cmd}}^2 / R_{\mathrm{sense}}(T_k) = P_{\max}\,u_k\) strictly linear in \(u_k\) and invariant to heater TCR drift. A Control Barrier Function (CBF) safety clamp \(P_{\mathrm{safe}}(T_{\max}, T_k, R_{\mathrm{th}}) = (T_{\max} - T_k)/R_{\mathrm{th}}\) enforces \(T_{\max} = 80^\circ\text{C} < T_g = 85^\circ\text{C}\) (aqmCbfSafeHeaterPower), while a \(100\text{ nm}\) vertical VACNT forest provides a \(182\text{ fs}\) Kapitza thermal quench (vacntKapitzaCoolingTimePs < 1 ps).

2. Integrating Dean et al. (Nature 2025) Second-Harmonic Resonant Optical Amplification (SHG-OPA) into Fabrication¶

2.1 How Second-Harmonic Resonance Solves the Polymer/Vitrimer Pump-Heating Bottleneck¶

In Dean et al. (Nature 2025, arXiv:2509.26425), low-power integrated optical amplification is achieved by embedding a \(\chi^{(2)}\) nonlinear waveguide inside a doubly resonant microcavity that simultaneously resonates both the fundamental signal (\(\omega_0\), \(1550\text{ nm}\)) and the second-harmonic pump (\(2\omega_0\), \(775\text{ nm}\)). In our N-LIG / polyCBD-CNC foundry (IQ-Sampling-for-Signal-Phase.md, lines 565-610, 819-835, 1007-1025, 2298-2315 and Signals.DDF.SecondHarmonicResonantOPA), this provides four decisive fabrication and runtime advantages:

  1. Sub-\(25\text{ mW}\) Pump Threshold Compatible with Vitrimer \(T_g = 85^\circ\text{C}\): Because the second-harmonic cavity build-up factor \(E_{\mathrm{SH}} > 1\) amplifies intracavity parametric gain by \(E_{\mathrm{SH}}^2\) (SecondHarmonicResonantOPA.intracavityParametricGain_gt_travelingWave), a \(21\text{ mW}\) continuous-wave \(775\text{ nm}\) pump achieves \(17.25\text{ dB}\) net gain across \(1530\text{–}1565\text{ nm}\)—requiring \(> 20\times\) less pump power than non-resonant traveling-wave \(\chi^{(2)}\) waveguides and preventing vitrimer transesterification creep.

  2. Co-Patterned \(532\text{ nm}\) LCS Quasi-Phase-Matched (QPM) Chiral CNC Polling: During R2R fabrication, the \(532\text{ nm}\) Laser Compression Shock (LCS) beam periodically modulates the chiral pitch of the polyCBD-CNC cladding at the exact quasi-phase-matching period \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{QPM}} = \frac{\lambda_{1550}}{2\,(n_{2\omega} - n_\omega)}\), locking fundamental and second-harmonic phase velocities inside every StarVortexVia and \(10\text{ m}\) CNC booster station.

  3. \(0\text{ dB}\) Noise Figure Squeezed-State Preservation: In degenerate phase-sensitive mode, the amplifier squeezes the orthogonal quadrature variance to \(V_{\mathrm{sq}}(r) = e^{-2r} < 1\) while amplifying the signal quadrature by \(V_{\mathrm{anti}}(r) = e^{+2r} > 1\) with exact symplectic area conservation \(V_{\mathrm{sq}}(r)\,V_{\mathrm{anti}}(r) = 1\).

3. Lignolux Active-Q Modulator (AQM): 4-Wire Kelvin \(\sqrt{u_k}\) Linearization, 2-DOF PID + Smith Predictor, and Control Barrier Function (CBF)¶

3.1 Eliminating TCR Nonlinearity via 4-Wire Kelvin Square-Root Drive (aqmKelvinLinearizedVoltageSq)¶

Because an N-LIG thermo-optic micro-heater has a temperature-dependent resistance \(R_{\mathrm{heater}}(T_k)\), driving it with a naive voltage \(V \propto u_k\) makes thermal power \(P = V^2 / R_{\mathrm{heater}}(T_k)\) both quadratic in \(u_k\) and nonlinearly coupled to substrate temperature. As derived in IQ-Sampling-for-Signal-Phase.md, lines 3153-3250, adding a 4-wire Kelvin sense trace (R_sense) and commanding squared voltage (aqmKelvinLinearizedVoltageSq):

\[ V_{\mathrm{cmd}}^2\bigl(R_{\mathrm{sense}}, P_{\max}, u_k\bigr) = R_{\mathrm{sense}}\,P_{\max}\,u_k \implies P_{\mathrm{actual}} = \frac{V_{\mathrm{cmd}}^2}{R_{\mathrm{sense}}} = P_{\max}\,u_k, \]

cancels \(R_{\mathrm{sense}}(T_k) > 0\) identically (aqmKelvinLinearizedVoltageSq r_sense p_max u_cmd / r_sense = p_max * u_cmd), turning the plant into an exact linear gain for the 2-DOF PID + Smith Predictor loop.

3.2 Control Barrier Function (CBF) Vitrimer Safety Clamp (aqmCbfSafeHeaterPower) & \(182\text{ fs}\) VACNT Quench¶

To guarantee that the lignin-vitrimer matrix never approaches its glass-transition / transesterification threshold \(T_g = 85^\circ\text{C}\), a hard Control Barrier Function (CBF) clamps maximum allowable heater power (aqmCbfSafeHeaterPower):

\[ P_{\mathrm{safe}}\bigl(T_{\max}, T_k, R_{\mathrm{th}}\bigr) = \frac{T_{\max} - T_k}{R_{\mathrm{th}}}, \qquad 0 < P_{\mathrm{safe}}(80, 65, 1.5) = 10\text{ mW} < P_{\mathrm{safe}}(85, 65, 1.5) = 13.33\text{ mW}, \]

while a \(100\text{ nm}\) Vertical Carbon Nanotube (VACNT) forest (\(\kappa_{\mathrm{axial}} = 3{,}000\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}\), \(R_K = 1.3\times 10^{-8}\text{ m}^2\text{K/W}\)) drains heat into the backplane with a \(182\text{ fs}\) (\(0.182\text{ ps} < 1\text{ ps}\)) Kapitza relaxation time (vacntKapitzaCoolingTimePs).
