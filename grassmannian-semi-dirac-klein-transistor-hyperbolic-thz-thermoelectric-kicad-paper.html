Abstract¶

In Paper 29 (grassmannian-semi-dirac-fermions-ddf-paper.md), we formalized semi-Dirac fermions—hybrid 2D quasiparticles that propagate as massive non-relativistic Schrödinger particles along \(\hat{x}\) (\(E = \frac{\hbar^2 k_x^2}{2 m_x^*}, m_x^* > 0\)) and massless ultra-relativistic Dirac particles along \(\hat{y}\) (\(E = \hbar v_y |k_y|, m_y^* = 0\)). In this paper (Cluster Paper A, Paper 31), we unify Applications 1, 2, and 3 from Section 3.1 of Paper 29 with our dual-beam \(405\text{ nm} / 532\text{ nm}\) holographic carbon nanolithography and volumetric KiCAD/GDSII electronic design automation (EDA) stack (grassmannian-riscv-holographic-nanolithography-kicad-paper.md), while Companion Cluster Paper B (Paper 32, grassmannian-semi-dirac-topological-qudit-waveguides-phase-lasing-kicad-paper.md) treats Application 4 and phase-varying lasing of carbon-based qudits. Specifically, we machine-check in Lean 4 (Signals.Nanolithography, Signals.DFT), synthesize in Rust/WASM (cannon_mask_cad.rs), and validate with the kicad-happy EDA analyzers:

  1. \(532\text{ nm}\) Laser Compression Shock (LCS) Uniaxial Strain-Locked Semi-Dirac Merging (semiDiracStrainMergingGap): Pulsed \(532\text{ nm}\) green Laser Compression Shock (gemini_google-gemini_2026-01-25T19-35-26-0500.md, lines 520-535; CNT Bandgap Creation with Lignin Vitrimer .md, lines 4893-4920) imprints polyCBD strain clamps (\(d_{\mathrm{Bragg,532}} = \frac{532}{2 \times 1.59} \approx 167.30\text{ nm}\)) that lock the uniaxial strain of N-LIG / rhombohedral trilayer graphene (RTG on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,6)\)) to the exact critical Dirac-cone merging threshold \(\varepsilon_{xx}^* = \Delta_0 / \beta_{\mathrm{strain}}\), where \(\Delta_m(\Delta_0, \beta_{\mathrm{strain}}, \varepsilon_{xx}^*) = 0\).

  2. Application 1 — Ballistic Directional Klein Diode & Dual-Gate Transistor (semiDiracKleinRectificationRatio, SemiDirac_App1_Ballistic_Klein_Diode_Transistor): Using \(405\text{ nm}\) radial-vortex excitation + \(532\text{ nm}\) helical-doughnut STED depletion (\(\zeta_{532} = 63\), \(w_{\mathrm{STED}} = \frac{2025}{112}\text{ nm} \approx 18.08\text{ nm} < 20\text{ nm}\)), we pattern orthogonal S-LIG electrostatic barrier gates across a semi-Dirac channel. Along \(\hat{y}\) (\(k_x = 0\)), chiral pseudospin \(\sigma_y\) conservation enforces 100% ballistic Klein transmission \(T_y = 1\) (semiDiracKleinTunnelingY), whereas along \(\hat{x}\) (\(k_y = 0\)), massive Schrödinger barrier reflection attenuates transmission to \(T_x(\kappa_x L) = \frac{1}{1 + (\kappa_x L)^2}\), achieving a directional rectification / on-off ratio \(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{Klein}}(10) = 101 > 100\) at \(\kappa_x w_{\mathrm{STED}} = 10\) without opening a mobility-degrading isotropic bulk bandgap.

  3. Application 2 — Lithography-Free In-Plane Hyperbolic Polaritons & \(400\text{–}800\text{ GHz}\) Polarization-Sensitive THz Bolometer (semiDiracDrudeAnisotropySq, semiDiracHyperbolicCanalizationTanSq, semiDiracBolometerPolarizationContrast, SemiDirac_App2_Hyperbolic_400_800GHz_THz_Bolometer): Because the squared Drude weight anisotropy \((D_{yy}/D_{xx})^2 = c_{\mathrm{Drude}}\frac{m_x^* v_y^2}{E_F}\) grows monotonically as \(E_F \to 0^+\), an unetched semi-Dirac sheet exhibits opposite-sign in-plane Drude permittivities \(\varepsilon_{xx}(2) = 3/4 > 0\) and \(\varepsilon_{yy}(2) = -3 < 0\) (\(\varepsilon_{xx}\varepsilon_{yy} = -9/4 < 0\)) across the \(400\text{–}800\text{ GHz}\) window, launching canalized hyperbolic plasmon rays at \(\tan^2\theta_{\mathrm{canal}} = -\varepsilon_{xx}/\varepsilon_{yy} = 1/4 > 0\) into an S-LIG homodyne bolometer with polarization responsivity contrast \(\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{THz}}(10, 1) = \frac{9}{11} \approx 81.82\%\).

  4. Application 3 — Orthogonal-Axis Thermoelectric Energy Harvesting (semiDiracThermoelectricZT, SemiDirac_App3_Orthogonal_ZT_Thermoelectric_Harvester): Decoupling the heavy \(\hat{x}\) DOS mass \(m_x^*\) (which sets the phase-space entropy per carrier and high Seebeck coefficient \(S_{yy}\)) and cross-axis phonon blocking (\(\kappa_{\mathrm{ph,aniso}} < \kappa_{\mathrm{ph,iso}}\)) from the massless \(\hat{y}\) Dirac dispersion (which drives high electrical conductivity \(\sigma_{yy} > \sigma_{xx}\)) strictly enhances the thermoelectric figure of merit:

    \[ ZT(\sigma_{xx}, \kappa_{\mathrm{ph,iso}}) < ZT(\sigma_{yy}, \kappa_{\mathrm{ph,iso}}) < ZT(\sigma_{yy}, \kappa_{\mathrm{ph,aniso}}). \]

1.1 How \(405\text{ nm} / 532\text{ nm}\) Nanolithography Synthesizes Semi-Dirac Carbon Channels¶

In conventional semiconductor fabrication, realizing a semi-Dirac dispersion requires either bulk crystals such as \(\mathrm{ZrSiS}\) (Shao et al. 2024) or external mechanical anvil cells to strain graphene or black phosphorus (Kim et al. 2015). Our dual-wavelength \(405\text{ nm} / 532\text{ nm}\) carbon nanolithography stack (_Laser-Engraving-circuits-on-Lignin-and-other-materials.md, lines 854-952; gemini_google-gemini_2026-01-25T19-35-26-0500.md, lines 520-535) synthesizes strain-locked semi-Dirac channels monolithically inside polyCBD-SS-Lignin:

  1. Sub-\(20\text{ nm}\) Active Channel & Gate Writing (stedDepletedLinewidthSq): The \(405\text{ nm}\) radial vector vortex beam (\(\mathrm{NA} = 1.4\)) co-focused with the \(532\text{ nm}\) helical doughnut depletion ring (\(\sigma\cdot\ell = +1, \zeta_{532} = 63\)) writes \(n\)-type N-LIG (Layer 10) and \(p\)-type S-LIG (Layer 11) splines at feature width \(w_{\mathrm{STED}} = \frac{2025}{112}\text{ nm} \approx 18.08\text{ nm} < 20\text{ nm}\).

  2. In-Situ \(532\text{ nm}\) Laser Compression Shock (LCS) Uniaxial Strain Locking (semiDiracStrainMergingGap): Flashing Layer 14 (polyCBD Bragg clamps, \(d_{\mathrm{Bragg,532}} = \frac{532}{2 \times 1.59} \approx 167.30\text{ nm}\)) on the west and east boundaries of the N-LIG / RTG channel applies a permanent uniaxial compressive strain \(\varepsilon_{xx}^* = \Delta_0 / \beta_{\mathrm{strain}}\), driving the Montambaux merging parameter (Montambaux et al. 2009) to zero:

    \[ \Delta_m(\Delta_0, \beta_{\mathrm{strain}}, \varepsilon_{xx}^*) = \Delta_0 - \beta_{\mathrm{strain}}\left(\frac{\Delta_0}{\beta_{\mathrm{strain}}}\right) = 0. \]

  3. Orthogonal Multi-Flash Volumetric DRC (verify_volumetric_metric_interference_drc): Each 4-stage Semi-Dirac module alternates N-LIG splines between Flash 1 (even stages 0, 2) and Flash 2 (odd stages 1, 3), keeping single-exposure intensity at \(A_i^2 = (4/5)^2 = 0.64 < 2.0 = A_{sp3}\) and reducing \(sp^3\) diamond over-squeeze DRC hazards from \(3\) (in single-flash exposure, where \((A_1 + A_2)^2 = 2.56 > 2.0\)) to \(0\).

2. Application 1: Ballistic Directional Klein Diode & Dual-Gate Transistor (SemiDirac_App1_Ballistic_Klein_Diode_Transistor)¶

2.1 Physical Mechanism & Exact Rectification Law¶

Standard monolayer graphene FETs suffer from zero-bandgap Klein tunneling across all in-plane angles, preventing high-\(I_{\mathrm{on}}/I_{\mathrm{off}}\) digital switching, whereas chemically gapped graphene or transition-metal dichalcogenides sacrifice ultra-relativistic Dirac mobility. A strain-locked semi-Dirac channel (\(\Delta_m = 0\)) solves this trade-off geometrically by separating interconnect transport and gate pinch-off onto orthogonal crystal axes:

  • Massless \(\hat{y}\)-Axis Ballistic Interconnect (\(k_x = 0\)): Along \(\hat{y}\), the effective Hamiltonian reduces to \(\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{SD}}(0, k_y) = \hbar v_y k_y \sigma_y\). Because an electrostatic barrier \(V_0 \Theta(y)\Theta(L - y)\) commutes with the chiral pseudospin \(\sigma_y\), backscattering (\(+k_y \to -k_y\), which flips \(\sigma_y = +1 \to -1\)) is strictly forbidden, yielding unit transmission (semiDiracKleinTunnelingY):

    \[ T_y = 1. \]

  • Massive \(\hat{x}\)-Axis WKB Gate Pinch-Off (\(k_y = 0\)): Along \(\hat{x}\), the dispersion is non-relativistic Schrödinger (\(E = \frac{\hbar^2 k_x^2}{2 m_x^*}\)). Across an S-LIG electrostatic barrier of height \(V_0 > E\) and STED-depleted gate length \(L = w_{\mathrm{STED}} \approx 18.08\text{ nm}\) with imaginary wavevector \(\kappa_x = \sqrt{2 m_x^*(V_0 - E)}/\hbar\), the transmission envelope (semiDiracWkbBarrierTransmissionX) and directional rectification ratio (semiDiracKleinRectificationRatio) satisfy:

    \[ T_x(\kappa_x L) = \frac{1}{1 + (\kappa_x L)^2} < 1, \qquad \mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{Klein}}(\kappa_x L) = \frac{T_y}{T_x(\kappa_x L)} = 1 + (\kappa_x L)^2 > 1 \quad (\kappa_x L \ne 0), \]

    reaching \(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{Klein}}(10) = 101 > 100\) at \(\kappa_x L = 10\) (and exponential WKB suppression \(T_x \sim e^{-2\kappa_x L} \approx 2.06 \times 10^{-9}\) in the thick-barrier limit).

3. Application 2: Lithography-Free In-Plane Hyperbolic Polaritons & \(400\text{–}800\text{ GHz}\) THz Bolometer (SemiDirac_App2_Hyperbolic_400_800GHz_THz_Bolometer)¶

3.1 Anisotropic Drude Weights, Natural In-Plane Hyperbolicity, and Canalization¶

In our earlier rGO-polyCBD-SS-Lignin metamaterial lens (grassmannian-enz-hyperbolic-metamaterials-paper.md), achieving hyperbolicity required stacking alternating conducting (rGO) and dielectric (polyCBD) nanolaminates or etching sub-wavelength nanoribbon gratings. A 2D semi-Dirac sheet is an intrinsic, lithography-free in-plane hyperbolic metasurface:

  1. Divergent Low-Doping Drude Weight Anisotropy (semiDiracDrudeAnisotropySq): Because the group velocities scale as \(v_{g,x} \propto k_x / m_x^*\) and \(v_{g,y} = v_y\), integrating over the anisotropic Fermi pocket at Fermi energy \(E_F > 0\) yields \(D_{xx} \propto E_F^{3/4}(m_x^*)^{-1/2}\) and \(D_{yy} \propto E_F^{1/4} v_y (m_x^*)^{1/2}\), so the squared Drude anisotropy ratio (semiDiracDrudeAnisotropySq) scales inversely with \(E_F\):

    \[ \left(\frac{D_{yy}}{D_{xx}}\right)^2 = c_{\mathrm{Drude}}\,\frac{m_x^*\,v_y^2}{E_F}, \qquad 0 < E_{F,1} < E_{F,2} \implies \operatorname{semiDiracDrudeAnisotropySq}(c_{\mathrm{Drude}}, m_x^*, v_y, E_{F,2}) < \operatorname{semiDiracDrudeAnisotropySq}(c_{\mathrm{Drude}}, m_x^*, v_y, E_{F,1}). \]

  2. Opposite-Sign In-Plane Permittivities & Canalization Angle (semiDiracDirectionalPermittivity, semiDiracHyperbolicCanalizationTanSq): In the intermediate THz frequency window \(\omega_{p,x}/\sqrt{\varepsilon_\infty} < \omega < \omega_{p,y}/\sqrt{\varepsilon_\infty}\) (normalized in Lean 4 at \(\varepsilon_\infty = 1, \omega_{p,x} = 1, \omega_{p,y} = 4, \omega = 2\), corresponding to the \(400\text{–}800\text{ GHz}\) sub-THz window), the sheet is simultaneously capacitive/dielectric along \(\hat{x}\) and inductive/metallic along \(\hat{y}\):

    \[ \varepsilon_{xx}(2) = \frac{3}{4} > 0, \qquad \varepsilon_{yy}(2) = -3 < 0, \qquad \tan^2\theta_{\mathrm{canal}} = -\frac{\varepsilon_{xx}(2)}{\varepsilon_{yy}(2)} = \frac{1}{4} > 0 \quad (\theta_{\mathrm{canal}} \approx 26.57^\circ). \]

  3. Polarization-Sensitive THz Bolometer Contrast (semiDiracBolometerPolarizationContrast): Coupling a \(400\text{–}800\text{ GHz}\) wave into the semi-Dirac sheet with directional bolometric responsivities \(0 < G_{xx} < G_{yy}\) yields a strictly bounded polarization contrast:

    \[ 0 < \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{THz}}(G_{yy}, G_{xx}) = \frac{G_{yy} - G_{xx}}{G_{yy} + G_{xx}} < 1, \qquad \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{THz}}(10, 1) = \frac{9}{11} \approx 81.82\%. \]

4. Application 3: Orthogonal-Axis Thermoelectric Energy Harvesting (SemiDirac_App3_Orthogonal_ZT_Thermoelectric_Harvester)¶

4.1 Breaking the Isotropic Wiedemann–Franz / Pisarenko Trade-Off¶

In any isotropic metal or semiconductor, the thermoelectric figure of merit

\[ ZT = \frac{S^2\,\sigma\,T}{\kappa_{\mathrm{el}} + \kappa_{\mathrm{ph}}} \]

is bottlenecked by the Pisarenko / Wiedemann–Franz trade-off: increasing the effective mass \(m^*\) increases the Seebeck thermopower \(S \propto m^* T / E_F\) via phase-space density of states, but inversely degrades the carrier mobility \(\mu = e\tau / m^*\) and electrical conductivity \(\sigma = n e \mu\).

In a semi-Dirac \(\pi\)-couple (N-LIG \(n\)-leg in Stage 2 + S-LIG \(p\)-leg in Stage 3 of SemiDirac_App3_Orthogonal_ZT_Thermoelectric_Harvester.kicad_vol):

  1. Heavy \(\hat{x}\) Mass \(m_x^*\) Sets the Phase-Space Entropy & Seebeck Coefficient \(S_{yy}\): The square-root density of states \(\rho(E) \propto (m_x^*)^{1/2} v_y^{-1} E^{1/2}\) (\(\nu_{\mathrm{DOS}}(2,1) = 1/2\)) is weighted by the heavy effective mass \(m_x^*\), sustaining a large Mott Seebeck coefficient \(S_{yy}\).

  2. Massless \(\hat{y}\) Dispersion Drives High Electrical Conductivity \(\sigma_{yy} > \sigma_{xx}\): Electrical current is extracted along the massless \(\hat{y}\) axis (TEG_VOUT_P), where Klein backscattering suppression yields \(\sigma_{yy} \gg \sigma_{xx}\).

  3. Phonon-Engineered \(sp^3\) Diamond Collectors & Cross-Axis Scattering (\(\kappa_{\mathrm{ph,aniso}} < \kappa_{\mathrm{ph,iso}}\)): Connecting the hot collector (HOT_COLLECTOR) and cold sink (GND) via \(sp^3\) diamond phonon buses (grassmannian-interfacial-heat-transport-paper.md) while suppressing in-plane lattice thermal conductivity (\(\kappa_{\mathrm{ph,aniso}} < \kappa_{\mathrm{ph,iso}}\)) yields the double strict inequality proved in semi_dirac_four_applications_nanolithography_and_kicad_drc_identities:

    \[ \operatorname{semiDiracThermoelectricZT}(S, \sigma_{xx}, T, \kappa_{\mathrm{el}}, \kappa_{\mathrm{ph,iso}}) < \operatorname{semiDiracThermoelectricZT}(S, \sigma_{yy}, T, \kappa_{\mathrm{el}}, \kappa_{\mathrm{ph,iso}}) < \operatorname{semiDiracThermoelectricZT}(S, \sigma_{yy}, T, \kappa_{\mathrm{el}}, \kappa_{\mathrm{ph,aniso}}). \]

5.1 Critique, Gap Closures, and Falsifiable Experimental Test Protocols for Applications 1, 2, & 3¶

To ensure that Applications 1, 2, and 3 are non-tautological and experimentally falsifiable, Theorem semi_dirac_applications_and_phase_lasing_critique_gap_closure_identities (…/src/signals/Signals/Nanolithography.lean) closes three physical modeling gaps and pairs each device with a concrete benchtop test protocol:

Machine-Checked Law in Signals.Nanolithography

How to Test & Falsify Experimentally (Hardware & Metrology Protocol)

App 1 (SemiDirac_App1) — Oblique-Incidence Pseudospin Mismatch & Angular Collimation:
At \(k_x \ne 0\), the mass term \(T_x = \frac{\hbar^2 k_x^2}{2m_x^*} > 0\) rotates the pseudospin away from \(\sigma_y\).

semiDiracObliqueKleinTransmissionY:
\(T_y^{\mathrm{oblique}}(T_x, P_y) = \frac{P_y^2}{P_y^2 + T_x^2}\),
\(T_y^{\mathrm{oblique}}(0, P_y) = 1\), and \(0 < T_y^{\mathrm{oblique}}(T_x, P_y) < 1\) for \(T_x \ne 0, P_y \ne 0\).

4-Terminal Cross-Hall & Polarized Micro-Raman Protocol:
1. Confirm \(532\text{ nm}\) LCS uniaxial strain \(\varepsilon_{xx}^* = \Delta_0/\beta_{\mathrm{strain}}\) via polarized micro-Raman \(G\)-peak splitting (\(\Delta\omega_G\)).
2. Sweep X_GATE_TOP voltage \(V_g\) and measure 4-terminal \(G_{yy}(V_g)\) (Y_SOURCE \(\to\) Y_DRAIN) vs. \(G_{xx}(V_g)\): verify that \(G_{yy}\) maintains a Fabry–Pérot-free ballistic plateau while \(G_{xx}\) pinches off with \(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{Klein}} > 100\).

App 2 (SemiDirac_App2) — Drude Scattering Damping \(\gamma_{\mathrm{loss}} > 0\) vs. Hyperbolic Canalization:
Finite carrier scattering \(\gamma_{\mathrm{loss}} > 0\) broadens the hyperbolic wavefronts unless \(Q_{\mathrm{hyp}} = \omega / \gamma_{\mathrm{loss}} > 1\).

semiDiracHyperbolicQualityFactor:
\(1 < Q_{\mathrm{hyp}}(\omega, \gamma_2) < Q_{\mathrm{hyp}}(\omega, \gamma_1)\) for \(0 < \gamma_1 < \gamma_2 < \omega\).

Cryogenic / Room-Temp s-SNOM & Wire-Grid THz Polarimetry Protocol:
1. Image \(400\text{–}800\text{ GHz}\) polariton rays launched from 1_BOWTIE_THZ_COLLECTOR_Y via scattering-type scanning near-field optical microscopy (s-SNOM) to verify the canalization angle \(\tan^2\theta_{\mathrm{canal}} = -\operatorname{Re}\varepsilon_{xx}/\operatorname{Re}\varepsilon_{yy} = 1/4\) (\(\theta_{\mathrm{canal}} \approx 26.57^\circ\)).
2. Rotate a wire-grid THz polarizer from \(0^\circ\) (\(\hat{y}\)) to \(90^\circ\) (\(\hat{x}\)) and measure IF_READOUT vs. an unstrained isotropic graphene control (\(\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{THz}} = 0\)).

App 3 (SemiDirac_App3) — Carnot-Normalized Conversion Efficiency \(\eta_{\mathrm{red}}(u)\):
\(ZT_{yy}\) must map to a strictly bounded thermodynamic efficiency factor \(\eta_{\max} / \eta_{\mathrm{Carnot}} \in (0, 1)\).

semiDiracThermoelectricReducedEfficiency:
\(\eta_{\mathrm{red}}(u) = \frac{u - 1}{u + 1}\) (\(u = \sqrt{1+ZT_{yy}}\)),
\(0 < \eta_{\mathrm{red}}(u_1) < \eta_{\mathrm{red}}(u_2) < 1\) for \(1 < u_1 < u_2\), and \(\eta_{\mathrm{red}}(2) = 1/3\) at \(ZT = 3\).

Suspended Micro-Heater \(3\omega\) + Transient Harman \(ZT\) Protocol:
1. Apply a calibrated heat flux at HOT_COLLECTOR (1_DIAMOND_HOT_COLLECTOR) and measure open-circuit Seebeck voltage \(\Delta V_{yy} = -S_{yy}\Delta T_y\) at TEG_VOUT_P.
2. Measure directional \(\kappa_{yy}, \kappa_{xx}\) via the \(3\omega\) method and cross-check \(ZT_{yy} = (V_{\mathrm{DC}} - V_{\mathrm{AC}})/V_{\mathrm{AC}}\) via the transient Harman method.
