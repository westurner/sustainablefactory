2.1 Exact Polar Substitution in the Gross–Pitaevskii Lagrangian Density¶

\[ \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{GP}}(\psi, \partial_t\psi, \nabla\psi) = \frac{i\hbar}{2}\bigl(\psi^*\partial_t\psi - \psi\partial_t\psi^*\bigr) - \frac{\hbar^2}{2 m_\phi}|\nabla\psi|^2 - V_{\mathrm{ext}}|\psi|^2 - \frac{g_\phi}{2}|\psi|^4. \]

Because \(\psi^*\partial_t\psi - \psi\partial_t\psi^* = 2 i\,\operatorname{Im}(\psi^*\partial_t\psi)\), the temporal symplectic term is real-valued and equals (grossPitaevskiiTemporalSymplecticTerm):

\[ \frac{i\hbar}{2}\bigl(\psi^*\partial_t\psi - \psi\partial_t\psi^*\bigr) = -\hbar\,\operatorname{Im}(\psi^*\partial_t\psi). \]

\[ \partial_\mu\psi = (\partial_\mu A + i A\,\partial_\mu\theta)e^{i\theta} = (\partial_\mu A\cos\theta - A\,\partial_\mu\theta\sin\theta) + i(\partial_\mu A\sin\theta + A\,\partial_\mu\theta\cos\theta), \]
