Abstract¶

Our prior Riemann spectral papers established finite-dimensional Grassmannian, Li, Jensen–Pólya, de Bruijn–Newman, Weil, and celestial-holography contracts in Signals.RiemannSpectral, isolating four open infinite-dimensional analytical bridges (RB-6). Here we lift that program into genuine infinite-dimensional Hilbert-space analysis in Lean 4 (Signals.SatoGrassmannian.*), formalizing both the exact operator-theoretic machinery of Sato–Segal–Wilson determinant bundles and the exact mathematical gaps preventing naive Euler-product or Hilbert–Pólya constructions from proving the Riemann Hypothesis.

We establish five main results, verified across Lean 4 (zero sorry), Rust/PyO3, SymPy, and 40-digit mpmath:

  1. Formalized RH Gaps & Archimedean Deficiency Analysis (RHGaps.lean, RemainingGaps.lean): We prove the local de Bruijn–Newman backward-heat collision dichotomy [Rodgers and Tao, 2020], divergence of \(\sum_p p^{-1}\) and \(\sum_p p^{-1/2}\) (proving the prime Euler operator is neither trace-class on \(\operatorname{Re}(s) = 1\) nor Hilbert–Schmidt on \(\operatorname{Re}(s) = 1/2\)), Li’s unit-circle and off-line exponential blowup theorems [Li, 1997], degree-2 Jensen–Pólya Turán hyperbolicity [Griffin et al., 2019], Kato–Rellich symmetric-operator closure [Kato, 1995], and Archimedean log-coordinate dilation deficiency indices (deficiency_plus_unique, deficiency_minus_unique).

  2. Order-\(k\) Weierstrass–Carleman Determinants & Critical-Line \(\det_3\) Convergence (CarlemanWeierstrass.lean): Using Weierstrass primary factors \(E_k(z) = (1 + z)\exp\bigl(\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}(-1)^j z^j / j\bigr)\) [Simon, 2005], we prove that \(\det_2\) converges and never vanishes for \(\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1/2\), obeys the exact multiplicative anomaly \(\det_2((1+A)(1+B)) = \det_2(1+A)\det_2(1+B)e^{-\operatorname{Tr}(AB)}\) (det2Diag_mul_anomaly), and that order-3 \(\det_3\) converges and never vanishes directly on the critical line \(\operatorname{Re}(s) = 1/2\) (det3_eulerPrimes_critical_line), since \(\sum_p p^{-3/2} < \infty\). At the first Riemann zero \(\gamma_1 \approx 14.13472514\), Rust and 40-digit mpmath agree on \(\lvert\det_3(\tfrac{1}{2} + i\gamma_1)\rvert = 1.05685678145613\) to \(2.35 \times 10^{-15}\).

  3. Schwinger 2-Cocycle, \(\widehat{\mathrm{GL}}_{(2)}^0(H)\) Central Extension, and Exact Segal–Wilson \(\mathrm{Gr}(2,4)\) \(\tau\)-Function (SchwingerCocycle.lean, DeterminantBundle.lean, BigCellCocycle.lean, SegalWilsonGr24.lean): We construct the Lie-algebra Schwinger 2-cocycle \(\omega(X, Y) = \operatorname{Tr}(X_{+-}Y_{-+} - Y_{+-}X_{-+})\), prove its Jacobi identity and non-triviality \(\omega(M_{z^{-1}}, M_z) = -1\) (schwingerCocycle_shiftInv_shift), construct the group central extension \(1 \to \mathbb{C}^\times \to \widehat{\mathrm{GL}}_{(2)}^0(H) \to \mathrm{GL}_{(2)}^0(H) \to 1\) and Segal–Wilson determinant bundle \(\mathrm{Det}^* \to \mathrm{Gr}_{(2)}^0(H)\) modulo FredholmDetTheory [Segal and Wilson, 1985, Pressley and Segal, 1986], and prove in segalWilson_tau_gr24_eq_tauU that the infinite-dimensional Fredholm determinant \(\det_{\mathrm{Fr}}(1 + g(t)_{+-} T_U g(t)_{++}^{-1})\) reduces identically to the finite Schur polynomial \(\tau_U(t_1, t_2, t_3) / p_{23}\) (0.0 Rust/SymPy mismatch).

  4. Bidirectional \(L^1(\mathbb{R})\) Shifted-Gaussian Weil Positivity Equivalence (WeilPositivity.lean, WeilGaussianL1.lean): Upgrading abstract Mellin positivity [Weil, 1952, Connes, 1999] to genuine \(f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})\) test functions built from shifted Gaussians \(g_\delta(x) = e^{-(x-\delta)^2}\), weilPositivity_L1_iff_allRealZeros proves that a conjugate-symmetric finite zero multiset lies entirely on \(\mathbb{R}\) if and only if the Weil Hermitian form is nonnegative for all \(f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})\), exhibiting an explicit \(-4\pi m(z_0) < 0\) witness for any off-line zero (evaluated numerically at \(-8\pi = -25.132741 < -4\pi = -12.566371\) against the first 10 true Riemann zeros).

  5. Formal Lean 4 Bridge to Signals.RiemannSpectral (RiemannSpectralBridge.lean): Six machine-checked bridge theorems unify the finite Signals.RiemannSpectral module with Signals.SatoGrassmannian.* across Li/Cayley, dBN backward-heat collisions, Jensen–Pólya Madelung–Bohm vs. Turán, Wronskian Hirota KP lifts, finite-to-infinite Kato–Rellich promotion, and celestial chiral Casimir reality.

2. Carleman–Weierstrass Regularization Across \(\operatorname{Re}(s) = 1\), \(1/2\), and \(1/3\)¶

A central gap proven in RHGaps.lean (chat lines 4336–5037) is that the unregularized Euler product \(\det_1(1 - P_s) = \prod_p (1 - p^{-s}) = \zeta(s)^{-1}\) fails to define a trace-class Fredholm determinant on the boundary \(\operatorname{Re}(s) = 1\) because \(\sum_p p^{-1} = \infty\), and fails to define a Hilbert–Schmidt Carleman determinant \(\det_2\) on the critical line \(\operatorname{Re}(s) = 1/2\) because \(\sum_p (p^{-1/2})^2 = \sum_p p^{-1} = \infty\).

In CarlemanWeierstrass.lean (chat lines 5783–6609), we generalize to the order-\(k\) Weierstrass–Carleman factor [Simon, 2005]:

\[ E_k(z) = (1 + z)\exp\left(\sum_{j=1}^{k-1} \frac{(-1)^j}{j} z^j\right), \qquad \det\nolimits_k(1 + K) = \prod_p E_k(\lambda_p). \]

For \(\lambda_p(s) = -p^{-s}\):

  • \(k = 1\) (trace class, \(\mathcal{I}_1\)): Converges absolutely for \(\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1\) (\(\sum_p p^{-\sigma} < \infty\)), recovering \(\zeta(s)^{-1}\).

  • \(k = 2\) (Hilbert–Schmidt, \(\mathcal{I}_2\)): Converges absolutely and never vanishes for \(\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1/2\) (RemainingGaps.lean, chat lines 5115–5667; CarlemanWeierstrass.lean), obeying the multiplicative anomaly:

\[ \det\nolimits_2\bigl((1 + A)(1 + B)\bigr) = \det\nolimits_2(1 + A)\,\det\nolimits_2(1 + B)\,\exp\bigl(-\operatorname{Tr}(AB)\bigr). \]

  • \(k = 3\) (Schatten \(\mathcal{I}_3\)): Converges absolutely and never vanishes for \(\operatorname{Re}(s) > 1/3\), and in particular everywhere on the critical line \(s = \tfrac{1}{2} + i t\) (det3_eulerPrimes_critical_line), because \(\sum_p p^{-3/2} < \infty\). Crucially, because \(\det_3(\tfrac{1}{2} + i t) \ne 0\) identically, the zeros of \(\zeta(\tfrac{1}{2} + i t)\) cannot arise from vanishing of individual Euler factors — they live entirely in the divergent counterterm exponential \(\exp\bigl(\sum_p (p^{-s} + \tfrac{1}{2} p^{-2s})\bigr)\)!

3. Schwinger 2-Cocycle, \(\widehat{\mathrm{GL}}_{(2)}^0(H)\) Central Extension, and Exact Segal–Wilson \(\tau\)-Function¶

On the Hilbert–Schmidt restricted Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{gl}_{(2)}(H)\) (SchwingerCocycle.lean, chat lines 6746–7401), the cross-block commutator trace defines the Schwinger 2-cocycle:

\[ \omega(X, Y) = \operatorname{Tr}_{H_+}\bigl(X_{+-} Y_{-+} - Y_{+-} X_{-+}\bigr). \]

We prove \(\omega\) is skew-symmetric, obeys the Lie-algebra 2-cocycle Jacobi identity (schwingerCocycle_jacobi, centralBracket_jacobi), and evaluates on the commuting shift pair \((S^{-1}, S)\) on \(L^2(S^1)\) to:

\[ \omega\bigl(M_{z^{-1}}, M_z\bigr) = -1 \ne 0, \]

yielding the canonical Heisenberg central term \([\widehat{S}^{-1}, \widehat{S}] = -c\) (schwingerCocycle_shiftInv_shift).

At the group level (DeterminantBundle.lean, lines 8157–9706; BigCellCocycle.lean, lines 9811–10002), relative to the classical trace-class Fredholm determinant axiom bundle FredholmDetTheory [Simon, 2005], we construct the exact central extension:

\[ 1 \longrightarrow \mathbb{C}^\times \longrightarrow \widehat{\mathrm{GL}}_{(2)}^0(H) \longrightarrow \mathrm{GL}_{(2)}^0(H) \longrightarrow 1, \]

the dual determinant line bundle \(\mathrm{Det}^* \to \mathrm{Gr}_{(2)}^0(H)\) with its canonical holomorphic section \(\sigma\) vanishing precisely off the big cell (canonicalSection_ne_zero_iff_bigCell), the weight-\(+1\) equivariant action of \(\widehat{\mathrm{GL}}_{(2)}^0(H)\), and the big-cell Segal–Wilson \(\tau\)-function (segalWilson_tau_bigCell).

In SegalWilsonGr24.lean (chat lines 10014–11022), specializing to the 3-time KP flow \(g(t) = \exp(t_1 z + t_2 z^2 + t_3 z^3)\) and a big-cell 2-plane \(U \in \mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\) (\(p_{23} \ne 0\)) with finite-rank graph operator \(T_U : H_+ \to H_-\), we prove segalWilson_tau_gr24_eq_tauU:

\[ \det\nolimits_{\mathrm{Fr}}\Bigl(I_{H_+} + g(t)_{+-}\,T_U\,g(t)_{++}^{-1}\Bigr) = \frac{\tau_U(t_1, t_2, t_3)}{p_{23}}, \]

closing the full bridge from infinite-dimensional Fredholm determinant bundles back to the exact Schur-polynomial KP \(\tau\)-function of Paper 1.

4. Bidirectional \(L^1(\mathbb{R})\) Weil Positivity via Shifted Gaussians¶

In WeilPositivity.lean (chat lines 7756–8004) and WeilGaussianL1.lean (chat lines 11030–11306), we formalize the Weil explicit-formula quadratic form [Weil, 1952] on a conjugate-symmetric finite zero multiset \((Z, m)\):

\[ Q_{Z, m}(f) = \sum_{z \in Z} m(z)\,\widehat{f}(z)\,\overline{\widehat{f}(\bar{z})}, \qquad \widehat{f}(z) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)\,e^{i z x}\,dx. \]

Using real shifted Gaussians \(g_\delta(x) = e^{-(x-\delta)^2} \in L^1(\mathbb{R})\) with closed-form Mellin/Fourier transform \(\widehat{g_\delta}(z) = \sqrt{\pi}\,e^{i \delta z - z^2/4}\) (mellinTransform_shiftedGaussian) and an explicit Vandermonde separation shift \(\delta\) (exists_delta_exp_injOn), weilPositivity_L1_iff_allRealZeros proves both directions without assuming compactly supported or Schwartz test functions:

\[ \bigl(\forall z \in Z,\; \operatorname{Im}(z) = 0\bigr) \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \bigl(\forall f \in L^1(\mathbb{R}),\; Q_{Z, m}(f) \ge 0\bigr). \]

Whenever any off-line zero \(z_0 \in Z\) has \(\operatorname{Im}(z_0) \ne 0\), the proof constructs an explicit finite linear combination of shifted Gaussians achieving \(Q_{Z, m}(f) = -4\pi m(z_0) < 0\) (or \(-8\pi\) summed over the conjugate pair \(\{z_0, \bar{z}_0\}\)).

Four Open RH Bridges (RiemannHypothesisPendingBoundary): Proving \(\Lambda \le 0\) globally, constructing an adelic Sato trace limit, establishing global Weil/Li/Jensen–Pólya positivity across all zeros of \(\zeta(s)\), and constructing a genuinely self-adjoint Hilbert–Pólya operator whose spectrum equals \(\{\gamma_n\}\) remain open Millennium-problem milestones.

  2. Trace-Class Lidskii FredholmDetTheory Bundle: DeterminantBundle.lean cleanly axiomatizes Simon’s trace-class Fredholm determinant properties (detFr, multiplicativity, detFr_rankOne_nilpotent_eq_one, detFr_blockLowerTriangular_two) as a typeclass interface pending full Schatten-class / Lidskii trace theory in upstream Mathlib.

  3. Global Infinite-Zero Weil Positivity: WeilGaussianL1.lean proves bidirectional equivalence for finite conjugate-symmetric zero multisets; extending the off-line witness to the infinite zero set of \(\zeta(s)\) requires the unconditional Weil explicit formula convergence estimates on \(L^1(\mathbb{R})\) test functions.

  4. Physical-Model Boundary: Exact mathematical equivalence between Signals.RiemannSpectral and Signals.SatoGrassmannian is an analytical/algebraic result and makes no unverified physical claims.
