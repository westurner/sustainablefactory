1. Epistemic Scope: Attached Gaps vs. Constructive Closure¶

In Section 2.2 of grassmannian-yang-mills-mass-gap-higgs-cp-cpt-paper.md and Signals.Pending.YangMillsMassGapHiggsCPTPendingBoundary (src/signals/Signals/Pending.lean), four coupled mathematical and experimental gaps separate our verified finite/lattice/algebraic core (Signals.YangMillsHiggsCPT, Signals.RiemannSpectral Sec. 12, Signals.DDF Sec. 30.8) from closing allYangMillsHiggsCptGatesClosed:

Attached Gap / Pending Gate

Root Obstruction Identified in Section 2.2 & Pending.lean

Constructive Solution & Formal Contract Implemented in This Plan (YM-1–YM-5)

Gap 1 / Gate 1 (continuum4DYangMillsOSConstructed)

Strong-coupling cluster expansions (\(\beta < 2N^2\)) break down before \(\beta \to \infty\) (\(a \to 0, V \to \infty\)), leaving 4D continuum OS reflection positivity, clustering, and a uniform physical mass gap \(M_{\mathrm{phys}} > 0\) open [Jaffe and Witten, 2006, Osterwalder and Schrader, 1973].

1. Prove 1-loop (\(a(\beta)\Lambda_{\mathrm{YM}} = \exp(-4\pi^2\beta/(b_0 N))\)) and 2-loop (latticeAsymptoticSpacingTwoLoop, \(b_1 = 34 N^2/3\)) asymptotic scaling contraction as \(\beta \to \infty\), while strong-coupling \(\Delta_{\mathrm{sc}}(\beta) = -\ln(\beta/(2N^2))\) turns non-positive at \(\beta \ge 2N^2\).
2. Prove the Conditional Continuum Uniform-Gap Theorem (\(0 < M_0 \le \Delta_{\mathrm{lat}}(a)/a \implies M_\infty \ge M_0 > 0\)).
3. Prove Finite OS Reflection Positivity \(\sum_{i,j}\overline{f_i}f_j S_2(t_i+t_j) = \sum_k w_k \lvert\sum_i f_i e^{-E_k t_i}\rvert^2 \ge 0\), two-time effective-mass clustering \(S_2(\tau_2) \le S_2(\tau_1)e^{-E_{\mathrm{gap}}(\tau_2-\tau_1)}\), and 4-state Sylvester principal minors \(> 0\).

Gap 2 & Gap 3 / Gate 2 (celestialConfiningWightmanReconstructed)

Celestial Kac–Moody Ward identities assume massless soft gluons absent from a confining LSZ spectrum; pure Yang–Mills trades \(g^2\) for \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}\), leaving no independent integer level \(k\); Toupin’s GKO formula yields \(165\text{–}300\text{ MeV}\) vs. lattice \(m_{0^{++}} \approx 1.61\text{–}1.73\text{ GeV}\) [Morningstar and Peardon, 1999].

1. Prove Källén–Lehmann Confined Spectral Support & Integrability: \(\omega \ge m_{\mathrm{gap}} > 0\) excludes massless soft poles and bounds both \(\Delta \ge 2\) exponential tails and the critical \(\Delta = 1\) square-root endpoint (kallenLehmann_critical_soft_pole_and_two_loop_rg).
2. Prove the Tree-Level GKO Integer-Level Obstruction: for \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}} = 0.22\text{ GeV}\) and \(m_{0^{++}} = 1.61\text{ GeV}\), \(M_k(N, \Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}) \le 2\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}} < m_{0^{++}}\) for every \(k \ge 0\), and solve uniquely for the running effective level \(k_{\mathrm{eff}} = N(2\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}/M_{\mathrm{phys}} - 1) < 0\).

Gate 3 (bsmHiggsCpViolationObserved)

Standard Model CKM \(J_{\mathrm{CP}}\) is too small for baryogenesis, and ATLAS/CMS BSM Higgs CP-mixing (\(\tilde{\kappa}\sin(2\alpha_{\mathrm{CP}}) \ne 0\)) has not yet been observed above zero [ATLAS Collaboration, 2023].

Formalize the physical Higgs–fermion EFT parameterization \(\mu_{\mathrm{sig}} = \kappa_f^2 + \tilde{\kappa}_f^2\), \(\kappa_f = \sqrt{\mu_{\mathrm{sig}}}\cos\alpha_{\mathrm{CP}}\), \(\tilde{\kappa}_f = \sqrt{\mu_{\mathrm{sig}}}\sin\alpha_{\mathrm{CP}}\), proving higgsCpEftSignedAsymmetry \(= 2\kappa_f\tilde{\kappa}_f = \mu_{\mathrm{sig}}\sin(2\alpha_{\mathrm{CP}}) = \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{CP}}(1,\mu_{\mathrm{sig}},\alpha_{\mathrm{CP}})\) and the ATLAS/CMS Exclusion-Envelope Bound \(\lvert\tilde{\kappa}_f\rvert \le \sqrt{\mu_{\max}}\sin\alpha_{\max}\) when \(\lvert\alpha_{\mathrm{CP}}\rvert \le \alpha_{\max} \le \pi/2\).

Gate 4 (electroweakBaryogenesisAndModel16Resolved)

Pure SM (\(m_H = 125.25\text{ GeV}\)) gives a smooth crossover rather than a strong first-order phase transition (\(v(T_c)/T_c \ge 1\)) [Kajantie et al., 1996, Kuzmin et al., 1985]; Model 16a/16b (\(\eta_{\mathrm{act}}^\gamma \in \{0,1\}\), \(\kappa_{\mathrm{IR}}\)) requires discriminator data.

1. Prove the exact finite-temperature effective potential double-well factorization at \(T_c\): \(V_{\mathrm{eff}}(\phi, T_c) = \frac{\lambda_T}{4}\phi^2(\phi-\phi_c)^2 \ge 0\) with positive barrier height \(E^4 T_c^4/(4\lambda_T^3) > 0\) at \(\phi_c/2\) and \(\phi_c/T_c = 2E/\lambda_T\), proving pure SM failure (\(2E_{\mathrm{SM}}/\lambda_{\mathrm{SM}} < 1\)) vs. BSM barrier restoration (\(2E \ge \lambda_T\)).
2. Formalize explicit certificate structures (ContinuumYangMillsOSCertificate, CelestialConfiningSpectralCertificate, BsmHiggsCpDiscoveryCertificate, ElectroweakBaryogenesisModel16Certificate) and prove the constructive solver YangMillsMassGapHiggsCPTPendingBoundary.solved_of_certificates discharging allYangMillsHiggsCptGatesClosed alongside OS clustering, out-of-equilibrium Sakharov bias, and positive Model 16 transfer.
