    2. Electroweak $\mathrm{SU}(2)_L \times \mathrm{U}(1)_Y \to \mathrm{U}(1)_{\mathrm{EM}}$ Higgs Mechanism:
      • Mexican-hat potential $V(\Phi) = -\mu^2|\Phi|^2 + \lambda|\Phi|^4$, unitary-gauge shift $|\Phi|^2 = (v + h)^2/2$ at $v^2 = \mu^2/\lambda$, exact vacuum energy $-\mu^4/(4\lambda)$, exact cancellation of the linear tadpole, radial Higgs mass $m_H^2 = 2\mu^2 = 2\lambda v^2 > 0$,
