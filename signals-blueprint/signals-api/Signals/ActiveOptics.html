    Active optical and plasmonic models #

    These finite records extract the active-gain and surface-plasmon models from Dean et al., Zhang et al., and Lei et al. They keep pump power, loss, phase matching, and simulation status explicit. A gain record is not a claim of free energy or passive amplification.
