    SASER and phonon-source bookkeeping #

    Sound Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation, with acoustic or phonon-vortex output. These records preserve the modeled momentum and thrust relations without treating them as demonstrated hardware performance.

    See also Signals.Lasers
