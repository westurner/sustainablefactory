    Grassmannian Geometry of Quantum Causal Inference, Counterfactual Semantics, Constructor Theory, and the Mpemba / Entropy-Siphoning Laws #

    This module formalizes the mathematical and physical synthesis of:

    1. Grassmannian Geometry in Causal Relations (Signals.Plabic, Signals.Geometry, Signals.DDF):
      • Acyclic Structural Causal Networks (WeightedAcyclicNetwork): In a finite Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) with nilpotent direct-effect matrix $W$ ($W^{h+1} = 0$), the total causal path transfer matrix satisfies $T = \sum_{k=0}^h W^k = I + W T$.
      • d-Separation & Confounder Bottlenecks as Vanishing Plücker Minors (Trek / LGV Rule): By the Lindström–Gessel–Viennot (LGV) and Sullivant–Talaska–Draisma Trek rules, $k$ causal sources have vertex-disjoint (unconfounded) pathways to $k$ targets iff their $k \times k$ Plücker minor $\det T_{A,B} > 0$ (interior of $\mathrm{Gr}_{>0}(k,n)$: lgv_two_by_two_det, lgv_multiLayer_det_positive), whereas factoring through $m < k$ shared confounders or mediators collapses $\det T_{A,B} = 0$ onto a boundary Schubert/positroid cell (singleHubRepresentative_det = 0, (A * B).det = 0).
      • Pearl's Do-Calculus Intervention (do(X := x)) as Incoming-Edge Surgery: Observation sums direct causal path $d$ and backdoor confounding trek $b$ (causalObservationalTransfer d b = d + b, matching WeightedAcyclicNetwork.diamond_transfer), whereas $\operatorname{do}(X := x)$ severs incoming confounder edges to isolate $d$ (causalInterventionalDoTransfer d b = d).
      • Lorentzian / DDF Lightcone Causality on $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)$: Via ddfThermalGr24Matrix β θ, the Plücker channel determinant product is $\|w_A^{\mathbb{C}}\|^2 \|w_B^{\mathbb{C}}\|^2 = 1 - \beta^2$, which is strictly positive inside the causal lightcone ($|\beta| < 1$), zero on the null horizon ($|\beta| = 1$), and negative in the spacelike exterior ($|\beta| > 1$).
    2. Quantum Causal Models, Counterfactual Semantics (arXiv:2302.11783), and the Quantum Switch:
      • Abduction–Action–Prediction Counterfactuals (Pearl 2009; Giarmatzi & Costa 2023): From factual observation $(x_{\mathrm{obs}}, y_{\mathrm{obs}})$ under structural mechanism $Y = \alpha X + U_E$, abduction recovers $u_E = y_{\mathrm{obs}} - \alpha x_{\mathrm{obs}}$, action sets $\operatorname{do}(X := x_{\mathrm{cf}})$, and prediction yields $Y_{x_{\mathrm{cf}}}(u_E) = y_{\mathrm{obs}} + \alpha(x_{\mathrm{cf}} - x_{\mathrm{obs}})$.
      • Indefinite Causal Order (Quantum Switch, Oreshkov–Costa–Brukner 2012; Chiribella et al. 2013): Superposing $A \prec B$ ($U_B U_A$) and $B \prec A$ ($U_A U_B$) projects onto the anticommutator channel $E_+ = \frac{1}{4}\|\{U_A, U_B\}\psi\|^2$ and commutator channel $E_- = \frac{1}{4}\|[U_A, U_B]\psi\|^2$, whose causal-order asymmetry $\delta_{\mathrm{switch}} = \frac{E_+ - E_-}{E_+ + E_-}$ maps onto $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)$ via $\frac{\|w_B^{\mathbb{C}}(M_{\mathrm{switch}})\|^2}{\|w_A^{\mathbb{C}}(M_{\mathrm{switch}})\|^2} = \frac{E_-}{E_+}$.
    3. Landau Free-Energy vs. Quantum Resource Theory (QRT) Models of the Mpemba Effect:
      • Landau Quartic Potential (Holtzman & Raz 2022, DOI: 10.1038/s42005-022-01063-2): $F(\eta, a, b, h) = \frac{1}{2} a \eta^2 + \frac{1}{4} b \eta^4 - h \eta$, together with Markovian two-eigenmode relaxation $D(a_2, a_3, \lambda_2, \lambda_3, t) = a_2 e^{-\lambda_2 t} + a_3 e^{-\lambda_3 t}$ and Strong Mpemba slow-mode orthogonal projection ($a_{2,\mathrm{hot}} = 0$).
