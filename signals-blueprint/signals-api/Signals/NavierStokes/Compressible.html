    Formalizes compressible Navier–Stokes and Navier–Stokes–Korteweg (NSK) fluid mechanics within the Three-Channel Plücker–Madelung and Six-Channel Dual-Schubert QFIM (F_μν = 4 g_FS_μν) framework:

    1. 3D Compressible Viscous Stress Tensor & Dissipation Positivity: Proves exact deviatoric–volumetric splitting and Φ_visc ≥ 0 for μ ≥ 0, ζ ≥ 0, plus Stokes' hypothesis ζ = 0 ↔ λ = -(2/3) μ.
    2. Exact Korteweg Capillary Stress Divergence Identity: Proves -(ρ / m) ∂_x Q = ∂_x K_{xx} with K_{xx} = (ℏ² / (4 m²)) (ρ_{xx} - ρ_x² / ρ), putting Bohmian quantum pressure into exact conservation-law flux form.
    3. Bresch–Desjardins (BD) Dual-Schubert QFIM Equivalence: For density-dependent viscosity μ(ρ) = ν ρ, proves that the forward/backward BD effective velocities v_± = v ± ν (ρ_x / ρ) satisfy (1/2) ρ (v_+² + v_-²) = ρ v² + ν² ρ_x² / ρ and identifies the BD osmotic energy with Channel 2 of three_channel_plucker_madelung_qfim at the quantum kinematic viscosity ν = ℏ / (2 m).
    4. Cole–Hopf Linearization & Exact Viscous Burgers Tanh Shock PDE: Proves both the pointwise Cole–Hopf heat-to-Burgers identity and full deriv / iteratedDeriv 2 PDE satisfaction for the moving viscous Burgers shock v(x,t) = c - a tanh((a/(2ν))(x - ct)).
    5. Linearized Compressible Stokes Acoustic Dispersion: Proves exact complex dispersion ω² + i δ k² ω - c_s² k² = 0 with sound diffusivity δ = (4/3) ν + ζ / ρ₀ and causal damping rate Im(ω) = -(1/2) δ k² ≤ 0.
