    Incompressible Navier–Stokes via Dual-Schubert QFIM (Ch. 2 = 0) #

    Formalizes incompressible Navier–Stokes fluid mechanics ($\rho = \rho_0$, $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0$) within the Three-Channel Plücker–Madelung and Six-Channel Dual-Schubert QFIM (F_μν = 4 g_FS_μν) framework:

    1. Vanishing Osmotic Channel (Ch. 2 = 0): Constant density eliminates the scalar Bohmian osmotic energy dρ² / (4 ρ) = 0, reducing the three-channel identity to macroscopic hydrodynamic flow (Ch. 1) + projective QFIM / spin-tension stress (Ch. 3).
    2. 2D Taylor–Green Decaying Vortex: Full deriv / iteratedDeriv 2 proof of $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0$, $x$- and $y$-momentum equations, vorticity, and exact energy–enstrophy dissipation $\dot{E} = -4 \nu k^2 E = -2 \nu \mathcal{Z}$.
    3. 2D Lamb–Oseen Viscous Vortex: Full deriv / iteratedDeriv 2 proof of the 2D viscous vorticity heat equation $\partial_t \omega = \nu \Delta \omega$ and core regularity.
    4. Plane Poiseuille Shear Flow: Exact parabolic velocity profile, no-slip walls, and viscous-pressure balance $\mu u_{yy} = -G$.
    5. 3D Beltrami / ABC Rotational Vortex Flow: Full 3D deriv / iteratedDeriv 2 proof of $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0$, $\nabla \times \mathbf{v} = k \mathbf{v}$ (vanishing Lamb vector), $\Delta \mathbf{v} = -k^2 \mathbf{v}$, and exact 3D decaying viscous incompressible Navier–Stokes momentum satisfaction.
