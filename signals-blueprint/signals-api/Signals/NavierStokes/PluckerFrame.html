    Plücker 1-Jet Stiefel Frame, Clebsch Spinor Vorticity, & Bateman Viscous Audit #

    Formalizes the geometric foundation of Navier–Stokes within the Three-Channel Plücker–Madelung and Six-Channel Dual-Schubert QFIM (F_μν = 4 g_FS_μν) framework, auditing and repairing two algebraic defects identified in data/chats/bohmian-mechanics-and-navier-stokes-and-nonlinearity.md:

    1. 4×2 1-Jet Stiefel Frame & Klein Quadric Singularity Cancellation (Chat Defect #1, lines 57–71): builds a genuine 4 × 2 complex matrix whose 6 Plücker minors satisfy p₀₁ p₂₃ - p₀₂ p₁₃ + p₀₃ p₁₂ = 0, cancels the 1 / p₀₁² Madelung denominator into p₂₃ / p₀₁, and establishes the smooth Schubert chart transition Ω₀ ↔ Ω₀₂ across the nodal divisor Σ₁ = {p₀₁ = 0}.
    2. 2-Component Clebsch Spinor Lift & Nonzero Continuous Vorticity: embeds a unit 2-spinor ψ̂ = (cos(θ/2) e^{iφ₁}, sin(θ/2) e^{iφ₂}) into Plucker6, proves exact F_μν = 4 g_FS_μν, and proves that its Berry curvature yields nonzero continuous vorticity ω_z = (ℏ / 2m) sin θ (∂_x θ ∂_y(φ₂ - φ₁) - ∂_y θ ∂_x(φ₂ - φ₁)).
    3. Hermitian vs. Bateman Doubled Viscous Operator (Chat Defect #2, lines 99–101): proves that the chat's off-diagonal ± i ℏ ν ∇² block is Hermitian (zero net dissipation), whereas the corrected Bateman / Keldysh doubled diagonal operator diag(-i ℏ ν k², +i ℏ ν k²) dissipates primal energy at rate -2 ν k² ‖ψ‖² while conserving the primal–dual pairing conj(ψ̃) · ψ identically.
