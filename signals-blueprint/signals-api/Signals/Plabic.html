    Finite bicolored strand routing with explicit rotation and exit data.

    A rotation system is not by itself a planar disk embedding. Same-color edges are allowed. Termination, the bijective boundary map, fixed-point decorations, and any later identification with positroid cells must not be inferred merely from the existence of a finite graph.
