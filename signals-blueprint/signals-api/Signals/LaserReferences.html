    Laser, graphene, nanodiamond, and holography references #

    This registry normalizes the canonical works listed in data/papers/proofs_of_cw_and_lignin_and_holography.bib. It is provenance and evidence-boundary data, not a claim that every listed work supplies a process recipe or an absolute laser wattage.
