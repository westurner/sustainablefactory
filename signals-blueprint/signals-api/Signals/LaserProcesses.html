    Laser-processing task models #

    These records keep optical power, pulse energy, fluence, pulse duration, and beam geometry separate. They are calibration contracts for process planning, not universal operating recipes. In particular, a consumer laser-head rating cannot be substituted for a pulsed-process fluence or a shock pressure.
