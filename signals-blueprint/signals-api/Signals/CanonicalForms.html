    Affine interval and planar canonical-form examples in fixed oriented coordinates.

    Forms are position-dependent alternating covectors, not just reciprocal scalars. Residues are punctured-neighborhood limits of regularized coefficients. These examples do not assert a general projective canonical-form existence theorem or an optical evaluation operation.
