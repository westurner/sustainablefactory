    Quantum Inverse Scattering Method (QISM) and Grassmannian Geometry #

    This module formalizes the algebraic core of the Quantum Inverse Scattering Method (QISM) and the Algebraic Bethe Ansatz (ABA), together with their reformulation in finite Grassmannian geometry:

    1. Yang's Rational $R$-Matrix, Unitarity, and the Yang--Baxter Equation: The explicit $4 \times 4$ permutation matrix $P$ and Yang rational $R$-matrix $R(u) = u I_4 + \eta P$ satisfy involutive swap ($P^2 = I_4$), the regularity condition $R(0) = \eta P$, triplet/singlet channel eigenvalue splitting ($u - v + \eta$ vs. $u - v - \eta$), quantum unitarity $R(u - v) R(v - u) = (\eta^2 - (u - v)^2) I_4$, and the three-site Yang--Baxter braid identity.
    2. Monodromy Matrices, Cyclic Transfer Traces, and Shifted Quantum Determinants: $2 \times 2$ Lax/monodromy matrices $T(u) = \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{pmatrix}$ compose by matrix multiplication along a 1D chain, with cyclic transfer trace $t(u) = \operatorname{Tr} T(u) = A + D$, Cauchy--Binet path-pair expansion, and shifted Yangian quantum determinants $\operatorname{qdet}_{L/R}(T(u), T(u-\eta))$.
    3. Baxter $Q$-Polynomials in $Gr(2, 4)$, $GL(2)$ Gauge Covariance, and the $QQ$-System: A 2-plane $W = \operatorname{span}\{Q_+, Q_-\}$ evaluated at four spectral shifts defines a $2 \times 4$ Grassmannian representative whose maximal minors are Casorati Wronskians. Left multiplication by any $2 \times 2$ basis change $B \in GL(2, \mathbb{R})$ scales all Wronskians by $\det B$ and leaves Wronskian ratios invariant, while the 3-term quadratic Pluecker relation on $Gr(2, 4)$ yields Baxter's functional $QQ$-system and Hirota bilinear equation.
    4. Wronskian Parameterization of Baxter's $TQ$ Equation and MTV Nondegeneracy: Three-point Casorati Wronskians satisfy Baxter's $TQ$ difference equation identically ($W(u-\eta, u+\eta) Q_+(u) = W(u, u+\eta) Q_+(u-\eta) + W(u-\eta, u) Q_+(u+\eta)$). Evaluating at a Bethe root $Q_+(u_m) = 0$ yields the Bethe Ansatz Equations, while Mukhin--Tarasov--Varchenko (MTV) Wronskian nondegeneracy rules out spurious $0/0$ triple zeros.
    5. Slavnov / Gaudin Determinant Compression and Pauli Exclusion: Two-magnon scalar products collapse into a single $2 \times 2$ Grassmannian minor that is antisymmetric under rapidity exchange and vanishes identically when either Bethe rapidities ($u_0 = u_1$) or dual rapidities ($v_0 = v_1$) collide.
