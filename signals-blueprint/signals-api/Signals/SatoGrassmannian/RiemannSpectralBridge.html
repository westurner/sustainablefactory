    Connects the finite-dimensional RB-6 Grassmannian / Li / de Bruijn–Newman / Jensen–Pólya / Weil / celestial-holography theorems in Signals.RiemannSpectral to the infinite-dimensional Hilbert-space Sato Grassmannian stack Signals.SatoGrassmannian.*:

    1. Li / Cayley bridge (liTransformModulusSq_eq_norm_sq_cayley): exact identity ‖cayley ((1/2 + σ_dev) + i γ)‖² = liTransformModulusSq σ_dev γ, uniting RiemannSpectral.liTransformModulusSq_eq_one_iff_critical_line with RHGaps.norm_sq_cayley_sub_one, liSummand_nonneg_of_re_ge_half, and cayley_pow_unbounded_of_re_lt_half.
    2. de Bruijn–Newman backward-heat collision bridge (dbnQuadraticHeatSolution_eq_localDoubleZero): exact normalization matching RiemannSpectral.dbnQuadraticHeatSolution to RHGaps.localDoubleZero.
    3. Jensen–Pólya Madelung–Bohm / Turán bridge (jensen2_turan_iff_madelung_bohm): degree-2 real-rootedness in RHGaps equals nonnegativity of the osmotic + Bohm discriminant in Signals.RiemannSpectral.
    4. Sato Hirota bilinear Plücker bridge (wronskianMinor_eq_pluckerCoord_cast): real Wronskian minors in Signals.RiemannSpectral lift directly to Gr24Truncation.pluckerCoord on the Klein quadric 𝒬₄, feeding into HirotaKP.hirotaKP_tauU_eq_zero and SegalWilsonGr24.segalWilson_tau_gr24_eq_tauU.

    6. Celestial chiral Casimir & Cayley critical-line equivalence (celestialChiralCasimir_and_cayley_critical_line_equiv): combines RiemannSpectral.celestialChiralCasimir_im_eq_zero_iff_critical_line with the Cayley unit-circle characterization ‖cayley ρ‖ = 1 ↔ σ_dev = 0.
