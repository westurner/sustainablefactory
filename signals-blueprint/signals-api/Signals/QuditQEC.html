    Qudit error-correction models #

    Konno et al. demonstrated a propagating optical GKP qubit, while Brock et al. demonstrated error-corrected GKP qutrits and ququarts beyond break-even. These records extract the finite algebra and performance bookkeeping needed to reason about a higher-dimensional OAM design. They do not claim that either experiment implemented an OAM qudit or that a 100-dimensional code has been constructed.
