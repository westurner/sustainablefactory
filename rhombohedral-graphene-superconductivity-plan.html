1. Executive Summary & Synthesis of the Eight Foundational Regimes (G-MGS)¶

Recent experimental breakthroughs (2021–2025) across crystalline rhombohedral trilayer graphene (RTG, \(N=3\)) [Arp et al., 2024, Nature Physics, 2024, Zhou et al., 2021, Zhou et al., 2021], magic-angle twisted symmetric trilayer graphene (MATTG) [Pierce et al., 2025, Park et al., 2021, Hao et al., 2021], and rhombohedral pentalayer graphene (R5G, \(N=5\)) [Lu et al., 2024, Dong et al., 2024, Chu, 2024, Han et al., 2024, Han et al., 2025] have revealed an extraordinary tapestry of intertwined quantum phases in pure carbon heterostructures—without magnetic dopants:

  1. Why Rhombohedral Stacking (\(N=3\) vs. \(N=5\)) Generates Ultra-Flat Bands (R1): In ABC-stacked \(N\)-layer graphene, dimerized interlayer hopping \(\gamma_1 \approx 380\text{ meV}\) projects the low-energy states onto the outer boundary sublattices \((A_1, B_N) \in \mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2, 2N)\) with chiral dispersion \(E_N(k) \propto k^N / \gamma_1^{N-1}\), Berry phase \(N\pi\) (\(3\pi\) for \(N=3\), \(5\pi\) for \(N=5\)) [Koshino and McCann, 2009, Zhang et al., 2010], and van Hove density-of-states divergence \(g_N(E) \propto E^{\frac{2}{N} - 1}\) (\(E^{-1/3}\) in trilayer vs. a much sharper \(E^{-3/5}\) in pentalayer).

  2. Multiple Superconducting Phases in Rhombohedral Trilayer & Pentalayer Graphene (R2): As carrier density \(n_e\) and displacement field \(D\) tune the Stoner parameter \(U_{\mathrm{int}}\,\nu_{\mathrm{DOS}}(n_e, D)\), the four spin–valley flavors \((K\uparrow, K\downarrow, K'\uparrow, K'\downarrow)\) undergo a cascade of projections onto \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(f, 4)\) (\(f \in \{4, 2, 1\}\)):

    • \(\mathrm{SC}_1\) (\(f = 4\), Intervalley-Coherent IVC Paramagnetic Parent in RTG, \(T_{c,1} \approx 106\text{ mK}\)): Spin-singlet pairing stabilized by intrinsic Kane–Mele spin–orbit coupling \(\lambda_{\mathrm{KM}} \approx 45\text{ }\mu\text{eV}\) [Arp et al., 2024, Nature Physics, 2024], obeying the paramagnetic Pauli limit (\(B_{c\parallel}/B_P \approx 1.0\)) [Zhou et al., 2021].

    • \(\mathrm{SC}_2\) (\(f = 2\), Spin-Polarized Half-Metal Parent in RTG, \(T_{c,2} \approx 50\text{ mK}\)): Equal-spin triplet valley-singlet Plücker pairing on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\) that violates the Pauli limit by \(> 10\times\) (\(B_{c\parallel}/B_P \approx 11.0\)) [Zhou et al., 2021].

    • \(\mathrm{SC}_{\mathrm{chiral}}\) (\(f = 1\), Spin- & Valley-Polarized Quarter-Metal Parent in R5G/R4G, \(T_{c,\mathrm{chiral}} \approx 300\text{ mK}\)): Intravalley chiral \(p_x \pm i p_y\) superconductivity inside a single isospin flavor \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(1,4)\) with spontaneous orbital time-reversal symmetry breaking and magnetic hysteresis [Han et al., 2025].

  3. Tunable Light–Heavy Electron Interplay in Twisted Trilayer Graphene (R3): In MATTG (\(\theta \approx 1.56^\circ\)), mirror symmetry \(M_z\) separates a heavy magic-angle flat band (\(f\)-orbital) from a light high-velocity Dirac cone (\(c\)-orbital); a displacement field \(D\) hybridizes them on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,6)\), boosting \(T_{c,\max}^{\mathrm{MATTG}} \approx 2.9\text{ K}\) via heavy-fermion pairing glue + light-fermion phase stiffness [Pierce et al., 2025, Park et al., 2021, Hao et al., 2021].

  4. Low-Energy Optical Sum Rule & Quantum-Geometric Superfluid Stiffness (R4): In the strong-coupling flat-band limit at integer fillings \(\nu \in \mathbb{Z}\) (\(\delta = |\nu - \nu_{\mathrm{int}}| = 0\)), the low-energy optical spectral weight vanishes identically (\(W_{\mathrm{opt}}(\delta=0) = 0\)) even when interaction-induced single-particle dispersion is finite; upon doping (\(0 < \delta < 1\)) or strain/tunneling (\(t_{\mathrm{strain}}, w_{AA} > 0\)), superfluid stiffness \(D_s^{\mathrm{tot}} = D_s^{\mathrm{conv}} + D_s^{\mathrm{geom}}\) is governed by the Fubini–Study quantum metric trace \(\operatorname{Tr}g_{\mathrm{FS}}\) on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(k, N)\) [Mendez-Valderrama et al., 2024, Peotta and Törmä, 2015].

  5. Large Quantum Anomalous Hall Effect (\(C = \pm 5\)) in Spin–Orbit Proximitized R5G–WS2 (R5): In crystalline pentalayer rhombohedral graphene proximitized by \(\mathrm{WS_2}\) at \(n_e = 0\) and \(B = 0\text{ T}\), combining the Layer-Antiferromagnetic (LAF) gap \(\Delta_{\mathrm{LAF}}\), displacement field \(\Delta_D\) (\(D/\varepsilon_0 = -0.165\text{ V/nm}\)), and proximity Ising SOC \(\Delta_{\mathrm{SOC}}\) selectively inverts only the \(K'\uparrow\) valley gap, aligning \(C_{K\uparrow} = -5/2\) and \(C_{K'\uparrow} = -5/2\) to yield \(C = \pm 5\) and \(R_{xy} = \pm \frac{h}{5e^2} \approx \pm 5.16\text{ k}\Omega\) up to \(T \approx 1.5\text{ K}\) [Han et al., 2024].

  6. Why Pentalayer (\(N=5\)) Hosts the \(C = 1\) Anomalous Hall Crystal & Fractional Quantum Anomalous Hall (FQAH) Effect (R6): Although non-interacting pentalayer graphene has winding number \(N = 5\), strong Coulomb exchange at large \(D\) drives spontaneous formation of an Anomalous Hall Crystal (AHC) that unwinds \(N - 1 = 4\) Berry-flux quanta into remote bands, isolating a flat \(C_{\mathrm{AHC}} = 1\) band at \(B = 0\text{ T}\) that hosts the Jain composite-fermion FQAH hierarchy \(\nu = \frac{p}{2p \pm 1} \in \{2/3, 3/5, 4/7, 4/9, 3/7, 2/5\}\) with fractional charges \(e^* = e/(2p \pm 1)\) [Lu et al., 2024, Dong et al., 2024, Chu, 2024].

  7. Quantum Tornadoes in Supersolids, Anomalous Hall Crystals, & Hydrodynamic Graphene Whirlpools (R7): Quantized vortices (\(\Gamma_\ell = 2\pi \ell \hbar / m^*\)) in a density-modulated supersolid or superconducting anomalous Hall crystal nucleate in low-density inter-droplet channels with reduced core barrier \(E_v(1 - \eta_{\mathrm{mod}})\) and Leggett superfluid fraction \(f_s = 1 - \eta_{\mathrm{mod}}^2 < 1\) [Casotti et al., 2024, Leggett, 1970], complementing viscous electron whirlpools in room-temperature hydrodynamic graphene when \(D_\nu > W/\pi\) [Palm et al., 2024].

  8. Symbiotic Superconductivity + QAHE/FQAH Architecture for Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing (R8): Rather than competing, flat-band graphene superconductivity and QAHE/FQAH form a monolithic gate-defined quantum computing stack (data/chats/_Superconductivity vs. QAHE in Quantum Computing .md, lines 114-165): lateral junctions between intrinsic rhombohedral graphene superconductors (\(\mathrm{SC}_{\mathrm{chiral}} / \mathrm{SC}_2\)) and odd-Chern QAHE (\(C = \pm 5, \pm 1\)) or FQAH (\(\nu = 2/3, 1/3\)) regions host unpaired chiral Majorana edge modes (\(d_{\mathrm{MZM}} = \sqrt{2}\)) and \(\mathbb{Z}_{2m}\) parafermions (\(d_{\mathrm{para}} = \sqrt{2m} = \sqrt{6}\) at \(m=3\)) at zero magnetic field (\(B = 0\text{ T}\)) [Lu et al., 2024, Han et al., 2024, Clarke et al., 2013, Lian et al., 2018].

2. How G-MGS Extends Our Existing Grassmannian Modules in Signals¶

Grassmannian Pillar in G-MGS

Existing Signals Module & Paper

Role in Trilayer (\(N=3\)) & Pentalayer (\(N=5\)) Graphene Superconductivity & QC

1. Boundary Sublattice Projection \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2, 2N)\) & Chiral Winding \(N\pi\)

Signals.DFT, Signals.Plabic (Paper 22, Paper 26)

Projects the \(2N\) carbon \(p_z\) orbitals of ABC-stacked \(N\)-layer graphene onto \((A_1, B_N)\), deriving \(W_N = t_0(t_0/\gamma_1)^{N-1}\), Berry phase \(N\pi\), and van Hove DOS exponent \(1 - 2/N\) (gmgsFlatBandBandwidth, gmgsRhombohedralEnergySq).

2. Isospin Flavor Cascade \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(f, 4)\) (\(f = 4 \to 2 \to 1\)) & Plücker Singlet/Triplet/Chiral Pairing

Signals.QISM, Signals.DFT Section 16 (Paper 2, Paper 26)

Governs the Stoner cascade from \(f=4\) (IVC \(\mathrm{SC}_1\), Pauli-limited) to \(f=2\) (HM spin-triplet \(\mathrm{SC}_2\), \(>10\times\) Pauli violation) to \(f=1\) (QM chiral \(p_x+ip_y\) \(\mathrm{SC}_{\mathrm{chiral}}\) in pentalayer R5G).

3. Mirror-Decomposed \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2, 6)\) Light–Heavy Kondo Hybridization

Signals.Scattering, Signals.DFT (Paper 7, Paper 24)

Models displacement-field-tuned hybridization \(V_{\mathrm{hyb}}(D)\) between the mirror-odd light Dirac cone and mirror-even heavy flat band in MATTG (gmgsLightHeavyHybridizedGapSq, gmgsMattgHybridTc) [Pierce et al., 2025].

4. Fubini–Study Kähler Metric \(\operatorname{Tr}g_{\mathrm{FS}}\) & Low-Energy Optical Sum Rule

Signals.DDF Section 30, Signals.DFT (Paper 19)

Proves exact vanishing of optical spectral weight \(W_{\mathrm{opt}}(\delta=0) = 0\) in the chiral flat-band limit at integer filling and quantum-geometric superfluid stiffness recovery \(D_s^{\mathrm{geom}} \propto |\Delta|\delta(1-\delta)g_{\mathrm{FS}} > 0\) [Mendez-Valderrama et al., 2024].

5. Generalized \(N=5\) Haldane Valley-Spin Mass Inversion & Anomalous Hall Crystal Unwinding

Signals.DFT Section 12, Signals.Plabic (Paper 23)

Proves \(C = \pm 5\) QAHE when \(\mathrm{WS_2}\) Ising SOC + \(D\) inverts only the \(K'\uparrow\) valley (gmgsProximitizedQaheChern) [Han et al., 2024], and proves interaction-driven unwinding \(C_{\mathrm{AHC}} = 5 - 4 = 1\) with Jain FQAH fractions \(\nu = p/(2p \pm 1)\) [Lu et al., 2024, Dong et al., 2024].

6. Plücker Madelung Supersolid Vortex Lattice & Topological Majorana/Parafermion Qubits

Signals.DDF, Signals.SolitonBus, Signals.NonDestructive (Paper 15, Paper 22)

Proves supersolid Leggett fraction \(1 - \eta_{\mathrm{mod}}^2\), inter-droplet vortex barrier reduction [Casotti et al., 2024], hydrodynamic whirlpool threshold [Palm et al., 2024], and QAHE/FQAH–SC Majorana (\(d=\sqrt{2}\)) / parafermion (\(d=\sqrt{2m}\)) qubit error suppression.

3. Staged Implementation Plan Across Lean 4, Rust/WASM, Python, and MyST¶

Phase 1: Formal Verification in Lean 4 (Signals.DFT Section 18 & Signals.Pending Section 36)¶

  1. Regime 1 (\(N\)-Layer Rhombohedral Flat-Band Hierarchy): Formalize gmgsRhombohedralEnergySq, gmgsFlatBandBandwidth, and gmgsEffectiveInteractionRs, proving \(W_5 < W_3 < W_1\) and \(r_s(N=5) > r_s(N=3) > r_s(N=1)\) for \(0 < t_0 < \gamma_1\).

  2. Regime 2 (Rhombohedral Trilayer & Pentalayer Multi-Phase Superconductivity): Formalize gmgsStonerFlavorCount, gmgsPauliLimitField, gmgsTripletEffectiveCriticalField, gmgsKaneMeleEnhancedTc, and gmgsChiralPxPyGapSq, proving:

    • \(\mathrm{SC}_1\) obeys the Pauli limit while \(\mathrm{SC}_2\) spin-triplet pairing achieves \(B_{c\parallel}^{\mathrm{triplet}} / B_P > 10\),

    • Intrinsic/proximitized Kane–Mele SOC \(\lambda_{\mathrm{KM}} > 0\) enhances \(\mathrm{SC}_1\) / IVC \(T_c\) [Arp et al., 2024],

    • Chiral \(p_x + i p_y\) gap \(\Delta_{\mathrm{chiral}}^2(k_x, k_y) = \Delta_0^2(k_x^2 + k_y^2)\) in pentalayer quarter-metal (R5G) is fully gapped for all \((k_x, k_y) \neq (0, 0)\) [Han et al., 2025].

  3. Regime 3 (Twisted Trilayer MATTG Light–Heavy Hybridization): Formalize gmgsLightHeavyHybridizedGapSq and gmgsMattgKondoSuperconductingTc, proving that displacement field \(D > 0\) opens a hybridization gap \(4 V_{\mathrm{hyb}}(D)^2 > 0\) and strictly enhances \(T_c^{\mathrm{MATTG}}(D) > T_{c,0}\) in the light–heavy coexistence regime [Pierce et al., 2025].

  4. Regime 4 (Low-Energy Optical Sum Rule & Quantum-Geometric Superfluid Stiffness): Formalize gmgsLowEnergyOpticalWeight and gmgsTotalSuperfluidStiffness, proving that in the chiral flat-band limit (\(t_{\mathrm{kin}} = 0, w_{AA} = 0\)) at integer filling (\(\delta = 0\)), \(W_{\mathrm{opt}}(0, 0, 0) = 0\), whereas for \(0 < \delta < 1\) and \(g_{\mathrm{FS}} > 0\), \(W_{\mathrm{opt}} > 0\) and \(D_s^{\mathrm{tot}}(0, \Delta_{\mathrm{pair}}, \delta, g_{\mathrm{FS}}) > 0\) [Mendez-Valderrama et al., 2024].

  5. Regime 5 (\(C = \pm 5\) QAHE in Spin–Orbit Proximitized R5G–WS2): Formalize gmgsValleySpinMass, gmgsProximitizedQaheChern, and gmgsQuantizedHallResistanceKOhm, proving \(C_{\mathrm{LAF}} = 0\) at \(\Delta_D = \Delta_{\mathrm{SOC}} = 0\), \(C_{\mathrm{QAH}} = 5\) (and \(-5\)) when only \(K'\uparrow\) inverts, and \(R_{xy}(5) < R_{xy}(1)\) [Han et al., 2024].

  6. Regime 6 (Why Pentalayer \(N=5\): \(C=1\) Anomalous Hall Crystal & Jain FQAH Fractions): Formalize gmgsAnomalousHallCrystalChern, gmgsJainFqahFilling, gmgsFqahHallResistanceRatio, and gmgsFractionalQuasiparticleCharge, proving \(C_{\mathrm{AHC}}(5) = 1\), exact Jain fillings \(\nu = 2/3, 3/5, 4/7, 4/9, 3/7, 2/5\), and fractional charge \(e^*(p=1, s=+1) = 1/3 < 1\) [Lu et al., 2024, Dong et al., 2024].

  7. Regime 7 (Supersolid Quantum Vortices & Hydrodynamic Graphene Whirlpools): Formalize gmgsSupersolidLeggettFraction, gmgsSupersolidVortexNucleationBarrier, and gmgsHydrodynamicWhirlpoolVorticity, proving \(0 < f_s^{\mathrm{Leggett}}(\eta_{\mathrm{mod}}) < 1\), \(E_{\mathrm{vortex}}^{\mathrm{supersolid}} < E_{v,0}\) for \(\eta_{\mathrm{mod}} \in (0, 1)\) [Casotti et al., 2024], and positive whirlpool backflow below the critical device width [Palm et al., 2024].

  8. Regime 8 (Symbiotic Superconducting + QAHE/FQAH Topological Quantum Computing): Formalize gmgsMajoranaEdgeModeCount, gmgsParafermionQuantumDimSq, gmgsTopologicalDecoherenceRate, and gmgsFlatBandKineticInductance, proving that odd-Chern QAHE + SC yields 5 (or 1) chiral Majorana modes, \(\nu = 1/3\) FQAH + SC yields \(\mathbb{Z}_6\) parafermions with \(d_{\mathrm{para}}^2 = 6 > d_{\mathrm{MZM}}^2 = 2\), exponential error suppression with topological gap/separation, and enhanced kinetic inductance in flat bands.

  9. Master & Deep Gap-Closure Theorems: Prove graphene_rhombohedral_trilayer_pentalayer_superconductivity_and_qc_identities, graphene_superconductivity_critique_sum_rule_fqah_and_error_identities, and graphene_superconductivity_deep_gap_closure_identities in Signals.DFT, plus RhombohedralGrapheneSuperconductivityPendingHypothesis in Signals.Pending.
