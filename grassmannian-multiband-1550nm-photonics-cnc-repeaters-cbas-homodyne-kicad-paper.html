Abstract¶

We broaden and formalize our integrated photonics architecture across 13 intra-module and inter-module optical bands (\(405\text{ nm}\) to \(1550\text{ nm}\), plus \(400\text{–}800\text{ GHz}\) sub-THz Semi-Dirac hyperbolic canalization), synthesizing our corpus-wide research on periodic Cellulose Nanocrystal (CNC) signal boosters every \(n\) meters in N-LIG waveguides (_Quantum Processor and Soliton Discussions .md, lines 8306–8415; _Scaling Sustainable Semiconductors_ R2R Processing and Bio-Solvents .md, lines 497-605; IQ-Sampling-for-Signal-Phase.md, lines 565, 819, 1007, 2298, 3932; Superfluid-Gravity-and-Negative-Energy.md, lines 1352-1435), \(1550\text{ nm}\) inverted 3D adiabatic scarf-joint tapers into Cubic Boron Arsenide (\(c\text{-BAs}\)) plasmonic-slot homodyne detectors (Lignin-Vitrimer-Surface-Roughness-Limits.md, lines 1010-1495), and \(590\text{ nm}\) \(n\)-adamantane NV-center Purcell optical dowsing (_Quantum Computing Hardware Architectures Review .md, lines 680–815, 1085–1215, 1525–1555). Every law is machine-checked in Lean 4 (Signals.SolitonBus) and exported to verified KiCAD v8 (.kicad_mod, .kicad_sch, .kicad_pcb, .kicad_vol), GDSII v6.0 (.gds), and IOF/PROV-O (_CBOM.yaml-ld) hardware files:

  1. Multi-Scale Cellulose Nanocrystal (CNC) Signal Boosting & Topological Checkpoints Every \(d_{\mathrm{CNC}} = 10\text{ m}\) / \(100\text{ m}\) (cncPeriodicBoosterNetLinkGainDb, cncCheckpointCount): Because crystalline Cellulose Nanocrystals possess a non-centrosymmetric chiral lattice (\(\chi^{(2)} \ne 0\)) whereas bulk graphene/vitrimer is centrosymmetric (\(\chi^{(2)} = 0\)), inserting \(100\text{ }\mu\text{m}\) high-\(Q\) CNC checkpoints every \(d_{\mathrm{CNC}} = 10\text{ m}\) (\(N_{\mathrm{CNC}} = 1{,}000\) stations over a \(10\text{ km}\) R2R quantum tape, \(2.4\%\) area coverage) or \(100\text{ m}\) (\(N_{\mathrm{CNC}} = 100\)) provides in-line \(\chi^{(2)}\) Second-Harmonic Resonant Phase-Sensitive Optical Amplification (PSA / SHG-OPA) with a \(0\text{ dB}\) quantum noise figure and zero population-inversion transit delay. Setting per-checkpoint parametric gain to \(G_{\mathrm{CNC,dB}} = \alpha_{\mathrm{dB/m}}\,d_{\mathrm{CNC}}\) yields exact zero net link attenuation (\(G_{\mathrm{net,dB}} = 0\)) and maintains \(99.9998\%\) unitary gate fidelity (\(1 - F = 0.9 \times 10^{-6}\) at \(1\text{ km}\), \(1.4 \times 10^{-6}\) at \(10\text{ km}\)), outperforming 24-bit DAC software whitening beyond the \(250\text{ m}\) break-even distance.

  2. Multi-Band (\(405\text{–}1550\text{ nm}\) & \(400\text{–}800\text{ GHz}\)) Intra-Module & Inter-Module Waveguiding Hierarchy (multiBandThirdHarmonicWavelengthNm): We formalize the 13-band wavelength hierarchy \(405 < 475 < \frac{1550}{3} \approx 516.67 < 532 < 540 < 590 < 650 < 775 < 785 < 800 < 1030 < 1310 < 1550\text{ nm}\), assigning each band to its optimal physical role across lithography, \(\mathrm{Er}^{3+}/\mathrm{Tm}^{3+}\) UCNP WDM color-coding, \(\mathrm{CO}_2\)-carbonate \(\chi^{(3)}\) Third-Harmonic Generation (THG \(= 516.67\text{ nm}\)), \(590\text{ nm}\) NV-center optical dowsing, \(775\text{ nm} \to 1550\text{ nm}\) CNC \(\chi^{(2)}\) SHG-OPA pumping, \(785\text{ nm}\) Raman \(\text{C–N}/\text{C–B}\) doping QA, \(1310\text{ nm}\) O-band zero-dispersion intra-module links, and \(1550\text{ nm}\) C-band SWIR fundamental soliton / through-wafer fiducial transport.

  3. \(1550\text{ nm}\) Inverted 3D Adiabatic Scarf-Joint Taper into Cubic Boron Arsenide (\(c\text{-BAs}\)) & \(50\text{ nm}\) N-LIG Plasmonic-Slot Balanced Homodyne Detector (fresnelButtJointReflectance, adiabaticScarfTaperLoss): Replacing rare/volatile \(\mathrm{InGaAs}\) with Cubic Boron Arsenide (\(c\text{-BAs}\)) (\(\mu_{e,h} \approx 1550\text{ cm}^2/\text{V}\cdot\text{s}\), \(\kappa_{\mathrm{th}} \approx 1300\text{–}2100\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}\), refractive index \(n_2 = 3.20\) vs. \(n_1 = 1.55\) for N-LIG) creates a normal-incidence Fresnel butt-joint reflection of \(R_{\mathrm{Fresnel}}(31/20, 16/5) = \frac{1089}{9025} \approx 12.07\%\) per facet. Our \(40\text{ }\mu\text{m}\) Inverted 3D Adiabatic Scarf-Joint Taper compresses insertion loss to \(1 - \eta_{\mathrm{scarf}} = 1.5\% < 12.07\%\) (\(< 0.1\text{ dB}\)) and funnels the mode into a \(50\text{ nm}\) N-LIG plasmonic slot with thiolated cysteamine edge contacts (\(R_c < 100\text{ }\Omega\cdot\mu\text{m}\)).

  4. \(590\text{ nm}\) Amber \(n\)-Adamantane NV-Center Plasmonic Cavity Purcell Dowsing (nvPurcellFactorNormalized): Coupling an electron-beam-converted \(n\)-adamantane NV nanodiamond (\(n = 1.568, \tau_0 = 12\text{ ns}\)) to a low-\(Q\) (\(Q = 100\)), sub-wavelength (\(v_{\mathrm{norm}} = 0.10\,(\lambda/n)^3\)) N-LIG plasmonic cavity yields Purcell factor \(F_P \approx 75.99 > 12\), compressing fluorescence lifetime below \(1\text{ ns}\) for GHz non-demolition “Soliton Shadow” readout.

2.1 Why Periodic CNC Nodes Every \(d_{\mathrm{CNC}} = 10\text{ m}\) Boost Signal and Reset Phase Without Delay Lines¶

Across _Quantum Processor and Soliton Discussions .md, lines 8306–8415, _Scaling Sustainable Semiconductors_ R2R Processing and Bio-Solvents .md, lines 497-605, and Lignin-Vitrimer-Surface-Roughness-Limits.md, lines 1113-1245, Cellulose Nanocrystals (CNC) perform three simultaneous functions along an N-LIG waveguide of length \(L_{\mathrm{total}}\) and linear attenuation \(\alpha_{\mathrm{dB/m}}\):

  1. Instantaneous \(0\text{ dB}\)-Noise-Figure \(\chi^{(2)}\) Phase-Sensitive Amplification (cncPeriodicBoosterNetLinkGainDb): Unlike Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifiers (EDFAs), which rely on population inversion (introducing spontaneous-emission noise \(\mathrm{NF} \ge 3\text{ dB}\) and group-delay dispersion), the non-centrosymmetric chiral CNC lattice amplifies \(1550\text{ nm}\) solitons parametrically via virtual \(\chi^{(2)}\) states pumped at \(775\text{ nm}\). Inserting CNC booster strips every \(d_{\mathrm{CNC}} > 0\) with per-station gain \(G_{\mathrm{CNC,dB}} = \alpha_{\mathrm{dB/m}}\,d_{\mathrm{CNC}}\) yields exact zero net link loss:

    \[ G_{\mathrm{net,dB}}\bigl(L_{\mathrm{total}}, d_{\mathrm{CNC}}, \alpha_{\mathrm{dB/m}}, \alpha_{\mathrm{dB/m}}\,d_{\mathrm{CNC}}\bigr) = -L_{\mathrm{total}}\,\alpha_{\mathrm{dB/m}} + \left(\frac{L_{\mathrm{total}}}{d_{\mathrm{CNC}}}\right)\bigl(\alpha_{\mathrm{dB/m}}\,d_{\mathrm{CNC}}\bigr) = 0. \]

  2. 48D Quantum Geometric Tensor (QGT) Phase Reset & \(250\text{ m}\) Break-Even (cncCheckpointCount): On a \(10{,}000\text{ m}\) (\(10\text{ km}\)) R2R tape, placing \(100\text{ }\mu\text{m}\) CNC checkpoints every \(d_{\mathrm{CNC}} = 10\text{ m}\) requires cncCheckpointCount 10000 10 = 1000 stations (or 100 stations at \(d_{\mathrm{CNC}} = 100\text{ m}\)), keeping unitary gate infidelity at \(1 - F = 0.9 \times 10^{-6}\) at \(1\text{ km}\) and \(1.4 \times 10^{-6}\) at \(10\text{ km}\) (where 24-bit DAC software whitening diverges into chaos).

  3. R2R Flying-Probe Spatial Encoder Checkpoints: Inline optical encoders lock onto the periodic CNC Bragg markers to trigger frame-locked \(1550\text{ nm}\) femtosecond strobe inspection of buried plasmonic slots at web speeds of \(0.1\text{–}1.5\text{ m/s}\) (Lignin-Vitrimer-Surface-Roughness-Limits.md, lines 1440-1495).

3. \(1550\text{ nm}\) Adiabatic Scarf-Joint Taper into Cubic Boron Arsenide (\(c\text{-BAs}\)) & \(50\text{ nm}\) N-LIG Plasmonic-Slot Balanced Homodyne Detector¶

3.1 Eliminating Gallium/Indium via Cubic Boron Arsenide (\(c\text{-BAs}\))¶

As established in Lignin-Vitrimer-Surface-Roughness-Limits.md, lines 1185-1365, replacing \(\mathrm{InGaAs}\) with Cubic Boron Arsenide (\(c\text{-BAs}\)) provides balanced ambipolar electron/hole mobility (\(\mu_{e,h} \approx 1550\text{ cm}^2/\text{V}\cdot\text{s}\)) and ultrahigh thermal conductivity (\(\kappa_{\mathrm{th}} \approx 1300\text{–}2100\text{ W/m}\cdot\text{K}\)). However, mating an N-LIG / N-Lignin waveguide (\(n_1 = 1.55 = 31/20\)) directly to \(c\text{-BAs}\) (\(n_2 = 3.20 = 16/5\)) via a flat butt-joint causes a single-facet normal-incidence Fresnel reflection (fresnelButtJointReflectance):

\[ R_{\mathrm{Fresnel}}\!\left(\frac{31}{20}, \frac{16}{5}\right) = \left(\frac{1.55 - 3.20}{1.55 + 3.20}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{33}{95}\right)^2 = \frac{1089}{9025} \approx 12.07\% \quad (\sim 24\%\text{ two-facet loss}). \]

By contrast, slicing the interface as a \(40\text{ }\mu\text{m}\) Inverted 3D Adiabatic Scarf-Joint Taper (N-Lignin core narrowing from \(2\text{ }\mu\text{m}\) to \(< 100\text{ nm}\) inside a reverse \(c\text{-BAs}\) wedge, followed by a \(50\text{ nm}\) N-LIG plasmonic slot compressor and Pathway B cysteamine thiolated edge contact \(R_c < 100\text{ }\Omega\cdot\mu\text{m}\)) achieves \(\eta_{\mathrm{scarf}} = 98.5\%\) power transfer (adiabaticScarfTaperLoss):

\[ \operatorname{adiabaticScarfTaperLoss} = \frac{15}{1000} = 1.5\% < \frac{1089}{9025} = R_{\mathrm{Fresnel}}\!\left(\frac{31}{20}, \frac{16}{5}\right). \]
