## 1. Executive Summary & Unified Theoretical Framework (`G-IEP`)

Modern complementary field-effect transistors (`CFETs`), gate-all-around nanosheet/nanowire transistors (`GAAFETs`), wide-bandgap $\mathrm{SiC}$ power MOSFETs, high-$\kappa$ $\mathrm{HfO_2/SiO_2/Si}$ gate stacks, and high-mobility $\mathrm{Ge} / \mathrm{SiGe}$ channels expose semiconductor interfaces to complex 3D curvatures, crystallographic orientations, and multi-step thermal/ambient recipes (oxidation, cooling, post-oxidation annealing, and forming-gas passivation). **Discovered Materials Grand Challenge Problem 02** (*"Interfacial Electrical Properties from Processing History — Predict how industrially relevant semiconductor interfaces form, evolve and acquire their electrical properties from the fabrication history: growth, cooling and annealing"*) {cite}`discoveredmaterials2026problem02` requires a **single computationally tractable framework**, unchanged in functional form, that maps the starting material, geometry, crystal orientation, composition, and thermal/ambient history to three target property classes:
1. **Layer thicknesses**: Oxide thickness $x_{\mathrm{ox}}(t)$, interlayer thickness $x_{\mathrm{IL}}(t)$, and consumed substrate thickness $\Delta x_{\mathrm{sub}}(t) = x_{\mathrm{ox}}(t) / \gamma_{\mathrm{PBR}}$ (where $\gamma_{\mathrm{PBR}} = 2.27$ is the Pilling–Bedworth molar volume expansion ratio for $\mathrm{Si} \to \mathrm{SiO_2}$),
2. **Composition profiles $C(z)$**: Oxidant steady-state flux balance $F_1 = F_2 = F_3$, suboxide transition width, $\mathrm{C}$ interstitial / $\mathrm{CO}$ out-diffusion in $\mathrm{SiC/SiO_2}$, $^{18}\mathrm{O}_2$ / $V_{\mathrm{O}}^{\bullet\bullet}$ exchange in $\mathrm{HfO_2/SiO_2/Si}$, and $\mathrm{Ge}$ "snowplow" enrichment pile-up $C_{\mathrm{Ge}}(z)$ in $\mathrm{SiGe/oxide}$, and
3. **Electrical quality**: Energy-resolved interface-trap density $D_{\mathrm{it}}(E)$ ($\mathrm{cm^{-2}\,eV^{-1}}$ across $E \in [E_v, E_c]$) and fixed oxide charge $Q_f$ ($\mathrm{cm^{-2}}$),

validated simultaneously across **all five benchmark semiconductor interfaces**:
- **Interface 1 — Planar $\mathrm{Si/SiO_2}$ across `(100)`, `(511)`, `(110)`, and `(111)` orientations, oxidation history, and $\mathrm{H_2}$ passivation** (Vitkavage, Irene & Massoud, *J. Appl. Phys.* **68**, 5262 (1990) {cite}`vitkavage1990interface`; Deal & Grove, *J. Appl. Phys.* **36**, 3770 (1965) {cite}`deal1965general`),
- **Interface 2 — Curved $\mathrm{Si}$ Nanowires and Nanosheets (`GAAFET` / `CFET`) with viscoelastic stress-retarded self-limiting oxidation** (Kao, McVittie, Nix & Saraswat, *IEEE Trans. Electron Devices* **35**, 25 (1988) {cite}`kao1988twodimensional`),
- **Interface 3 — $\mathrm{SiC/SiO_2}$ Si-face `(0001)` vs. C-face `(000\bar{1})` growth kinetics, carbon removal, and $\mathrm{NO}/\mathrm{H_2}$ passivation** (Song et al., *J. Appl. Phys.* **95**, 4953 (2004) {cite}`song2004modified`),
- **Interface 4 — $\mathrm{HfO_2/SiO_2/Si}$ High-$\kappa$ Gate Stack oxygen exchange, $\mathrm{SiO_2}$ interlayer regrowth, and trap/dipole evolution** (Goncharova et al., *Phys. Rev. B* **83**, 115329 (2011) {cite}`goncharova2011oxygen`), and
- **Interface 5 — $\mathrm{Ge/GeO_2}$ volatile $\mathrm{GeO(g)}$ desorption loss and $\mathrm{SiGe/oxide}$ selective oxidation & $\mathrm{Ge}$ snowplow enrichment** (Wang et al., *Appl. Phys. Lett.* **111**, 052101 (2017) {cite}`wang2017interfacial`; Rabie, Haddara & Carette, *J. Appl. Phys.* **98**, 074904 (2005) {cite}`rabie2005analytical`).

## 2. Staged Execution Plan Across All Five Interfaces

### Phase 1: Formal Verification in Lean 4 (`Signals.DFT` Section 17 & `Signals.Pending` Section 35)
1. **Universal 3-Flux Deal–Grove–Massoud & Pilling–Bedworth Laws**:
   - Formalize `giepThreeFluxSteadyState`, `giepDealGroveTimeForThickness`, `giepConsumedSiliconThickness`, `giepHydrogenPassivatedDit`, `giepEnergyResolvedDit`, and `giepDealOxideChargeCoolingTriangle`.
2. **Interface 1 — Planar $\mathrm{Si/SiO_2}$ Orientation & Passivation (`Vitkavage, Irene & Massoud, JAP 1990`)**:
   - Formalize the Vitkavage et al. (1990) crystallographic orientation hierarchy `(100) < (511) < (110) < (111)` for linear oxidation rate $B/A$, unpassivated/passivated midgap $D_{\mathrm{it}}$, and fixed oxide charge $Q_f$, proving $\mathrm{H_2}$ forming-gas annealing (`FGA`) strictly reduces $D_{\mathrm{it}}$ by $> 10\times$ and that $D_{\mathrm{it}}(E)$ forms a U-shaped band-gap spectrum with minimum at midgap $E = E_m$.
3. **Interface 2 — Curved $\mathrm{Si}$ Nanowires & Nanosheets (`Kao et al., IEEE TED 1988`)**:
   - Formalize `giepNanowireStressRetardedRate` and prove that compressive normal stress $\sigma_n > 0$ and tensile oxide pressure $P > 0$ on tighter nanowire radii strictly retard oxidation compared to planar $\mathrm{Si}$ while elevating unpassivated interfacial bond strain.
4. **Interface 3 — $\mathrm{SiC/SiO_2}$ Face-Dependent Kinetics & Carbon Removal (`Song et al., JAP 2004`)**:
   - Formalize `giepSiCCarbonRetardedRate` and prove that carbon interstitial accumulation retards oxidation and accounts for the faster oxidation of the `(000\bar{1})` C-face relative to the `(0001)` Si-face, while $\mathrm{NO}$ annealing passivates carbon-cluster near-interface traps.
5. **Interface 4 — $\mathrm{HfO_2/SiO_2/Si}$ Gate Stack Interlayer Regrowth & `EOT` (`Goncharova et al., PRB 2011`)**:
   - Formalize `giepHfO2InterlayerRegrowthSq` and `giepHighKGateStackEot`, proving monotonic $\mathrm{SiO_2}$ interlayer regrowth with oxygen diffusion flux and annealing time, and sub-nanometer `EOT` scaling.
6. **Interface 5 — $\mathrm{Ge/GeO_2}$ Desorption & $\mathrm{SiGe/oxide}$ Snowplow Pile-Up (`Wang et al., APL 2017`; `Rabie et al., JAP 2005`)**:
   - Formalize `giepGeOxideNetRate` and `giepSiGeSnowplowSurfaceFraction`, proving the transition from net oxide growth ($v_{\mathrm{growth}} > v_{\mathrm{desorb}}$) to volatile $\mathrm{GeO(g)}$ oxide loss ($v_{\mathrm{growth}} < v_{\mathrm{desorb}}$) and $\mathrm{Ge}$ snowplow enrichment at the $\mathrm{SiGe/oxide}$ boundary ($z = 0$).
