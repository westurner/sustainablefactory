   $$
   \frac{\|w_B^{\mathbb{C}}(M_{\mathrm{aot}})\|^2}{\|w_A^{\mathbb{C}}(M_{\mathrm{aot}})\|^2} = \frac{I_{\mathrm{tag}}}{I_{\mathrm{car}}}.
   $$

3. **Time-Reversed Ultrasonically Encoded (`TRUE`) Optical Focusing & Iterative `DORT` Compression (`trueOpticalPeakToBackgroundGain`, `iterativeTrueFocalVarianceSq`)**:
   Recording the complex hologram of the $\omega_{+1}$-tagged wavefront on a 2D detector array and phase-conjugating it across $N_{\mathrm{modes}} > 1$ independent optical modes on an SLM or active `rGO-Vitrimer` mask yields the phase-only time-reversal peak-to-background enhancement ([Vellekoop & Mosk 2007](#vellekoop2007); [Xu, Liu, & Wang 2011](#xu2011true)):
