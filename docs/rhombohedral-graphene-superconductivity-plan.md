## 1. Executive Summary & Synthesis of the Eight Foundational Regimes (`G-MGS`)

Recent experimental breakthroughs (2021–2025) across crystalline **rhombohedral trilayer graphene (`RTG`, $N=3$)** {cite}`zhou2021rtg_sc,zhou2021rtg_metals,arp2024intervalley,naturephys2024rtg_briefing`, **magic-angle twisted symmetric trilayer graphene (`MATTG`)** {cite}`pierce2025light_heavy,park2021mattg,hao2021mattg`, and **rhombohedral pentalayer graphene (`R5G`, $N=5$)** {cite}`lu2024fqah,dong2024ahc_prl,mitnews2024fractions,han2024large_qahe,han2025chiral_sc` have revealed an extraordinary tapestry of intertwined quantum phases in pure carbon heterostructures—without magnetic dopants:
1. **Why Rhombohedral Stacking ($N=3$ vs. $N=5$) Generates Ultra-Flat Bands (`R1`)**: In ABC-stacked $N$-layer graphene, dimerized interlayer hopping $\gamma_1 \approx 380\text{ meV}$ projects the low-energy states onto the outer boundary sublattices $(A_1, B_N) \in \mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2, 2N)$ with chiral dispersion $E_N(k) \propto k^N / \gamma_1^{N-1}$, Berry phase $N\pi$ ($3\pi$ for $N=3$, $5\pi$ for $N=5$) {cite}`koshino2009trigonal,zhang2010abc`, and van Hove density-of-states divergence $g_N(E) \propto E^{\frac{2}{N} - 1}$ ($E^{-1/3}$ in trilayer vs. a much sharper $E^{-3/5}$ in pentalayer).
2. **Multiple Superconducting Phases in Rhombohedral Trilayer & Pentalayer Graphene (`R2`)**: As carrier density $n_e$ and displacement field $D$ tune the Stoner parameter $U_{\mathrm{int}}\,\nu_{\mathrm{DOS}}(n_e, D)$, the four spin–valley flavors $(K\uparrow, K\downarrow, K'\uparrow, K'\downarrow)$ undergo a cascade of projections onto $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(f, 4)$ ($f \in \{4, 2, 1\}$):
   - **$\mathrm{SC}_1$ ($f = 4$, Intervalley-Coherent `IVC` Paramagnetic Parent in `RTG`, $T_{c,1} \approx 106\text{ mK}$)**: Spin-singlet pairing stabilized by intrinsic Kane–Mele spin–orbit coupling $\lambda_{\mathrm{KM}} \approx 45\text{ }\mu\text{eV}$ {cite}`arp2024intervalley,naturephys2024rtg_briefing`, obeying the paramagnetic Pauli limit ($B_{c\parallel}/B_P \approx 1.0$) {cite}`zhou2021rtg_sc`.
   - **$\mathrm{SC}_2$ ($f = 2$, Spin-Polarized Half-Metal Parent in `RTG`, $T_{c,2} \approx 50\text{ mK}$)**: Equal-spin triplet valley-singlet Plücker pairing on $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)$ that violates the Pauli limit by $> 10\times$ ($B_{c\parallel}/B_P \approx 11.0$) {cite}`zhou2021rtg_sc`.
   - **$\mathrm{SC}_{\mathrm{chiral}}$ ($f = 1$, Spin- & Valley-Polarized Quarter-Metal Parent in `R5G`/`R4G`, $T_{c,\mathrm{chiral}} \approx 300\text{ mK}$)**: Intravalley chiral $p_x \pm i p_y$ superconductivity inside a single isospin flavor $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(1,4)$ with spontaneous orbital time-reversal symmetry breaking and magnetic hysteresis {cite}`han2025chiral_sc`.
3. **Tunable Light–Heavy Electron Interplay in Twisted Trilayer Graphene (`R3`)**: In `MATTG` ($\theta \approx 1.56^\circ$), mirror symmetry $M_z$ separates a heavy magic-angle flat band ($f$-orbital) from a light high-velocity Dirac cone ($c$-orbital); a displacement field $D$ hybridizes them on $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,6)$, boosting $T_{c,\max}^{\mathrm{MATTG}} \approx 2.9\text{ K}$ via heavy-fermion pairing glue + light-fermion phase stiffness {cite}`pierce2025light_heavy,park2021mattg,hao2021mattg`.
4. **Low-Energy Optical Sum Rule & Quantum-Geometric Superfluid Stiffness (`R4`)**: In the strong-coupling flat-band limit at integer fillings $\nu \in \mathbb{Z}$ ($\delta = |\nu - \nu_{\mathrm{int}}| = 0$), the low-energy optical spectral weight vanishes identically ($W_{\mathrm{opt}}(\delta=0) = 0$) even when interaction-induced single-particle dispersion is finite; upon doping ($0 < \delta < 1$) or strain/tunneling ($t_{\mathrm{strain}}, w_{AA} > 0$), superfluid stiffness $D_s^{\mathrm{tot}} = D_s^{\mathrm{conv}} + D_s^{\mathrm{geom}}$ is governed by the Fubini–Study quantum metric trace $\operatorname{Tr}g_{\mathrm{FS}}$ on $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(k, N)$ {cite}`mendez2024sumrule,peotta2015superfluid`.
5. **Large Quantum Anomalous Hall Effect ($C = \pm 5$) in Spin–Orbit Proximitized `R5G–WS2` (`R5`)**: In crystalline pentalayer rhombohedral graphene proximitized by $\mathrm{WS_2}$ at $n_e = 0$ and $B = 0\text{ T}$, combining the Layer-Antiferromagnetic (`LAF`) gap $\Delta_{\mathrm{LAF}}$, displacement field $\Delta_D$ ($D/\varepsilon_0 = -0.165\text{ V/nm}$), and proximity Ising SOC $\Delta_{\mathrm{SOC}}$ selectively inverts only the $K'\uparrow$ valley gap, aligning $C_{K\uparrow} = -5/2$ and $C_{K'\uparrow} = -5/2$ to yield $C = \pm 5$ and $R_{xy} = \pm \frac{h}{5e^2} \approx \pm 5.16\text{ k}\Omega$ up to $T \approx 1.5\text{ K}$ {cite}`han2024large_qahe`.
6. **Why Pentalayer ($N=5$) Hosts the $C = 1$ Anomalous Hall Crystal & Fractional Quantum Anomalous Hall (`FQAH`) Effect (`R6`)**: Although non-interacting pentalayer graphene has winding number $N = 5$, strong Coulomb exchange at large $D$ drives spontaneous formation of an **Anomalous Hall Crystal (`AHC`)** that unwinds $N - 1 = 4$ Berry-flux quanta into remote bands, isolating a flat $C_{\mathrm{AHC}} = 1$ band at $B = 0\text{ T}$ that hosts the Jain composite-fermion FQAH hierarchy $\nu = \frac{p}{2p \pm 1} \in \{2/3, 3/5, 4/7, 4/9, 3/7, 2/5\}$ with fractional charges $e^* = e/(2p \pm 1)$ {cite}`lu2024fqah,dong2024ahc_prl,mitnews2024fractions`.
7. **Quantum Tornadoes in Supersolids, Anomalous Hall Crystals, & Hydrodynamic Graphene Whirlpools (`R7`)**: Quantized vortices ($\Gamma_\ell = 2\pi \ell \hbar / m^*$) in a density-modulated supersolid or superconducting anomalous Hall crystal nucleate in low-density inter-droplet channels with reduced core barrier $E_v(1 - \eta_{\mathrm{mod}})$ and Leggett superfluid fraction $f_s = 1 - \eta_{\mathrm{mod}}^2 < 1$ {cite}`casotti2024supersolid,leggett1970supersolid`, complementing viscous electron whirlpools in room-temperature hydrodynamic graphene when $D_\nu > W/\pi$ {cite}`palm2024whirlpools`.
8. **Symbiotic Superconductivity + QAHE/FQAH Architecture for Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing (`R8`)**: Rather than competing, flat-band graphene superconductivity and QAHE/FQAH form a monolithic gate-defined quantum computing stack ({download}`data/chats/_Superconductivity vs. QAHE in Quantum Computing .md, lines 114-165 <../data/chats/_Superconductivity vs. QAHE in Quantum Computing .md>`): lateral junctions between intrinsic rhombohedral graphene superconductors ($\mathrm{SC}_{\mathrm{chiral}} / \mathrm{SC}_2$) and odd-Chern QAHE ($C = \pm 5, \pm 1$) or FQAH ($\nu = 2/3, 1/3$) regions host **unpaired chiral Majorana edge modes** ($d_{\mathrm{MZM}} = \sqrt{2}$) and **$\mathbb{Z}_{2m}$ parafermions** ($d_{\mathrm{para}} = \sqrt{2m} = \sqrt{6}$ at $m=3$) at zero magnetic field ($B = 0\text{ T}$) {cite}`han2024large_qahe,lu2024fqah,clarke2013parafermions,lian2018chiral_majorana`.

## 3. Staged Implementation Plan Across Lean 4, Rust/WASM, Python, and MyST

### Phase 1: Formal Verification in Lean 4 (`Signals.DFT` Section 18 & `Signals.Pending` Section 36)
1. **Regime 1 ($N$-Layer Rhombohedral Flat-Band Hierarchy)**: Formalize `gmgsRhombohedralEnergySq`, `gmgsFlatBandBandwidth`, and `gmgsEffectiveInteractionRs`, proving $W_5 < W_3 < W_1$ and $r_s(N=5) > r_s(N=3) > r_s(N=1)$ for $0 < t_0 < \gamma_1$.
2. **Regime 2 (Rhombohedral Trilayer & Pentalayer Multi-Phase Superconductivity)**: Formalize `gmgsStonerFlavorCount`, `gmgsPauliLimitField`, `gmgsTripletEffectiveCriticalField`, `gmgsKaneMeleEnhancedTc`, and `gmgsChiralPxPyGapSq`, proving:
   - $\mathrm{SC}_1$ obeys the Pauli limit while $\mathrm{SC}_2$ spin-triplet pairing achieves $B_{c\parallel}^{\mathrm{triplet}} / B_P > 10$,
   - Intrinsic/proximitized Kane–Mele SOC $\lambda_{\mathrm{KM}} > 0$ enhances $\mathrm{SC}_1$ / IVC $T_c$ {cite}`arp2024intervalley`,
   - Chiral $p_x + i p_y$ gap $\Delta_{\mathrm{chiral}}^2(k_x, k_y) = \Delta_0^2(k_x^2 + k_y^2)$ in pentalayer quarter-metal (`R5G`) is fully gapped for all $(k_x, k_y) \neq (0, 0)$ {cite}`han2025chiral_sc`.
3. **Regime 3 (Twisted Trilayer `MATTG` Light–Heavy Hybridization)**: Formalize `gmgsLightHeavyHybridizedGapSq` and `gmgsMattgKondoSuperconductingTc`, proving that displacement field $D > 0$ opens a hybridization gap $4 V_{\mathrm{hyb}}(D)^2 > 0$ and strictly enhances $T_c^{\mathrm{MATTG}}(D) > T_{c,0}$ in the light–heavy coexistence regime {cite}`pierce2025light_heavy`.
4. **Regime 4 (Low-Energy Optical Sum Rule & Quantum-Geometric Superfluid Stiffness)**: Formalize `gmgsLowEnergyOpticalWeight` and `gmgsTotalSuperfluidStiffness`, proving that in the chiral flat-band limit ($t_{\mathrm{kin}} = 0, w_{AA} = 0$) at integer filling ($\delta = 0$), $W_{\mathrm{opt}}(0, 0, 0) = 0$, whereas for $0 < \delta < 1$ and $g_{\mathrm{FS}} > 0$, $W_{\mathrm{opt}} > 0$ and $D_s^{\mathrm{tot}}(0, \Delta_{\mathrm{pair}}, \delta, g_{\mathrm{FS}}) > 0$ {cite}`mendez2024sumrule`.
5. **Regime 5 ($C = \pm 5$ QAHE in Spin–Orbit Proximitized `R5G–WS2`)**: Formalize `gmgsValleySpinMass`, `gmgsProximitizedQaheChern`, and `gmgsQuantizedHallResistanceKOhm`, proving $C_{\mathrm{LAF}} = 0$ at $\Delta_D = \Delta_{\mathrm{SOC}} = 0$, $C_{\mathrm{QAH}} = 5$ (and $-5$) when only $K'\uparrow$ inverts, and $R_{xy}(5) < R_{xy}(1)$ {cite}`han2024large_qahe`.
6. **Regime 6 (Why Pentalayer $N=5$: $C=1$ Anomalous Hall Crystal & Jain `FQAH` Fractions)**: Formalize `gmgsAnomalousHallCrystalChern`, `gmgsJainFqahFilling`, `gmgsFqahHallResistanceRatio`, and `gmgsFractionalQuasiparticleCharge`, proving $C_{\mathrm{AHC}}(5) = 1$, exact Jain fillings $\nu = 2/3, 3/5, 4/7, 4/9, 3/7, 2/5$, and fractional charge $e^*(p=1, s=+1) = 1/3 < 1$ {cite}`lu2024fqah,dong2024ahc_prl`.
7. **Regime 7 (Supersolid Quantum Tornadoes & Hydrodynamic Graphene Whirlpools)**: Formalize `gmgsSupersolidLeggettFraction`, `gmgsSupersolidVortexNucleationBarrier`, and `gmgsHydrodynamicWhirlpoolVorticity`, proving $0 < f_s^{\mathrm{Leggett}}(\eta_{\mathrm{mod}}) < 1$, $E_{\mathrm{vortex}}^{\mathrm{supersolid}} < E_{v,0}$ for $\eta_{\mathrm{mod}} \in (0, 1)$ {cite}`casotti2024supersolid`, and positive whirlpool backflow below the critical device width {cite}`palm2024whirlpools`.
8. **Regime 8 (Symbiotic Superconducting + QAHE/FQAH Topological Quantum Computing)**: Formalize `gmgsMajoranaEdgeModeCount`, `gmgsParafermionQuantumDimSq`, `gmgsTopologicalDecoherenceRate`, and `gmgsFlatBandKineticInductance`, proving that odd-Chern QAHE + SC yields 5 (or 1) chiral Majorana modes, $\nu = 1/3$ FQAH + SC yields $\mathbb{Z}_6$ parafermions with $d_{\mathrm{para}}^2 = 6 > d_{\mathrm{MZM}}^2 = 2$, exponential error suppression with topological gap/separation, and enhanced kinetic inductance in flat bands.
9. **Master & Deep Gap-Closure Theorems**: Prove `graphene_rhombohedral_trilayer_pentalayer_superconductivity_and_qc_identities`, `graphene_superconductivity_critique_sum_rule_fqah_and_error_identities`, and `graphene_superconductivity_deep_gap_closure_identities` in `Signals.DFT`, plus `RhombohedralGrapheneSuperconductivityPendingHypothesis` in `Signals.Pending`.
