    • Remaining: Non-paraxial 3D Rayleigh–Sommerfeld vector boundary-integral remainder, arbitrary-rank (\(k \ge 4\)) Plücker syzygies, and calibrated laboratory interferometric/Raman/thermal-wave dataset ingestion.
