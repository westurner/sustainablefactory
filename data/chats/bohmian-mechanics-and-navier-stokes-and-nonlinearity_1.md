Let w = x + iy be the complex spatial coordinate, and let ε(t) = ν t + i(t - t₀) track the viscous core radius and reconnection time t₀:

$$Z(x,y,t)=\left(\begin{matrix}w^{2}-\epsilon (t)&\alpha \={w}\\ \beta w&1\\ \partial _{x}(w^{2}-\epsilon (t))&\alpha \\ \partial _{y}(w^{2}-\epsilon (t))&-i\alpha \end{matrix}\right)=\left(\begin{matrix}w^{2}-\epsilon (t)&\alpha \={w}\\ \beta w&1\\ 2w&\alpha \\ 2iw&-i\alpha \end{matrix}\right)$$

where α, β > 0 set the dual shear coupling and circulation strength.

Watching the Schubert Cell Transition

Computing the six Plücker minors $p_{ij} = z_{i1}z_{j2} - z_{i2}z_{j1}$:

*   **Density Minor:** p₁₂ = (w² - ε(t)) - αβ |w|²

*   **Momentum Minors:** $p_{13} = \alpha(w^2 - \epsilon(t) - 2\vert{}w\vert{}^2)$, p₂₃ = αβ w - 2w

*   **Stress Minor:** p₃₄ = -4iα w

1.  **Before Reconnection (t < t₀):** Two distinct vortex cores sit at $w_\pm \approx \pm \sqrt{\epsilon(t)}$ where p₁₂ = 0. Away from the cores, p₁₂ ≠ 0, so the fluid lives in the open big cell Ω₀.
2.  **At the Reconnection Singularity (t = t₀, w = 0):** Both vortices merge at the origin. Here, p₁₂(0,0,t₀) = 0, so in classical Ω₀ coordinates, the velocity v ~ p₁₃/p₁₂ → ∞.
3.  **Chart Switch to Ω₁₃:** At (w=0, t=t₀), note that p₁₃ does **not** vanish if ε(t₀) = ν t₀ ≠ 0 (or if we shift the primal-dual offset); even as ν → 0, p₁₄ and p₂₄ remain non-zero near the core. Switching to the $\Omega_{13} = \{p_{13} \neq 0\}$ chart, the local affine coordinates are:

$$\xi _{1}=\frac{p_{12}}{p_{13}}=\frac{w^{2}-\epsilon (t)-\alpha \beta |{}w|{}^{2}}{\alpha (w^{2}-\epsilon (t)-2|{}w|{}^{2})},\qquad \xi _{2}=\frac{p_{34}}{p_{13}}=\frac{-4iw}{w^{2}-\epsilon (t)-2|{}w|{}^{2}}$$

    Both ξ₁ and ξ₂ pass smoothly through zero at w = 0 without any blowup. As t > t₀, p₁₂ becomes non-zero again, and the trajectory re-enters Ω₀ with the vortex dipoles rotated by 90°.

3\. Structure-Preserving Numerical Integrator (Generalized iGPE)

To integrate $i\hbar \partial_t Z = \mathcal{H}_{\text{eff}}(Z)Z - Z\Lambda(Z)$ without leaving the Grassmannian manifold or violating the Plücker relations, we generalize **Grassmannian Purified Extrapolation (iGPE)** using a **Lie-Trotter-Munthe-Kaas geometric splitting**:

Algorithm: Grassmannian Plücker–Madelung Stepper ($Z^n \to Z^{n+1}$)

1.  **Linear Dispersion & Viscous Half-Step (Spectral/FFT):**
    Evolve the kinetic and constant-coefficient viscous operators in Fourier space over Δ t / 2:

$$\~{Z}^{(1)}=\exp \left(-\frac{i\Delta t}{2\hbar }\mathcal{H}_{0}(\mathbf{k},\nu )\right)Z^{n}$$

2.  **Nonlinear Plücker-Potential Step (Pointwise):**
    Compute the six Plücker minors $p_{ij}^{(1)}$ from $\~{Z}^{(1)}$. Because $U_{\text{cl}}$ and $\mathbf{A}_{\text{vort}}$ depend only on projective ratios of $p_{ij}$, evaluate the nonlinear potential block $\mathcal{V}(p_{ij}^{(1)})$ pointwise (using the smooth Fubini–Study denominator $\Vert{}P\Vert{}^2 = \sum \vert{}p_{ij}\vert{}^2$ instead of |p₁₂|² near nodes):

$$\~{Z}^{(2)}=\exp \left(-\frac{i\Delta t}{\hbar }\mathcal{V}(p_{ij}^{(1)})\right)\~{Z}^{(1)}$$

3.  **Second Linear Half-Step:**

$$\~{Z}^{(3)}=\exp \left(-\frac{i\Delta t}{2\hbar }\mathcal{H}_{0}(\mathbf{k},\nu )\right)\~{Z}^{(2)}$$

4.  **Grassmannian Retraction (QR Purification):**
    The gauge term -ZΛ(Z) is enforced *exactly* without computing Λ explicitly by performing a thin QR decomposition at every spatial grid point $\mathbf{x}$:

$$\~{Z}^{(3)}(\mathbf{x})=Q(\mathbf{x})R(\mathbf{x})\implies Z^{n+1}(\mathbf{x})=Q(\mathbf{x})$$

    Because $Q^\dagger Q = \mathbb{I}_2$, this retraction projects $\~{Z}^{(3)}$ orthogonally back onto the Stiefel manifold $\mathrm{St}(2,4)$, guaranteeing $P^T J P = 0$ to machine precision (10⁻¹⁶) across all Schubert cell transitions.

4\. Incompressible Navier–Stokes in the Plücker–Grassmannian Framework

Standard Madelung hydrodynamics is inherently compressible because ρ = |ψ|² varies dynamically. To enforce **incompressibility** ($\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0$ and ρ = ρ₀ = const), we impose two geometric constraints on $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)$:

Constraint 1: Constant Primal Determinant (Volume-Preserving Submanifold)

Setting ρ = ρ₀ fixes the modulus of the first Plücker minor globally:

$$|{}p_{12}(\mathbf{x},t)|{}=|{}z_{11}z_{22}-z_{12}z_{21}|{}=\rho _{0}$$

Geometrically, this restricts the fluid trajectory from $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)$ to an $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})$\-fibered submanifold where $p_{12} = \rho_0 e^{i\theta(\mathbf{x},t)}$ is a pure phase factor. All density variations are frozen out, leaving only rotational/vortical degrees of freedom.

Constraint 2: Solenoidal Plücker Flux ($\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0$)

In terms of Plücker minors, the velocity field on Ω₀ is $\mathbf{v} = \frac{\hbar}{m} \operatorname{Im}\left(\frac{\mathbf{p}_{\text{mom}}}{p_{12}}\right)$. Requiring $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0$ translates into a linear differential constraint on the momentum minors:

$$\mathrm{Im}\>\left[\nabla \cdot \left(e^{-i\theta }\mathbf{p}_{\text{mom}}\right)\right]=0$$

The Incompressible Plücker–Navier–Stokes System

With ρ = ρ₀ fixed, the classical pressure $P_{\text{cl}}$ ceases to be a thermodynamic function of ρ and instead becomes a **Lagrange multiplier** enforcing $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0$. In the 4 × 4 operator $\mathcal{H}_{\text{eff}}$:

1.  **Elimination of Bulk Viscosity & Quantum Pressure:** Since $\nabla \sqrt{\rho_0} = 0$, the quantum potential $Q = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\nabla^2 \sqrt{\rho_0}}{\sqrt{\rho_0}} \equiv 0$ vanishes identically in the bulk, and the viscous operator simplifies to pure Laplacian shear $\mathcal{L}_{\text{visc}} = \nu \nabla^2 \mathbb{I}_2$.
2.  **Hodge–Leray Projection on the Grassmannian:** In the numerical stepper (Step 2), the potential $U_{\text{cl}}$ is replaced by the hydrodynamic pressure $\Pi(\mathbf{x},t)$, determined at each step by solving the **Plücker–Poisson equation**:

$$\nabla ^{2}\Pi =-\rho _{0}\nabla \cdot \left[(\mathbf{v}\cdot \nabla )\mathbf{v}\right]=-\frac{\hbar ^{2}\rho _{0}}{m^{2}}\nabla \cdot \left[\mathrm{Im}\>\left(\frac{p_{34}}{p_{12}}\right)\mathbf{e}_{\text{conv}}\right]$$

    Equivalently, in spectral space, we apply the **Leray projector** $\mathbb{P}_{ab} = \delta_{ab} - \frac{k_a k_b}{k^2}$ directly to the momentum Plücker minors (p₁₃, p₂₄, p₁₄, p₂₃) before the QR purification step.
3.  **Incompressible Vortex Reconnection:** Even though ρ = ρ₀ is constant globally, **incompressible vortex reconnection** still triggers a Schubert cell transition! At a 3D incompressible vortex reconnection or null point, the individual Clebsch spinor components cross zero (z₁₁, z₂₁ → 0) in such a way that the phase $\theta(\mathbf{x},t)$ of $p_{12} = \rho_0 e^{i\theta}$ develops a 2π branch cut (vortex filament), while the derivative minors p₁₃, p₃₄ spike. Lifting to $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)$ resolves the phase branch cut as a smooth chart transition between Ω₀ and Ω₁₃ without infinite vorticity gradients.
