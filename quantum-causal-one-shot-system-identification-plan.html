1. Scientific Motivation & Architectural Mapping¶

Boullé, Halikias, Otto, and Townsend [Boullé et al., 2026, Boullé et al., 2026] prove a sharp zero-one law for linearly parameterized analytic systems \(\mathcal{L}(u, f) = \sum_{i=1}^N c_i^* \mathcal{D}_i(u, f) = \mathcal{D}_0(u, f)\): either every admissible input \(f \in \mathcal{F}\) fails to make the parameter-to-recovery map \(L_{f,\mathcal{G}(f)} : \mathbb{R}^N \to \mathcal{V}\) injective (\(\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{deg}}(\mathbf{c}^*) = \mathcal{F}\)), or \(\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{deg}}(\mathbf{c}^*)\) is compactly slice-null and has zero measure under every nondegenerate Gaussian measure on \(\mathcal{F}\). Their Weak Identification of Analytic Systems (WIAS) algorithm tests rank of the \(r_0 \times N\) matrix \(G_{ij} = \psi_i(\mathcal{D}_j(u, f))\) via column-pivoted QR, providing an a posteriori noiseless uniqueness certificate.

From our applied quantum physics and Grassmannian Quantum Causal Inference (QCI) perspective, WIAS leaves five physical and mathematical gaps that our causal mechanics resolves directly:

Boullé et al. (2026) Mechanism

Physical / Causal Gap in Quantum & Lab Systems

QCI-OSID Reimplementation in Signals

Determinant Test \(\det(\Psi L_{f,\mathcal{G}(f)} E) \neq 0\)

Treats \(\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{deg}}\) purely analytically; does not connect degeneracy to graph bottlenecks or exterior-rank collapse.

Plabic / LGV Grassmannian Determinant Dichotomy: \(G(f)\) defines an \(N\)-plane in \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{R}}(N, r_0)\); Cauchy–Binet \(\det(G^\top G) = \sum_I \Delta_I(G)^2 > 0\) certifies open-cell unconfounded identification, whereas factoring through \(m < N\) intermediate modes collapses \(\det G = 0\) (singleHubRepresentative_det = 0, Hankel \(n < 2n-1\) vs. Circulant \(n = n\)).

Observational \((f, u)\) Assumption

Assumes no unobserved backdoor confounder \(U\) drives both probe \(f\) and state \(u\); observational closed-loop trajectories bias \(\hat{\mathbf{c}}\) by \(b_{\mathrm{conf}}\).

Pearl \(\operatorname{do}(f := f_{\mathrm{probe}})\) Incoming-Edge Surgery + Giarmatzi–Costa Abduction: Severs \(U \to f\) to eliminate backdoor confounding bias (causalObservationalTransfer - causalInterventionalDoTransfer = b_conf), while abducing exogenous drift \(u_E = y_{\mathrm{obs}} - \mathbf{c}^* \cdot \mathbf{d}_{\mathrm{obs}}\) in one shot.

Multi-Shot Stacking for \(N > n\) (Hankel Matrices)

Classical \(n \times n\) Hankel (\(N = 2n - 1 > n\)) cannot be identified in 1 right probe (\(q_R = 1 \implies \mathrm{rank} \le n < 2n - 1\)), requiring \(q_R = 2\) sequential shots.

Coherent \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\) Quantum Switch & Pole-Free VRC Probe: A control qubit \(|+\rangle_c\) superposes forward and adjoint/commutator-anticommutator probe orderings (\(\{A_1, A_2\}\) vs. \([A_1, A_2]\)), extracting \(E_+\), \(E_-\), and Plücker minor ratios from one coherent quantum shot while avoiding rational-chart poles.

Dense Operator / Noiseless Idealization

Dense PDE dictionaries scale quadratically across spatiotemporal grids, and noisy soft observations violate noiseless point evaluation.

Tautological Bundle (Z, v) + Cross-Lag Plücker + Jeffrey Kinematics + QFIM = 4 g_FS: Separates invariant law subspace \(Z\) from trajectory fiber \(v = Z\alpha\), preserves multi-lag rank via cross-lag wedges (\(\mathcal{Q}_4 > 0\)), updates soft noisy partitions via deconfounded Jeffrey Kinematics, and bounds estimation variance via the Quantum Cramér–Rao Bound \(\mathrm{Var}(\hat{c}) \ge 1 / (M \cdot \mathrm{QFIM})\).

Qualitative “Irreversible Alteration” Motivation

Motivates 1-shot recovery informally by noting measurements alter systems irreversibly.

Closed-Loop Conditional Landauer Bound: Proves \(M\)-shot identification incurs \(\ge M \cdot k_B T \ln 2 \cdot N_{\mathrm{bits}}\) cyclic heat (closedLoopErasureAndMemoryResetHeat), establishing an \(M\times\) thermodynamic floor for 1-shot over \(M\)-shot protocols.

3. Part II: Integrating QCI-OSID with Tequila and Our signals / grassmann Backend (OSID-TQ)¶

3.1 Motivation¶

Sections OSID-1–OSID-6 established QCI-OSID in standalone Lean 4, Rust/PyO3 (quantum_causal_inference.rs), and SymPy/mpmath. However, practical quantum system identification in chemistry and many-body hardware requires executing probes, variational eigenstates, noise channels, and expectation-value batches directly inside an operator/circuit compiler stack. Integrating QCI-OSID with Tequila [Kottmann et al., 2021, Kottmann et al., 2021] and our native Rust/PyO3 signals / grassmann Tequila backend (sustainablefactory/tequila_signals_backend.py, src/signals/numerical_adapter/src/tequila_simulator.rs, and the Tequila Rust/PyO3 simulator paper) bridges symbolic quantum circuits (QCircuit, QubitHamiltonian, ExpectationValue, BraKet, tq.minimize) with QCI-OSID recovery.

3.2 Advantages vs. Disadvantages Trade-Off Analysis¶

Dimension

Advantages of Tequila + signals/grassmann Integration

Disadvantages, Costs, & Pitfalls

Circuit & Hamiltonian Compilation

BackendCircuitSignals compiles ExpPauli, controlled rotations, SWAP, and Toffoli natively into RustFastCircuit without CNOT decomposition ladders; RustFastHamiltonianSet evaluates all \(\{L_i, L_j\}\) and \([L_m, \mathcal{O}_j]\) batches in one Rayon-parallelized pass.

Adds Python/FFI + sympy lambdify marshaling overhead (~ms scale) on tiny \(n \le 6\) systems compared to raw Rust quantum_causal_inference.rs; requires editable workspace installs (-e ., -e ./src/tequila, -e ./src/signals/numerical_adapter) in requirements.txt.

Hilbert Space vs. Continuous PDEs

Unlocks 128-bit sparse Plücker HCB-SPA identification (16 * 2^n_occ bytes) up to \(n_{\mathrm{orb}} = 126\) qubits (Master Theorem 11.2), beating the \(2^n\) dense memory wall (qulacs, cirq, qiskit).

Tequila operates on discrete qubit Hilbert spaces, not continuous Banach/Sobolev spaces \(H^1(\Omega)\); classical PDEs/ODEs (Duffing, Allen–Cahn, Navier–Stokes) still belong in weak-form CPQR (WIAS), avoiding \(O(2^n)\) QRAM loading.

Shot Noise vs. Exact Quadrature

tq.simulate(..., backend="signals") supports both exact statevectors and realistic Born-rule shot sampling (samples=S) for hardware emulation.

Finite-shot sampling introduces \(O(1/\sqrt{S})\) matrix noise \(\Delta M\), amplified by \(1/\lambda_2(v)\) via Davis–Kahan (osidTequilaShotCountRequired: halving \(\lambda_2\) causes a \(4\times\) shot penalty), vs. \(10^{-11}\text{–}10^{-15}\) deterministic quadrature.

Noise & McWeeny Purification

do_simulate_density(mcweeny_passes=k) (3ρ² - 2ρ³) and MCWF/homodyne/heterodyne trajectories heal weakly depolarized pure eigenstates (\(p \in (1/2, 1)\)), shrinking eigenstate OSID error (Master Theorem 11.2).

Dense density matrices scale as \(O(4^n)\) (16 * 4^n bytes, limit \(n \lesssim 12\)); crucially, McWeeny purification distorts genuine mixed/Gibbs states (\(p \in (0, 1/2) \implies 3p^2 - 2p^3 < p\)), corrupting thermal-state OSID.

VQE, Quantum Switch, & Non-Unitary Gates

tq.minimize(..., backend="signals") + compute_natural_gradient (QFIM = 4 g_FS) enables VQE-eigenstate OSID + circuit-level \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\) Quantum Switch control-qubit readout \(\langle Z_c\rangle = E_+ - E_-\).

Non-unitary CausalDo and NonlinearKerr gates sit outside Tequila’s two-term unitary tq.grad parameter-shift rule; symmetry-unbroken states (\(\lambda_2 = 0\)) and overall scale/identity shifts (\(H \mapsto \alpha H + \beta I\)) remain unidentifiable from a single eigenstate.
