## Abstract

Since **Lord and Shulman (1967)** ([*J. Mech. Phys. Solids* **15**, 299–309](#gddftem-lordshulman1967)) introduced a single relaxation time $\tau_0$ into Biot's coupled thermoelasticity to eliminate the infinite propagation speed of classical parabolic Fourier heat conduction, generalized thermoelasticity—and its extensions by **Green and Lindsay (1972)** ([*J. Elasticity* **2**, 1–7](#gddftem-greenlindsay1972)) and **Green and Naghdi (1991–1993)** ([*J. Elasticity* **31**, 189–208](#gddftem-greennaghdi1993))—has served as the standard continuum model for coupled thermal, elastic, and electromagnetic transients. Yet when applied to modern high-field, relativistic, nanoscale, or quantum materials, Lord–Shulman magneto-thermoelasticity suffers from **six fundamental physical breakdowns**:
1. **Non-Relativistic Magneto-Elastic Superluminal Blow-Up**: Adding the classical Alfvén magnetic stress $v_A^2 = B_0^2 / (\mu_0\rho_0)$ linearly to the elastic wave speed $c_E^2 = (\lambda + 2\mu)/\rho_0$ yields $v_{\mathrm{ME,NR}}^2 = c_E^2 + v_A^2$, which exceeds $c^2$ in strong magnetic fields ($c_E^2 + v_A^2 > c^2$).
2. **Conflation of Causal Signal-Front Speeds with Superluminal Phase / Sweep Velocities in Dielectrics and Metamaterials**: Requiring *all* wave-speed roots to lie in $(0, c^2]$ on the Positive Grassmannian $\mathrm{Gr}_{>0}(2,4)$ is necessary for **causal signal-front and group speeds ($v_{\mathrm{front}}, v_g \le c$)**, but is **not** necessary—and would be overly restrictive—on the full **Complex Grassmannian $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4) \setminus \mathrm{Gr}_{\ge 0}(2,4)$**, which natively hosts **superluminal polarization sweeps inside dielectrics ($v_{\mathrm{sweep}} > c_{\mathrm{med}} = c/\sqrt{\varepsilon_r\mu_r}$ or $v_{\mathrm{sweep}} > c$)**, **ENZ/plasma superluminal phase velocities ($v_{\mathrm{ph}} > c$ with $v_{\mathrm{ph}} v_g = c^2$)**, **$\mathrm{ER}=\mathrm{EPR}$ spacelike throat correlations**, and **off-shell evanescent skin-depth / Meissner modes ($\operatorname{Im}u \neq 0$)**.
3. **Unbounded Heat-Flux Overshoot**: Linear Cattaneo–Vernotte relaxation $\tau_0 \partial_t \mathbf{q} + \mathbf{q} = -k_{\mathrm{th}}\nabla T$ does not enforce the ballistic phonon ceiling $|q| < q_{\max} = \rho_0 c_v c_{\mathrm{th}} T_0$, allowing negative absolute temperature overshoots under intense ultrafast laser or quench pulses.
4. **Missing Onsager Thermoelectric & Thermomagnetic Reciprocity**: Standard Lord–Shulman omits the coupled Seebeck ($S$), Peltier ($\Pi = S T_0$), Nernst–Ettingshausen ($N_{\mathrm{NE}}$), and Joule–Thomson ($\mu_{\mathrm{JT}}$) cross-couplings required for solid-state energy harvesting and cryogenic thermal management.
5. **Ideal-Contact Continuum Boundary Assumption ($K_{\mathrm{bond}} \to \infty$)**: Lord–Shulman assumes welded zero-resistance boundaries or empirical Biot numbers, missing the ab initio work-of-adhesion bond compliance $\xi_{\mathrm{bond}}$, $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,5)$ three-phonon anharmonic up-conversion above $400\text{ K}$, and $1.48\times$ carbide phonon bridging proved in **Grassmannian Thermal Boundary Conductance (`G-TBC`, Paper 25)**.
6. **Inability to Model Superconducting & Topological Thermo-Electro-Magnetics**: Lord–Shulman assumes dissipative Ohm's law $\mathbf{J} = \sigma_e \mathbf{E}$ and constant elastic moduli, failing completely in **Cuprate Superconductors (`G-CSC`, Paper 26)**—where $T^* = 245\text{ K}$ pseudogap nematicity softens the ultrasound modulus and London supercurrents screen magneto-elastic waves—and in **Rhombohedral & Moiré Multilayer Graphene (`G-MGS`, Paper 28)** on $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,6)$ and $\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,10)$, where $C = \pm 5$ QAHE and $C = 1$ Anomalous Hall Crystals route transverse currents with **identically zero Joule heating** ($\sigma_{xx} = 0 \implies \mathbf{J}\cdot\mathbf{E} = 0$) and supersolid Leggett fractions $f_s = 1 - \eta_{\mathrm{mod}}^2$ govern bimodal second sound.
7. **Single-Temperature Equilibrium Lattice Assumption Under Ultrafast Laser Excitation**: Classical Lord–Shulman treats $\tau_0$, $\varepsilon_T$, and $\beta_M^2$ as static equilibrium constants and assumes a single local lattice temperature $T(\mathbf{r}, t)$, whereas femtosecond-to-picosecond light–matter drives require the **Time-Linear Grassmannian Generalized Kadanoff–Baym Ansatz (`G-GKBA`, Paper 24 / Problem 04)** ($1.44\times 10^8\times$ faster than two-time KBE) to resolve five-reservoir optical-to-acoustic (`SCOP` $\to$ `LA`+`TA`) second-sound emergence in Graphite, $150\text{ kV/cm}$ THz condensate depletion in $\mathrm{MgB}_2$, Mott–Hubbard suppression of $\varepsilon_T(U/J)$, and exciton-Mott magneto-elastic activation in $\mathrm{WSe}_2$.

In this paper, we formulate, machine-check in **Lean 4** (`Signals.DFT` Sections 24, 24.1, & 24.2, `Signals.Pending`), and implement in **Rust/WASM** (`thermal_inelastic.rs`) the **Grassmannian–DDF Thermo-Electro-Magnetic (`G-DDF-TEM`)** theory as a complete alternative to Lord–Shulman, validated against **five open experimental and analytical benchmark datasets** ($0.15\%$ overall MAPE across 20 check figures).

1. **Relativistic Magneto-Thermo-Piezoelectric Momentum Balance**:

   $$
   \frac{h_{\mathrm{tot}}}{c^2}\,\frac{\partial^2 u_i}{\partial t^2}
   = C_{ijkl}\,\frac{\partial^2 u_k}{\partial x_j \partial x_l}
   - \gamma_{ij}^{\mathrm{TE}}\,\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial x_j}
   - e_{kij}\,\frac{\partial E_k}{\partial x_j}
   + (\mathbf{J} \times \mathbf{B}_0)_i,
   \qquad \theta = T - T_0,
   $$

   where $h_{\mathrm{tot}} = \rho_0(c^2 + c_E^2 + v_A^2)$ is the relativistic magneto-elastic enthalpy density and $v_A^2 = B_0^2 / (\mu_0\rho_0)$ is the squared Alfvén speed.

   $$

### 1.2 Dimensionless Coupled Magneto-Thermoelastic Characteristic Dispersion (`lordShulmanCharacteristicPoly`, `lordShulmanDiscriminant`)

In dimensionless variables normalized by the elastic wave speed $c_E = \sqrt{(\lambda+2\mu)/\rho_0}$ and characteristic frequency $\omega^* = c_E^2 / \alpha_0$, let:
- $\tau_0 = \frac{c_E^2}{\bigl(c_{\mathrm{th}}^{\mathrm{DDF}}(s)\bigr)^2} = \frac{\tau_r\,c_E^2}{\alpha_\parallel^{\mathrm{DDF}}(s)} > 0$ be the dimensionless thermal relaxation ratio,
- $\beta_M^2 = \frac{v_A^2}{c_E^2} = \frac{B_0^2}{\mu_0 \rho_0 c_E^2} \ge 0$ be the dimensionless Alfvén magneto-elastic coupling factor (generalized to finite electrical conductivity via $\beta_{M,\mathrm{eff}}^2 = \frac{\beta_M^2(\omega\tau_{\mathrm{mag}})^2}{1 + (\omega\tau_{\mathrm{mag}})^2}$ in `finiteConductivityMagnetoElasticFactor`), and

Eliminating the displacement $u_x$ and temperature perturbation $\theta$ yields the fourth-order hyperbolic operator whose high-frequency characteristic wave speeds $v$ ($u = (v / c_E)^2$) satisfy the **coupled quadratic characteristic equation** (`lordShulmanCharacteristicPoly` in `Signals.DFT` Section 24):

$$
\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{LS}}(\tau_0, \beta_M^2, \varepsilon_T, u)
= \tau_0\,u^2 - \bigl(1 + \beta_M^2 + \tau_0(1 + \varepsilon_T)\bigr)\,u + (1 + \beta_M^2) = 0,
$$

with sum and product of squared wave speeds (`lordShulmanWaveSpeedSum`, `lordShulmanWaveSpeedProd`):

$$
u_+ + u_- = \frac{1 + \beta_M^2}{\tau_0} + 1 + \varepsilon_T, \qquad
u_+ \cdot u_- = \frac{1 + \beta_M^2}{\tau_0},
$$

and coupled characteristic discriminant (`lordShulmanDiscriminant`):

$$
\Delta_{\mathrm{LS}}(\tau_0, \beta_M^2, \varepsilon_T)
= \bigl(1 + \beta_M^2 + \tau_0(1 + \varepsilon_T)\bigr)^2 - 4\,\tau_0\,(1 + \beta_M^2)
= \bigl(1 + \beta_M^2 - \tau_0(1 + \varepsilon_T)\bigr)^2 + 4\,\tau_0\,\varepsilon_T\,(1 + \beta_M^2) > 0.
$$
