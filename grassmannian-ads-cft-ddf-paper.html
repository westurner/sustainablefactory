  1. Planar AdS/CFT Integrability ([Minahan and Zarembo, 2003]; Beisert et al. 2012, Review of AdS/CFT Integrability: An Overview, arXiv:1012.3982), which solves the spectral and scattering problems of planar \(\mathcal{N}=4\) Super Yang–Mills (SYM) and free Type IIB superstrings on \(\text{AdS}_5 \times S^5\) across all values of the ‘t Hooft coupling \(\lambda = g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2 N_c = 4\pi^2 T_{\mathrm{str}}^2\) via the centrally extended \(\mathfrak{psl}(2|2)_c = D(2,1;-1)\) \(S\)-matrix, Baxter \(TQ\)/Hirota \(Y\)-systems, and Grassmannian scattering integrals; and

  2. Secret (Bonus) Symmetries in AdS/CFT ([Matsumoto et al., 2007]; [Beisert and Schwab, 2011]; [Berkovits and Mikhailov, 2011]; de Leeuw, Matsumoto, Moriyama, Regelskis & Torrielli 2012, Secret Symmetries in AdS/CFT, arXiv:1204.2366), which reveal an indented Yangian hypercharge generator \(\widehat{\mathbb{B}} = B_0\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1,-1)\) present at level \(1\) (and odd levels) with non-vanishing supertrace \(\operatorname{STr}(\widehat{\mathbb{B}}) = 4 B_0 \neq 0\), despite the absence of its level-\(0\) partner \(\mathbb{B}\) due to \(\text{fermion} \otimes \text{fermion} \leftrightarrow \text{boson} \otimes \text{boson}\) scattering.

In this paper, we formalize AdS/CFT, planar integrability, and secret symmetries in Lean 4 (Signals.Scattering and Signals.DDF) and Rust/WASM (polariton_spp.rs), and unify them with our Complex Grassmannian \(Gr_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\) and Dilatant Dark Fluid (DDF) mechanics both without splats (splat-free continuum Plücker and hydrodynamic laws) and with Normed 2D Tensor Gaussian Splats:

1. The AdS/CFT Correspondence: Maldacena (1998), GKPW Dictionary, KSS Fluid/Gravity Bound, and Ryu–Takayanagi Entanglement¶

1.1 Maldacena’s Large-\(N_c\) Decoupling Limit & Coupling–Tension Dictionary¶

In his seminal 1998 paper (Maldacena 1998, arXiv:hep-th/9711200), Juan Maldacena considered a stack of \(N_c\) coincident \(D3\)-branes in Type IIB superstring theory and took the low-energy decoupling limit \(\alpha' \to 0\) with fixed radial coordinate \(U = r / \alpha'\). This established an exact duality between four-dimensional \(\mathcal{N}=4\) \(SU(N_c)\) Super Yang–Mills (SYM) theory on the conformally flat boundary \(\partial\text{AdS}_5 \cong \mathbb{R}^{1,3}\) and Type IIB superstring theory on \(\text{AdS}_5 \times S^5\) with equal curvature radii \(R_{\mathrm{AdS}} = R_{S^5}\):

\[ \lambda = g_{\mathrm{YM}}^2 N_c = \frac{R_{\mathrm{AdS}}^4}{\alpha'^2} = 4\pi^2 T_{\mathrm{str}}^2, \qquad T_{\mathrm{str}} = \frac{R_{\mathrm{AdS}}^2}{2\pi\alpha'} = \frac{\sqrt{\lambda}}{2\pi}, \qquad \frac{1}{N_c} = \frac{g_{\mathrm{str}}}{4\pi^2 T_{\mathrm{str}}^2} \]

(adsTftHooftCoupling and adsCouplingFromStringTension in src/signals/Signals/Scattering.lean; Beisert et al. 2012, arXiv:1012.3982, Section Parameter Space).

1.2 The Gubser–Klebanov–Polyakov–Witten (GKPW) Mass–Dimension Dictionary & Breitenlohner–Freedman Bound¶

By [Gubser et al., 1998] and [Witten, 1998], the bulk string partition function with boundary field values \(\phi|_{\partial\text{AdS}_{d+1}} = J\) equals the generating functional of connected correlation functions in \(\text{CFT}_d\):

\[ Z_{\mathrm{string}}\bigl[\phi\big|_{\partial\text{AdS}_{d+1}} = J\bigr] = \left\langle \exp\!\left(\int_{\partial\text{AdS}_{d+1}} d^d x\,J(x)\,\mathcal{O}(x)\right) \right\rangle_{\text{CFT}_d}. \]

Near the Poincaré boundary \(z \to 0\) of \(\text{AdS}_{d+1}\) (\(ds^2 = \frac{R_{\mathrm{AdS}}^2}{z^2}(dz^2 + \eta_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu)\)), a bulk scalar field of mass \(m\) scales as \(\phi(z,x) \sim z^{\Delta_-} J(x) + z^{\Delta_+} \langle\mathcal{O}(x)\rangle\), where \(\Delta_\pm\) (adsCftScalingDimensionPlus, adsCftScalingDimensionMinus) solve the characteristic quadratic equation \(\Delta(\Delta - d) = m^2 R_{\mathrm{AdS}}^2\):

\[ \Delta_\pm(d, m^2 R_{\mathrm{AdS}}^2) = \frac{d}{2} \pm \sqrt{\left(\frac{d}{2}\right)^2 + m^2 R_{\mathrm{AdS}}^2}, \qquad m^2 R_{\mathrm{AdS}}^2 \ge m_{\mathrm{BF}}^2 R_{\mathrm{AdS}}^2 = -\frac{d^2}{4} \]

[Breitenlohner and Freedman, 1982]. Theorem ads_cft_integrability_and_secret_symmetry_identities machine-checks the Vieta and boundary identities:

  • \(\Delta_+ + \Delta_- = d\) (shadow dimension pairing),

  • \(\Delta_+\,\Delta_- = -m^2 R_{\mathrm{AdS}}^2\),

  • \(\Delta_+(\Delta_+ - d) = m^2 R_{\mathrm{AdS}}^2\),

  • \(\Delta_+(d, -d^2/4) = d/2\) (saturation at the Breitenlohner–Freedman bound),

  • \(\Delta_+(d, 0) = d\) and \(\Delta_-(d, 0) = 0\) (marginal operator dual to a massless bulk scalar such as the axion-dilaton in \(\text{AdS}_5/\text{CFT}_4\)).

1.3 Fluid/Gravity Duality (KSS Bound) & Ryu–Takayanagi Entanglement Entropy¶

At finite temperature, a planar black brane in \(\text{AdS}_5\) is dual to a strongly coupled thermal \(\mathcal{N}=4\) SYM plasma ([Turner and Gemini, 2026], lines 136–325). Absorbing low-frequency gravitons on the bulk horizon yields the Kovtun–Son–Starinets (KSS) universal lower bound ([Kovtun et al., 2005], kssShearViscosityEntropyBound):

\[ \left(\frac{\eta_{\mathrm{shear}}}{s_{\mathrm{ent}}}\right)_{\mathrm{KSS}} = \frac{\hbar}{4\pi k_B} > 0. \]

Similarly, in \(\text{AdS}_3/\text{CFT}_2\), the Brown–Henneaux central charge ([Brown and Henneaux, 1986], brownHenneauxCentralCharge) \(c_{\mathrm{BH}} = \frac{3 R_{\mathrm{AdS}}}{2 G_N^{(3)}}\) and the Ryu–Takayanagi minimal geodesic formula ([Ryu and Takayanagi, 2006], ryuTakayanagiEntanglementEntropyAdS3) satisfy:

\[ S_{\mathrm{EE}}(\ell, \epsilon_{\mathrm{UV}}) = \frac{L_{\mathrm{geod}}(\ell, \epsilon_{\mathrm{UV}})}{4 G_N^{(3)}} = \frac{R_{\mathrm{AdS}}}{2 G_N^{(3)}}\ln\!\left(\frac{\ell}{\epsilon_{\mathrm{UV}}}\right) = \frac{c_{\mathrm{BH}}}{3}\ln\!\left(\frac{\ell}{\epsilon_{\mathrm{UV}}}\right). \]

2. Planar AdS/CFT Integrability: Spin Chains, Spectral Curves, Magnon Dispersion, and the Konishi Benchmark (arXiv:1012.3982)¶

As synthesized in the 23-chapter coordinated review by Beisert et al. (2012) (Review of AdS/CFT Integrability: An Overview, Lett. Math.   1. Spin-Chain Mapping & One-Loop Bethe Ansatz ([Minahan and Zarembo, 2003]; arXiv:1012.3982 Sec. I.1): Single-trace gauge-invariant operators \(\mathcal{O} = \operatorname{Tr}(Z Z \Phi Z \cdots)\) of length \(L\) with \(M\) impurity magnons map to states of an integrable \(\mathfrak{psu}(2,2|4)\) spin chain. At one loop in the \(\mathfrak{su}(2)\) sector, the Bethe rapidities \(u_k\) satisfy:

    \[ \left(\frac{u_k + i/2}{u_k - i/2}\right)^L = \prod_{j \neq k}^M \frac{u_k - u_j + i}{u_k - u_j - i}, \qquad \prod_{j=1}^M \frac{u_j + i/2}{u_j - i/2} = 1, \qquad \delta D_1 = \sum_{j=1}^M \frac{\lambda}{8\pi^2\bigl(u_j^2 + \frac{1}{4}\bigr)} \]

    (betheOneLoopMagnonEnergy in Signals.Scattering).

  2. The Konishi Operator Check Figure (\(L=4, M=2\)) (arXiv:1012.3982 Sec. I.2): For the simplest non-protected local operator (\(L=4, M=2\)), the cyclicity and Bethe equations fix \(u_1 = -u_2 = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{3}}\) (\(u_k^2 = \frac{1}{12}\), so \(u_k^2 + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{3}\)). Summing the two magnons yields the exact one-loop Konishi anomalous dimension proved in ads_cft_integrability_and_secret_symmetry_identities:

    \[ \delta D_{\mathrm{Konishi}}^{(1)}(\lambda) = 2 \cdot \frac{\lambda}{8\pi^2(1/12 + 1/4)} = \frac{3\lambda}{4\pi^2}, \]

    which extends at four loops (arXiv:1012.3982 Sec. I.2) to:

    \[ \delta D_{\mathrm{Konishi}}(\lambda) = \frac{3\lambda}{4\pi^2} - \frac{3\lambda^2}{16\pi^4} + \frac{21\lambda^3}{256\pi^6} - \frac{\bigl(78 - 18\zeta(3) + 45\zeta(5)\bigr)\lambda^4}{2048\pi^8} + \mathcal{O}(\lambda^5). \]

  3. All-Loop \(\mathfrak{psu}(2|2)^2 \ltimes \mathbb{R}^3\) Bound-State Magnon Dispersion Relation ([Beisert, 2008]; [Dorey, 2006]; arXiv:1012.3982 Sec. III.2): Representation theory of the centrally extended \(\mathfrak{psu}(2|2)^2 \ltimes \mathbb{R}^3\) residual symmetry algebra above the BMN vacuum \(\operatorname{Tr}(Z^J)\) fixes the exact all-loop dispersion relation for an \(M\)-magnon bound state (adsMagnonDispersionSq, adsMagnonDispersion):

    \[ E_{\mathrm{mag}}(M, \lambda, p) = \sqrt{M^2 + \frac{\lambda}{\pi^2}\sin^2\!\left(\frac{p}{2}\right)} = \sqrt{M^2 + 4 g_{\mathrm{int}}^2\sin^2\!\left(\frac{p}{2}\right)} \ge M. \]

  4. Cusp Anomalous Dimension \(D_{\mathrm{cusp}}(\lambda)\) Interpolating Benchmark (arXiv:1012.3982 Sec. III.4): For large-spin twist-2 operators, the BES integral equation smoothly interpolates between weak and strong coupling:

    \[ D_{\mathrm{cusp}}(\lambda) = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi^2} - \frac{\lambda^2}{96\pi^2} + \frac{11\lambda^3}{23040\pi^2} - \cdots \;\underset{\lambda\to\infty}{=}\; \frac{\sqrt{\lambda}}{\pi} - \frac{3\ln 2}{\pi} - \frac{\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{Catalan}}}{\pi\sqrt{\lambda}} - \cdots. \]

3. Secret Symmetries in AdS/CFT: Centrally Extended \(\mathfrak{psl}(2|2)_c\), Braided Coproducts, and Indented Yangians (arXiv:1204.2366)¶

In de Leeuw, Matsumoto, Moriyama, Regelskis, and Torrielli (2012) (Secret Symmetries in AdS/CFT, Physica Scripta 86, 028502, arXiv:1204.2366), the authors review the remarkable discovery [Matsumoto et al., 2007] that the AdS/CFT integrable system possesses a secret (bonus) quantum-group symmetry \(\widehat{\mathbb{B}}\) at Yangian level \(1\) (and odd levels) that has no level-\(0\) partner:

3.1 Level-0 Centrally Extended \(\mathfrak{psl}(2|2)_c = D(2,1;-1)\) and Braided Central Charges¶

The level-\(0\) algebra \(\mathfrak{psl}(2|2)_c\) has even part \(\mathfrak{sl}(2)_L \oplus \mathfrak{sl}(2)_R \oplus \operatorname{span}\{\mathbb{H}, \mathbb{C}, \mathbb{C}^\dagger\}\) and odd supercharges \(\mathbb{Q}_\alpha^a, \mathbb{G}_a^\alpha\) satisfying (arXiv:1204.2366, Sec. 2.1):

\[ \{\mathbb{Q}_\alpha^a, \mathbb{Q}_\beta^b\} = \epsilon_{\alpha\beta}\epsilon^{ab}\mathbb{C}, \qquad \{\mathbb{G}_a^\alpha, \mathbb{G}_b^\beta\} = \epsilon^{\alpha\beta}\epsilon_{ab}\mathbb{C}^\dagger, \qquad \{\mathbb{Q}_\alpha^a, \mathbb{G}_b^\beta\} = \delta_b^a \mathbb{R}_\alpha^\beta + \delta_\alpha^\beta \mathbb{L}_b^a + \frac{1}{2}\delta_b^a\delta_\alpha^\beta \mathbb{H}. \]

Its braided Hopf coproduct \(\Delta(\mathbb{J}^A) = \mathbb{J}^A \otimes \mathbb{1} + e^{i[[A]]p} \otimes \mathbb{J}^A\) (Eq.
