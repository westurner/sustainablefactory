Abstract¶

Across nanophotonics, plasma electrodynamics, terrestrial geophysics, and laser-driven fusion, Epsilon-Near-Zero (ENZ) (\(\operatorname{Re}(\varepsilon) \to 0\)) and Hyperbolic Metamaterials (HMMs) (\(\operatorname{Re}(\varepsilon_\parallel)\cdot\operatorname{Re}(\varepsilon_\perp) < 0\)) provide the physical mechanism that converts transverse electromagnetic waves into intense, sub-diffraction longitudinal electric fields (\(E_z\)) (Podolskiy & Narimanov 2005; Poddubny et al. 2013; Liberal & Engheta 2017). In this paper, we synthesize and formally verify in Lean 4 (Signals.DDF, with LeanArchitect @[blueprint] annotations) and Rust/WASM (polariton_spp.rs) our corpus-wide research on ENZ and hyperbolic metamaterials across data/chats/IQ-Sampling-for-Signal-Phase.md, data/chats/Argon-Plasma-Wave-Energy.md, data/chats/Longitudinally-polarized-Continuous-Wave-Laser-emissions-from-Sunlight.md, and data/chats/_Quantum Processor and Soliton Discussions .md:

  1. Effective Medium Theory (EMT) & The Three Operating Regimes of rGO-polyCBD-SS-Lignin (rgo_polycbd_enz_hyperbolic_and_plasma_metamaterial_identities): For a sub-wavelength nanolaminate combining 2D reduced Graphene Oxide nanosheets (\(\varepsilon_m = -|\varepsilon_m| < 0\)) with low-loss hemp-derived polyCBD-SS-Lignin (\(\varepsilon_d > 0\), Xu et al. 2026; Davis et al. 2026) at volume fill fraction \(f\), we prove:

    • Exact partition of unity \(f_{\mathrm{ENZ}} + f_{\perp,\mathrm{pole}} = 1\) with \(0 < f_{\mathrm{ENZ}} = \frac{\varepsilon_d}{\varepsilon_d + |\varepsilon_m|} < \frac{1}{2} < f_{\perp,\mathrm{pole}} = \frac{|\varepsilon_m|}{\varepsilon_d + |\varepsilon_m|} < 1\) (for \(\varepsilon_d < |\varepsilon_m|\)),

    • Regime 1 (ENZ Horizon at \(f = f_{\mathrm{ENZ}}\)): \(\varepsilon_\parallel(f_{\mathrm{ENZ}}) = 0\) and \(\varepsilon_\perp(f_{\mathrm{ENZ}}) = \frac{|\varepsilon_m|\varepsilon_d(\varepsilon_d + |\varepsilon_m|)}{|\varepsilon_m|^2 - \varepsilon_d^2} > 0\), driving \(50\times\text{–}400\times\) longitudinal \(E_z\) boundary amplification,

    • Regime 2 (Type-II Hyperbolic Lens for \(f_{\mathrm{ENZ}} < f < f_{\perp,\mathrm{pole}}\)): \(\varepsilon_\parallel(f) < 0\) and \(\varepsilon_\perp(f) > 0\) (\(\varepsilon_\parallel\varepsilon_\perp < 0\)), supporting unbounded high-\(k\) axial wavevectors \(k_z^2(k_x) = \varepsilon_\perp(\omega_c^2 + k_x^2/|\varepsilon_\parallel|) > \varepsilon_\perp\omega_c^2\) and exact transverse momentum cancellation \(k_{x,1} + (-k_{x,1}) = 0\) for non-collinear \(400 \to 800\text{ GHz}\) Sum-Frequency Generation (SFG),

    • Regime 3 (Disulfide \(90^\circ\) Flake-Rotation Voxel Switch above \(T_v = 115^\circ\text{C}\)): Rotating rGO flakes \(90^\circ\) swaps \((\varepsilon_\parallel, \varepsilon_\perp) \mapsto (\varepsilon_\perp, \varepsilon_\parallel)\), converting a Type-II reflector (\(\varepsilon_\parallel < 0, \varepsilon_\perp > 0\)) into a Type-I sub-wavelength transmission needle (\(\varepsilon_\parallel^{(90^\circ)} > 0, \varepsilon_\perp^{(90^\circ)} < 0\)),

    • \(Gr_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\) Plücker Permittivity Duality: Embedding \(\chi_{\mathrm{HMM}} = \frac{|\varepsilon_m| - \varepsilon_d}{|\varepsilon_m| + \varepsilon_d}\) into ddfThermalGr24Matrix recovers \(\frac{|w_A^{\mathbb{C}}|^2}{|w_B^{\mathbb{C}}|^2} = \frac{|\varepsilon_m|}{\varepsilon_d}\).

  2. Magnetized Argon & Planetary Ionospheric Trivelpiece–Gould Hyperbolicity & Denisov Mode Conversion: We prove that an unmagnetized plasma hits its isotropic ENZ cutoff \(\varepsilon_\parallel(\omega_{pe}, \omega_{pe}) = 0\) at \(\omega = \omega_{pe}\), while a magnetized plasma in the Trivelpiece–Gould / ionospheric whistler band (\(0 < \omega < \min(\omega_{pe}, \omega_{ce})\)) satisfies \(\varepsilon_\parallel(\omega) < 0\) and \(\varepsilon_\perp(\omega) > 1 > 0\) (\(\varepsilon_\parallel\varepsilon_\perp < 0\), a natural Type-I hyperbolic metamaterial), and that Denisov linear mode conversion (\(\tau_D = (k_0 L_n)^{1/3}\sin\theta_{\mathrm{in}}\)) vanishes at normal incidence (\(\theta_{\mathrm{in}} = 0\)), requiring oblique incidence (\(\theta_{\mathrm{opt}} \approx 21.74^\circ\)).

  3. Argon Ion-Acoustic Speeds, CPA-TNSA ENZ-polyCBD \(p\text{-}^{11}\text{B}\) Fusion Gain (\(Q_E = 2.584\)), \(s\text{CO}_2\) Brayton Recovery (\(Q_E = 2.7892\)), & 10 Hz Supercapacitor Bus (argon_ion_acoustic_tnsa_enz_polycbd_and_sco2_ledger_identities): We prove the ion-acoustic speed hierarchy \(v_{s,\mathrm{Ar}}^2 < v_{s,\mathrm{He}}^2 < v_{s,\mathrm{H}}^2\) alongside mass-invariance of \(\gamma k_B T_e = M_i v_{s,i}^2\), optimal ENZ skin-depth thickness \(d_{\mathrm{opt}} = \frac{\lambda_{\mathrm{laser}}}{4\pi\sqrt{\varepsilon_i}} > 0\), the progression of engineering gain from flat-foil deficit (\(Q_E = 0.51 < 1\)) to micro-cone (\(Q_E = 2.295\)) to ENZ + polyCBD ribbon (\(Q_E = 2.584\)) to \(182.4\text{ kg/s}\) \(s\text{CO}_2\) Brayton waste-heat recovery (\(Q_E = 2.7892\)), and the exact 10 Hz DC bus capacitance \(C = \frac{14212}{1100} \approx 12.92\text{ F}\).
