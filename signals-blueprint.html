Signals Lean Blueprint¶

The generated blueprint covers the Signals Lean package and links formal nodes to their Lean declarations.

The selected roadmap nodes cover finite routing and weighted measurements, ordered minors and geometric representatives, quantum inverse scattering and Grassmannian Bethe ansatz nodes (Signals.QISM), scalar propagation and amplitude controls, measure-derived quadrature, and affine forms with oriented residues. Readiness markers mean that formal declarations are checked, not that physical models or experiments are validated.

Annotation Workflow¶

Keep a declaration docstring for the Lean API and supply explicit statement, title, and, for proof milestones, proof metadata in @[blueprint]. LeanArchitect does not copy ordinary declaration docstrings into blueprint statements. Use stable labels; let it infer statement and proof dependencies from the Lean terms unless a specific additional dependency is needed.

Include each annotated module using \inputleanmodule in the existing blueprint chapter. Extraction alone does not add its nodes to the rendered document. From the Signals package directory, lake build :blueprint extracts LaTeX, lake build :blueprintJson exports metadata, and make lean-api generates doc-gen API pages and the declaration finder in .lake/build/doc. make blueprint builds that API reference, extracts the nodes, and renders the standalone HTML blueprint with a relative signals-api/ link to the API tree. The documentation build copies both into its embedded blueprint output, so declaration links work under the same URL prefix when served locally or published.

JSON preserves raw annotation configuration. Check inferred dependency links and leanok readiness markers in the generated LaTeX and rendered HTML. Importing Architect also enables tactic docstrings: align declaration docstrings with their declarations so they are not consumed by a preceding tactic proof.

1. Neon Glow-Discharge Lamp & 2. Cathode Ray Tube (CRT) Benchmarks¶

In Twistors-and-Positive-Grassmannian-Interference.md, lines 2167-2245, the neon lamp and CRT serve as classical baselines:

  • Neon Glow Discharge: Free electrons accelerated in a low-pressure gas undergo stochastic inelastic collisions, emitting uncollimated photons with zero net momentum transfer (\(\sum \mathbf p_\gamma = \mathbf 0\)). Even if a \(100\text{ W}\) source were perfectly collimated into a unidirectional photon rocket (\(I_{sp} = c/g_0 \approx 3.05\times 10^7\text{ s}\)), its radiation thrust would be \(F = P/c \approx 0.33\,\mu\text{N}\).

  • Cathode Ray Tube (CRT): Operating in hard vacuum at \(V = 20\text{ kV}\) and \(I = 1\text{ mA}\) (lines 2238-2244), ballistic electrons reach \(v_e \approx 8.4\times 10^7\text{ m/s}\) with mass flow \(\dot m = I m_e / e \approx 5.69\times 10^{-15}\text{ kg/s}\) and thrust \(F = \dot m v_e \approx 0.47\,\mu\text{N}\). Magnetic deflection coils steer the beam via \(\mathbf F = q(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)\) (def:signals-magnetic-lorentz-force), which performs zero mechanical work (thm:signals-magnetic-lorentz-work-free). Without a neutralizing ion current, expelling electrons charges the vehicle positively and stalls the beam.

3. Argon MHD Wakefield Electricity Plant & Pulsed Monolithic Thruster¶

A. Resonant Langmuir Cavity & Inverse Landau Amplification (Sections 6–7)¶

The plant traps longitudinal electrostatic plasma waves inside a Fabry-Pérot cavity (\(L = n\lambda/2\), lines 2046-2065) governed by the warm Bohm-Gross dispersion relation (lines 3590-3637, Twistors-and-Positive-Grassmannian-Interference.md.lean, lines 539-626):

\[ \omega^2(k) = \omega_p^2 + 3 k^2 v_{\mathrm{th}}^2, \qquad v_\phi = \frac{\omega}{k}, \qquad v_g = \frac{\partial\omega}{\partial k} = \frac{3 k v_{\mathrm{th}}^2}{\omega}. \]

  • Blueprint nodes: def:signals-bohm-gross-omega-sq, def:signals-langmuir-phase-velocity, def:signals-bohm-gross-group-velocity, thm:signals-bohm-gross-velocity-product (\(v_\phi v_g = 3 v_{\mathrm{th}}^2\)), and thm:signals-bohm-gross-phase-velocity-bound (\(3 v_{\mathrm{th}}^2 \le v_\phi^2\)).

  • Bump-on-tail Inverse Landau pump: With the signed-wavenumber Plemelj residue convention \(2 k |k| n_0\) (def:signals-landau-growth-rate, lem:signals-landau-growth-rate-pos-k, lem:signals-landau-growth-rate-neg-k), injecting a fast forward electron beam (\(k > 0\)) with positive velocity-distribution slope \(\partial_v f_0|_{v=\omega/k} > 0\) (lines 2067-2081, lines 3640-3680) yields linear growth rate

\[ \gamma = \frac{\pi \omega_p^3}{2 k |k| n_0}\left.\frac{\partial f_0}{\partial v}\right|_{v=\omega/k} > 0 \quad (\text{\texttt{thm:signals-inverse-landau-growth-pos}}), \]

whereas a thermal Maxwellian damps both forward waves (\(k > 0\), \(\partial_v f_0 < 0\), thm:signals-thermal-landau-damping-neg) and backward waves (\(k < 0\), \(\partial_v f_0 > 0\) at \(v_\phi = \omega/k < 0\), thm:signals-backward-thermal-landau-damping), and quasilinear plateau formation (\(\partial_v f_0 = 0\)) saturates growth at \(\gamma = 0\) (lem:signals-quasilinear-plateau-saturation). Resonant wave-particle power exchange is conservative: \(\frac{dU_{\mathrm{wave}}}{dt} + \frac{dK_{\mathrm{beam}}}{dt} = 2\gamma U_{\mathrm{wave}} - 2\gamma U_{\mathrm{wave}} = 0\) (thm:signals-resonant-energy-conservation).

  • Dawson Wave-Breaking Ceiling: Coherent longitudinal oscillation saturates at the Dawson field \(E_{\mathrm{Dawson}} = m_e \omega_p v_\phi / e\) (lines 2083-2093), bounding the electrostatic energy density \(u_E(\varepsilon_0, E_z) = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0 E_z^2 \le u_E(\varepsilon_0, E_{\mathrm{Dawson}})\) (def:signals-electrostatic-wave-energy-density, thm:signals-dawson-energy-density-bound).

B. Halbach Transverse MHD Extraction & UHV Substation Hull (Sections 4 & 9)¶

To convert axial electron oscillations \(v_z\) into transverse current \(J_x\) for B-LIG electrode capture (lines 2354-2378, lines 2461-2525):

  1. Lorentz Deflection (thm:signals-halbach-transverse-conversion): A static Halbach field \(\mathbf B = (0, B_y, 0)\) deflects \(\mathbf v = (0, 0, v_z)\) with magnetic force \(\mathbf F = q(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B) = (-q v_z B_y, 0, 0)\).

  2. Work-Free Identity & Faraday Load Requirement (thm:signals-magnetic-lorentz-work-free): Because \(q(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)\cdot\mathbf v = 0\), the static magnetic field alone does zero work and cannot extract energy without an opposing transverse load electric field \(E_x\) (FaradayChannel in MHD.lean), whose matched-load power density is bounded by \(\frac{1}{4}\sigma v^2 B^2\) (FaradayChannel.power_density_le_quarter_base) and whose plant efficiency cannot exceed unity (MHDPowerAccounting.full_efficiency_le_one, ClosedLoopArgonMHD.exported_le_declared_input).

  3. Dawson Cavity Energy Bound (def:signals-pulsed-wakefield-halbach, lem:signals-extracted-pulse-energy-le-dawson): The chat estimate of \(100\text{ GV/m} \times 0.1\text{ m} = 10\text{ GV}\) and \(10\text{ GV}\times 100\text{ A} = 1\text{ TW}\) (lines 2368-2376) extrapolates the peak field monotonically across \(10\text{ cm}\) rather than integrating over the plasma wavelength \(\lambda_p \approx 33\,\mu\text{m}\). In PulsedWakefieldHalbachHypothesis, extracted pulse energy is bounded by the total electrostatic energy stored in the cavity volume: \(U_{\mathrm{extracted}} \le u_E(\varepsilon_0, E_{\mathrm{Dawson}})\,V_{\mathrm{cav}}\).

  4. Beltrami/Taylor State & Active Cooling Pinch (thm:signals-taylor-state-force-free, thm:signals-active-cooling-stabilizes-pinch, thm:signals-discrete-four-cycle-gauge-invariance): A collinear Beltrami current \(\mathbf J = \alpha\mathbf B\) eliminates internal disruptive Lorentz forces (\(\mathbf J\times\mathbf B = \mathbf 0\)), active thermal extraction (\(\Delta P_{\mathrm{cool}} > \Delta P_{\mathrm{ohm}}\)) maintains a positive radial magnetic pressure margin, and discrete 4-link circulation is invariant under vertex gauge transformations.

C. Ponderomotive Thruster, Aerodynamic Hull Moments, & Bi-Twistor Sink (Sections 1–2)¶

When operated as an Earth-to-orbit or high-G thruster (lines 279-337, lines 2289-2341, lines 2577-2587):

  • Ponderomotive Cycle Averaging (def:signals-ponderomotive-potential, def:signals-ponderomotive-force, lem:signals-ponderomotive-down-gradient, lem:signals-oscillatory-lorentz-cancels, thm:signals-ponderomotive-cycle-average): First-order quiver motion \(\cos(\omega t)\) integrates to zero over each RF period \(T = 2\pi/\omega\), isolating the secular drift force \(\mathbf F_p(\mathbf x) = -\frac{e^2}{4 m_e \omega^2}\nabla(\lVert\mathbf E_0(\mathbf x)\rVert^2)\) directed down the intensity gradient.

  • Pulsed Duty-Cycle Scaling (def:signals-pulsed-average-force, lem:signals-pulsed-average-force-le-peak, lem:signals-pulsed-wakefield-avg-thrust-le-peak): Dumping hull supercapacitors in nanosecond pulses raises instantaneous peak force \(F_{\mathrm{peak}}\), but time-averaged thrust remains bounded by duty cycle \(d\in[0,1]\): \(F_{\mathrm{avg}} = d\,F_{\mathrm{peak}} \le F_{\mathrm{peak}}\).

  • Classical Aerodynamic Hull Traction & Moments (def:signals-bernoulli-dynamic-pressure, def:signals-bernoulli-static-pressure, def:signals-pressure-coefficient, def:signals-bernoulli-freestream-pressure, thm:signals-bernoulli-static-eq-freestream, thm:signals-bernoulli-stagnation-recovery, def:signals-bernoulli-surface-traction, lem:signals-bernoulli-traction-at-stagnation, def:signals-bernoulli-aerodynamic-moment, def:signals-total-bernoulli-aerodynamic-moment, lem:signals-bernoulli-moment-orthogonal-lever, lem:signals-bernoulli-moment-orthogonal-force): The chat’s raw formula \(-q(\mathbf x)\,\mathbf n(\mathbf x)\) (def:signals-differential-pressure-force) substitutes dynamic pressure \(q(\mathbf x) = \frac{1}{2}\rho\lVert\mathbf V(\mathbf x)\rVert^2\) for static pressure and vanishes at a stagnation point (\(\mathbf V(\mathbf x)=\mathbf 0\), lem:signals-pressure-force-proxy-at-stagnation). bernoulliStaticPressure and bernoulliSurfaceTraction model true static Bernoulli pressure \(p(\mathbf x) = p_0 - q(\mathbf x) = p_\infty + q_\infty C_p(\mathbf x)\) (thm:signals-bernoulli-static-eq-freestream), recovering full stagnation pressure \(p_0\) and \(C_p = 1\) at \(\mathbf V(\mathbf x)=\mathbf 0\) (thm:signals-bernoulli-stagnation-recovery) and decreasing monotonically with local flow speed (lem:signals-bernoulli-static-decreases-with-speed). Integrating over an explicit 2D surface-area measure \(\mu\) (fixing the chat’s 3D Lebesgue volume measure bug) yields differential moments orthogonal to both the lever arm \(\mathbf x - \mathbf x_{\mathrm{CM}}\) and normal force.

  • Bi-Twistor Momentum Sink (def:signals-twistor-momentum-sink, lem:signals-craft-momentum-opposite-wake, lem:signals-closed-polygon-zero-thrust): In dual spacetime (lines 437-449), momentum leaked to the wakefield leaves an unclosed polygon residual \(\Delta\mathbf P_{\mathrm{wake}} = \mathbf x_n - \mathbf x_1\) represented by a bi-twistor sink in \(Gr_{\ge 0}(k, n+2)\), enforcing exact Newton-pair conservation \(\Delta\mathbf P_{\mathrm{craft}} = -\Delta\mathbf P_{\mathrm{wake}}\).

4. Deborah-Number Shear-Fracture / Dilatant Dark Fluid (DDF) Thruster (Sections 3 & 8)¶

In Twistors-and-Positive-Grassmannian-Interference.md, lines 1268-1346 and lines 1546-1618, the alternative vacuum thruster replaces plasma ions with Marco Fedi’s Dilatant Dark Fluid (DDFSubstrate in DDF.lean):

  • Madelung Hydrodynamics & Quantized Circulation (def:signals-madelung-density, def:signals-bohm-quantum-potential, def:signals-superfluid-velocity, thm:signals-quantized-vortex-circulation, thm:signals-ddf-substrate-circulation-eq): Writing \(\psi = \sqrt{\rho}e^{iS}\) gives \(\mathbf v = (\hbar/m)\nabla S\) and Bohm potential \(Q = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\Delta\sqrt{\rho}}{\sqrt{\rho}}\). Around a vortex of integer winding \(n\in\mathbb Z\) at radius \(R\ne 0\), \(\int_0^{2\pi} v_\theta R\,d\theta = n(2\pi\hbar/m)\).

  • Discrete Plaquette Cohomology (thm:signals-plaquette-coboundary-zero, thm:signals-plaquette-phase-slip-syndrome): On a 2D lattice plaquette, single-valued site phases have zero coboundary (\(\delta^1\circ\delta^0 = 0\)), while a \(2\pi n\) branch cut extracts the exact topological charge \(2\pi n\).

  • Bogoliubov Spectrum & Landau Critical Velocity (def:signals-bogoliubov-phase-vel-sq, thm:signals-bogoliubov-landau-bound, thm:signals-subcritical-no-dissipation, thm:signals-supercritical-excitation-onset): Since \((\epsilon(p)/p)^2 = c_s^2 + (p/(2m))^2 \ge c_s^2\), the Landau critical velocity is \(v_c = c_s\). For \(v < v_c\), \(\Delta E = \epsilon(p) - p v > 0\) (frictionless flow); for \(v > v_c\), acoustic modes at \(\epsilon(p) = v_c p\) have \(\Delta E < 0\), nucleating vortices.

  • Pending Hull-Stress & Power-Accounting Boundary (def:signals-ddf-phase-slip-hull-stress, lem:signals-ddf-hull-boundary-density-pos, lem:signals-ddf-zero-drive-zero-thrust): Supercritical vortex shedding in a superfluid is a dissipative drag mechanism, and \(Q\) diverges at \(\rho = 0\) unless regularized by a positive density floor \(0 < \rho_{\mathrm{floor}} \le \rho_{\mathrm{hull}}\). DDFPhaseSlipHullStressHypothesis requires \(F_{\mathrm{thrust}} v \le P_{\mathrm{drive}}\), proving that zero external drive power (\(P_{\mathrm{drive}} = 0\)) yields zero net forward thrust.

5. \(\text{D-He3}\) Aneutronic Deep-Space Reactor & AMB Centrifugal Cooling Spool (Section 5)¶

In Twistors-and-Positive-Grassmannian-Interference.md, lines 2577-2798, deep-space transit pairs aneutronic \(\text{D-He3}\) direct conversion with a levitated centrifugal cooling spool:

  • Radial Centrifugal Head & Closed-Loop Thermosyphon (def:signals-centrifugal-pressure-gradient, thm:signals-centrifugal-pump-head, thm:signals-centrifugal-thermosyphon-head, lem:signals-centrifugal-isothermal-zero): Integrating \(\frac{dP}{dr} = \rho\omega^2 r\) over \([r_{\mathrm{in}}, r_{\mathrm{out}}]\) gives \(\Delta P = \frac{1}{2}\rho\omega^2(r_{\mathrm{out}}^2 - r_{\mathrm{in}}^2)\). In a closed loop, outward and inward radial legs cancel identically unless cooled/heated density differences drive a thermosyphon head \(\Delta P_{\mathrm{loop}} = \frac{1}{2}(\rho_{\mathrm{cold}} - \rho_{\mathrm{hot}})\omega^2(r_{\mathrm{out}}^2 - r_{\mathrm{in}}^2)\) (centrifugal_thermosyphon_net_head).

  • Gyroscopic Attitude Control (def:signals-spool-angular-momentum, def:signals-gyroscopic-torque, thm:signals-pitch-induces-yaw-torque, lem:signals-gyroscopic-torque-orthogonal-precession, lem:signals-gyroscopic-torque-orthogonal-momentum): Precessing the \(z\)-axis spool (\(\mathbf L = (0, 0, I_{\mathrm{spool}}\omega_z)\)) around the \(x\)-axis (\(\boldsymbol\Omega = (\Omega_x, 0, 0)\)) produces the orthogonal \(y\)-axis yaw torque \((0, -\Omega_x I_{\mathrm{spool}}\omega_z, 0)\).

  • 5-Axis \(\text{MgB}_2\) AMB & \(\text{He-3}\) Phase Diagram Critique (def:signals-cryogenic-spool-amb, lem:signals-he3-supercritical-at-39k, lem:signals-he3-no-solid-seal-below-3-4-mpa): Because \(\text{He-3}\) has critical temperature \(T_c = 3.32\text{ K}\) and minimum solidification pressure \(P_{\mathrm{solid}} = 3.4\text{ MPa}\), operating at the \(\text{MgB}_2\) temperature of \(39\text{ K}\) places \(\text{He-3}\) in the supercritical gas regime (supercritical_at_39K), and eddy currents cannot freeze electrically insulating \(\text{He-3}\) into a solid labyrinth seal below \(3.4\text{ MPa}\) (no_solid_seal_below_3_4_MPa).
