## Abstract

Autoregressive (`AR`) Large Language Models generate text sequentially in $L$ steps ($O(L)$ sequential weight-fetch memory bottlenecks), whereas discrete and continuous Diffusion Language Models (`DLMs`, such as `MDLM` {cite}`sahoo2024mdlm` and `DUO` {cite}`sahoo2025duo`) process the full length-$L$ sequence at every denoising step $t \in \{1,\dots,T\}$, forfeiting causal KV-caching and requiring $4\text{--}5\times$ larger training-token budgets. Recently, Kumbong et al. ({cite}`kumbong2026ncm`, [`arXiv:2610.14162`](#ncm-kumbong2026)) introduced **Next-Concept Modeling (`NCM`)**, a two-stage coarse-to-fine discrete hierarchical framework in which a multiscale residual VQ-VAE encodes text into $K \ll L$ levels of progressively longer code sequences $\ell_1 < \ell_2 < \cdots < \ell_K = L$ and a block-diagonal transformer generates one level at a time, predicting all $\ell_k$ codes within level $k$ in parallel from the cumulative reconstruction $\hat{z}^{(k-1)}$. While `NCM` achieves **$10\text{--}31\times$ faster wall-clock inference** and **$4\text{--}16\times$ fewer function evaluations (`NFE`)** than diffusion baselines, our mathematical and causal audit identifies four structural bottlenecks in classical `NCM`: **(i) intra-level conditional independence** ($\prod_{i=1}^{\ell_k} p_\theta(r_k^i \mid \hat{z}^{(k-1)})$ has zero intra-level conditional mutual information $I(r_k^i : r_k^j \mid \hat{z}^{(k-1)}) = 0$, allowing conflicting parallel choices in Hard Sudoku and named-entity/numeral substitutions), **(ii) teacher-forcing exposure confounding** (where the encoder latent $z = E_\phi(x)$ acts as an unobserved backdoor confounder between $\hat{z}^{(k-1)}$ and $r_k$), **(iii) quadratic intra-block self-attention** ($\sum_{k=1}^K \ell_k^2 d_{\mathrm{head}}$ as $\ell_k \to L$), and **(iv) electronic HBM weight-fetch and sequential $M_{\mathrm{cand}} \cdot K$ Minimum Bayes Risk (`MBR`) overhead**.

In this paper, we synthesize `NCM` with our **Grassmannian Quantum Causal Inference (`QCI`)** ([Paper 18](grassmannian-quantum-causal-inference-mpemba-paper.md); [Paper 20](grassmannian-quantum-causal-jev-bundle-transformer-videogen-paper.md); [Paper 21](grassmannian-quantum-causal-splat-nanoimaging-surgical-graphrag-paper.md)), **Pressed / Mask-Locked 1T-Parameter `LHAT` Holographic Tape** ([Paper 38](grassmannian-pressed-llm-holographic-tape-soliton-qram-steering-kicad-paper.md)), and provenance chat architectures ({download}`_Read our A2Q and quantum review chats about N-LIG and B-LIG...md, lines 105-285 <../data/chats/_Read our A2Q and quantum review chats about N-LIG and B-LIG.__Read our chats on hardware implementation of LLMs with _steering_ layers__Design a USB A+C thumb drive given same. .md>`; {download}`_LLMs, Fourier, and Quantum Logic .md, lines 28-695 <../data/chats/_LLMs, Fourier, and Quantum Logic .md>`; {download}`_Mpemba Effect_ Landau vs. Quantum .md, lines 395-535 <../data/chats/_Mpemba Effect_ Landau vs. Quantum .md>`) to develop **Quantum Causal Next-Concept Modeling (`QCI-NCM` and `QCI-G-NCM`)** and its **Pressed-`LHAT` Optoelectronic Accelerator (`Pressed-NCM-QCI`)**, machine-checked in **Lean 4** (`Signals.QuantumCausalInference`, Section 15) and **Rust/WASM** (`quantum_causal_inference.rs`, Section 10; `cannon_mask_cad.rs`):

### 1.1 Mathematical Formulation of `NCM` (`Kumbong et al. 2026`)

In *Next-Concept Modeling: Fast, Parallel Coarse-to-Fine Text Generation* ({cite}`kumbong2026ncm`, [`arXiv:2610.14162`](#ncm-kumbong2026)), Kumbong, Khare, Mlauzi, Woen, Ding, Buchanan, Arora, Mirhoseini, and Ré reformulate non-autoregressive language modeling around a two-stage coarse-to-fine discrete concept hierarchy:

1. **Stage 1 — Multiscale Residual VQ-VAE Tokenization with Nested Dropout**:
   Given a token sequence $x \in \mathcal{T}^L$ of length $L$, an encoder $E_\phi$ maps $x$ to continuous latents $z = E_\phi(x) \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times d}$. Across $K \ll L$ hierarchical levels with strictly increasing sequence lengths $\ell_1 < \ell_2 < \cdots < \ell_K = L$ and level-specific codebooks $\mathcal{V}_k = \{e_{k,1}, \dots, e_{k,|\mathcal{V}_k|}\} \subset \mathbb{R}^d$, multiscale residual quantization initializes $z^{(0)} = z$ and $\hat{z}^{(0)} = 0$, and at each level $k \in \{1, \dots, K\}$ downsamples the residual $z^{(k-1)}$, quantizes each of the $\ell_k$ positions by nearest-neighbor lookup, and maps the quantized vectors back to length $L$ via a reconstruction operator $R_k$:

   $$
   u_k = \mathrm{Down}_k\bigl(z^{(k-1)}\bigr) \in \mathbb{R}^{\ell_k \times d}, \qquad
   r_k^i = \arg\min_{j \in \{1,\dots,|\mathcal{V}_k|\}} \bigl\|u_k^i - e_{k,j}\bigr\|_2^2,
   $$

   $$
   \hat{z}^{(k)} = \hat{z}^{(k-1)} + R_k\bigl(e_k(r_k)\bigr), \qquad
   z^{(k)} = z - \hat{z}^{(k)}, \qquad
   \hat{z}^{(K)} = \sum_{k=1}^K R_k\bigl(e_k(r_k)\bigr).
   $$

   To force early coarse levels ($\ell_k \ll L$) to encode global semantic **concepts** rather than deferring all information to the finest level $\ell_K = L$, `NCM` applies **nested dropout** during tokenizer training: a random cutoff level $k \in \{1, \dots, K\}$ is sampled, and both the token reconstruction cross-entropy and VQ commitment/codebook MSE losses are evaluated on the truncated cumulative reconstruction $\hat{z}^{(k)}$:

   $$
   \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{tok}} = -\frac{1}{L}\sum_{i=1}^L \log p_\psi\bigl(x^i \mid \hat{z}^{(k)}\bigr) + \beta\,\mathrm{MSE}\bigl(z, \mathrm{sg}[\hat{z}^{(k)}]\bigr) + \mathrm{MSE}\bigl(\mathrm{sg}[z], \hat{z}^{(k)}\bigr).
   $$

2. **Stage 2 — Coarse-to-Fine Parallel Level Generation with Block-Diagonal Attention**:
   With the multiscale VQ-VAE frozen, the `NCM` generator transformer $p_\theta$ models the joint prior over the discrete concept hierarchy $r_{1:K} = (r_1, \dots, r_K)$ autoregressively **across levels** $k = 1, \dots, K$, while predicting **all $\ell_k$ codes within level $k$ conditionally independently in parallel**:

   $$
   p_\theta(r_{1:K}) = \prod_{k=1}^K p_\theta\bigl(r_k \mid \hat{z}^{(k-1)}\bigr) = \prod_{k=1}^K \prod_{i=1}^{\ell_k} p_\theta\bigl(r_k^i \mid \hat{z}^{(k-1)}\bigr).
   $$

   Because the cumulative reconstruction $\hat{z}^{(k-1)}$ already summarizes levels $1, \dots, k-1$, resizing and projecting $\hat{z}^{(k-1)}$ to $\ell_k$ positions allows the transformer to use **block-diagonal attention** (attending only within the $\ell_k$ positions of level $k$, plus the prompt prefix $P$ in conditional generation). During training, all $K$ levels are trained simultaneously in a single forward pass under **conditioning corruption** (replacing a level-dependent fraction of conditioning codes $r_k$ with random codebook entries $\tilde{r}_k$ to form $\tilde{z}^{(k-1)}$ while keeping target labels $r_k$ clean):

   $$
   \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{NCM}} = -\sum_{k=1}^K w_k \sum_{i=1}^{\ell_k} \log p_\theta\bigl(r_k^i \mid \tilde{z}^{(k-1)}\bigr), \qquad
   w_k = \frac{\ell_k^{-\alpha}}{\sum_{j=1}^K \ell_j^{1-\alpha}}.
   $$

3. **Deterministic Point-Mass Variational ELBO (`Appendix A` of `arXiv:2610.14162`)**:
   Using the deterministic nearest-neighbor assignment $r_{1:K}^*(x)$ as a point-mass variational posterior $q(r_{1:K} \mid x) = \mathbf{1}\{r_{1:K} = r_{1:K}^*(x)\}$ with zero entropy ($H(q) = 0$), the Evidence Lower Bound (`ELBO`) on the marginal text log-likelihood $\log p_{\theta,\psi}(x)$ is exact up to the posterior KL divergence:

   $$
   \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ELBO}}(x) = \underbrace{\sum_{n=1}^L \log p_\psi\bigl(x^n \mid \hat{z}_*^{(K)}\bigr)}_{\text{Reconstruction}} + \underbrace{\sum_{k=1}^K \sum_{i=1}^{\ell_k} \log p_\theta\bigl(r_k^{*i} \mid \hat{z}_*^{(k-1)}\bigr)}_{\text{Coarse-to-Fine Generation}} \le \log p_{\theta,\psi}(x),
   $$

   $$
   \log p_{\theta,\psi}(x) - \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ELBO}}(x) = D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(q(r_{1:K} \mid x) \,\|\, p_{\theta,\psi}(r_{1:K} \mid x)\right) = -\log p_{\theta,\psi}\bigl(r_{1:K}^*(x) \mid x\bigr) \ge 0.
   $$

### 1.3 Four Structural and Causal Bottlenecks in Classical `NCM`

Despite `NCM`'s dramatic empirical gains over autoregressive and diffusion models, analyzing `arXiv:2610.14162` through the lens of **Structural Causal Models (`SCMs`)**, **Quantum Causal Inference (`QCI`)**, **Grassmannian fiber-bundle geometry**, and **optoelectronic hardware** reveals four fundamental limitations:

1. **Bottleneck 1 — Intra-Level Conditional Independence ($I(r_k^i : r_k^j \mid \hat{z}^{(k-1)}) = 0$)**:

### 3.1 Why Conditioning Corruption Works: Eliminating Teacher-Forcing Backdoor Confounding (`ncmCorruptionDeconfoundedBias`)

Why does uncorrupted teacher forcing degrade so severely in `NCM` (`Figure 10` of `arXiv:2610.14162`), and why does a linearly decreasing corruption schedule improve TinyStories generative perplexity from $>25$ down to $15.41$?

In our causal decision framework ([Paper 20](grassmannian-quantum-causal-jev-bundle-transformer-videogen-paper.md), `jevEvidentialExpectedUtility` vs. `cevCausalDoExpectedUtility`), let $z = E_\phi(x)$ be the ground-truth encoder latent during training. $$
\hat{z}^{(k-1)} \longleftarrow z = E_\phi(x) \longrightarrow r_k.
$$

When the generator is trained with uncorrupted teacher forcing ($p_{\mathrm{corr}} = 0$), it learns the **observational (evidential) conditional distribution** $P\bigl(r_k \mid \hat{z}^{(k-1)}\bigr) = P\bigl(r_k \mid \operatorname{do}(\hat{z}^{(k-1)})\bigr) + b_{\mathrm{conf}}$, whose expected concept utility is the confounded **Jeffrey Expected Value (`JEV`)**:

$$
\mathrm{JEV} = \operatorname{cevCausalDoExpectedUtility}(p_{\mathrm{do},1}, 1 - p_{\mathrm{do},1}, u_1, u_2) + b_{\mathrm{conf}}(u_1 - u_2).
$$

$$
\operatorname{ncmCorruptionDeconfoundedBias}(p_{\mathrm{corr}}, b_{\mathrm{conf}}) = (1 - p_{\mathrm{corr}})\, b_{\mathrm{conf}},
$$

$$
\operatorname{ncmCorruptedExpectedConceptUtility}(p_{\mathrm{do},1}, u_1, u_2, p_{\mathrm{corr}}, b_{\mathrm{conf}}) = \mathrm{CEV} + (1 - p_{\mathrm{corr}})\,b_{\mathrm{conf}}(u_1 - u_2).
$$

We machine-check in `qci_ncm_multiscale_counterfactual_corruption_vrc_and_pressed_llm_identities` that:
1. At $p_{\mathrm{corr}} = 0$, `ncmCorruptedExpectedConceptUtility` reduces identically to the confounded `jevEvidentialExpectedUtility`,
2. At $p_{\mathrm{corr}} = 1$, `ncmCorruptionDeconfoundedBias 1 b_conf = 0`, recovering Pearl's exact interventional `cevCausalDoExpectedUtility`, and
