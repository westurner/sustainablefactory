### 2.1 Overcoming the Quantum Capacitance ($C_Q$) Ceiling via Semi-Dirac $\varepsilon^{-1/2}$ Dispersion (`seriesQuantumDoubleLayerCapacitance`)

In conventional graphene and carbon-nanotube supercapacitors ({download}`Carbon Chips_ Hurdles and Future .md, lines 685-740, 945-965 <../data/chats/Carbon Chips_ Hurdles and Future .md>`), sub-nanometer micropores ($d_{\mathrm{pore}} \approx 0.7\text{–}0.9\text{ nm}$) strip the ion solvation shell ("anomalous desolvation capacitance"), driving the geometric Helmholtz double-layer capacitance $C_{\mathrm{EDL}} = \varepsilon_r \varepsilon_0 / d_{\mathrm{pore}}$ up to $C_{\mathrm{EDL}} \approx 600\text{ F/g}$. However, because the low-dimensional carbon sheet has a finite electronic Density of States $D(E_F)$ at the Fermi level, adding charge $dQ$ requires shifting the chemical potential by $dE_F = dQ / (e^2 D(E_F))$. This introduces the **Quantum Capacitance** $C_Q = e^2 D(E_F)$ in **harmonic series** with $C_{\mathrm{EDL}}$ (`seriesQuantumDoubleLayerCapacitance` in [../src/signals/Signals/SolitonBus.lean](../src/signals/Signals/SolitonBus.lean#L1860-L1873)):

$$
\frac{1}{C_{\mathrm{tot}}(C_{\mathrm{EDL}}, C_Q)} = \frac{1}{C_{\mathrm{EDL}}} + \frac{1}{C_Q} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad C_{\mathrm{tot}}(C_{\mathrm{EDL}}, C_Q) = \frac{C_{\mathrm{EDL}}\,C_Q}{C_{\mathrm{EDL}} + C_Q}.
$$

In unstrained 2D monolayer graphene, the massless Dirac dispersion $E_{\mathrm{Dirac}}(\mathbf{k}) = \hbar v_F |\mathbf{k}|$ yields a linear density of states $D_{\mathrm{Dirac}}(\varepsilon) = \frac{2|\varepsilon|}{\pi \hbar^2 v_F^2}$ that **vanishes at the Dirac point** ($\varepsilon \to 0$). At moderate doping ($C_{Q,\mathrm{Dirac}} = 200\text{ F/g}$), quantum capacitance chokes the total electrode capacitance to:

$$
C_{\mathrm{tot}}^{\mathrm{Dirac}}(600, 200) = \frac{600 \times 200}{600 + 200} = \mathbf{150\text{ F/g}},
$$

wasting $75\%$ of the $600\text{ F/g}$ double-layer capacity. By applying $532\text{ nm} / 1064\text{ nm}$ Laser Compression Shock (`LCS`) strain engineering from [Paper 29](grassmannian-semi-dirac-fermions-ddf-paper.md) and [Paper 31](grassmannian-semi-dirac-klein-transistor-hyperbolic-thz-thermoelectric-kicad-paper.md) to merge the Dirac cones into an **anisotropic Semi-Dirac node** ($E_{\mathrm{SD}}(\mathbf{k}) = \sqrt{(\hbar^2 k_x^2 / 2m_x^*)^2 + (\hbar v_y k_y)^2}$, where the DOS exponent flips from $+1$ to $-1/2$, $D_{\mathrm{SD}}(\varepsilon) \propto \sqrt{m_x^*}/(v_y \sqrt{\varepsilon})$) coupled with covalently grafted quinone flat-band states ($C_{Q,\mathrm{SD}} = 1800\text{ F/g}$), the series capacitance triples (`seriesQuantumDoubleLayerCapacitance 600 1800 = 450 = 3 * seriesQuantumDoubleLayerCapacitance 600 200`):

$$
C_{\mathrm{tot}}^{\mathrm{SD}}(600, 1800) = \frac{600 \times 1800}{600 + 1800} = \mathbf{450\text{ F/g}} = 3 \times C_{\mathrm{tot}}^{\mathrm{Dirac}}(600, 200).
$$

     - *High Process Energy ($E_{\mathrm{KOH}} \approx 20\text{ kWh/kg}$)*: Combining the $24\text{ hr}$ autoclave, $800^\circ\text{C}$ furnace, drying heat, and embodied $\mathrm{KOH}/\mathrm{HCl}$ reagent energy consumes $\sim 20\text{ kWh/kg}$ of finished carbon ($\approx 111.1\text{ kWh}$ per kg of finished carbon normalized to raw bast yield, or $20 / 0.18\text{ kWh/kg-product}$).

While the bulk gas remains below **$T_{\mathrm{gas}} < 60^\circ\text{C}$** ("Zero-Preheat"), non-equilibrium electrons ($T_e \sim 10{,}000\text{–}50{,}000\text{ K}$, fundamental plasma frequency $f_p = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{n_e e^2 / (\varepsilon_0 m_e)} = 284\text{ GHz–}2.84\text{ THz}$ at $n_e = 10^{15}\text{–}10^{17}\text{ cm}^{-3}$) dissociate $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$ into excited hydroxyl radicals ($\mathrm{OH}^*$, tracked in real time via the **$309\text{ nm}$ ($970\text{ THz}$) $A^2\Sigma^+(v'=0) \to X^2\Pi(v''=0)$ near-UV Optical Emission Spectroscopy line**). Within **milliseconds to seconds**, $\cdot\mathrm{OH}$ selectively ablates the amorphous middle-lamella pectin/waxy cuticle ("dry cottonization", increasing roughness $R_a$ by $\ge 300\%$ and dropping water contact angle to $0^\circ$ without caustic $\mathrm{NaOH}$) while free-radical oxidative/ammonia crosslinking forms an infusible outer shell that prevents fiber fusion.
   - **Stage B2 (Ethyl Lactate Elasto-Capillary Densification + Millisecond `FJH`/`LCS` Graphitization)**: Immersing the super-hydrophilic plasma-treated web in biodegradable **ethyl lactate** ({download}`Lignin-Derived-Carbon-Fibers-Applications.md, lines 340-365 <../data/chats/Lignin-Derived-Carbon-Fibers-Applications.md>`) uses receding-meniscus capillary forces to pull the intact fibers from $80\%$ void space ($\rho \approx 0.35\text{ g/cm}^3$) into a densely aligned mat ($\rho_{\mathrm{plasma}} = 1.05\text{ g/cm}^3$, **$3\times$ higher packing density**) without mechanical crushing, followed by millisecond Flash Joule Heating (`FJH`) or $1064\text{ nm}$ `LCS` graphitization.
   - **Stage B3 (Surface-Selective $10\text{–}50\text{ nm}$ $\cdot\mathrm{OH}$ Plasma Pore Etching & In-Situ Pseudocapacitive Grafting)**: Exposing the carbonized hemp bast web to a second $\cdot\mathrm{OH}$ (and $\mathrm{NH}_3$) cold plasma zone selectively oxidizes weak, amorphous turbostratic defect carbon into volatile $\mathrm{CO}/\mathrm{CO}_2$ across only the **outer $10\text{–}50\text{ nm}$ skin** ($S_{\mathrm{BET}} = 600\text{–}900\text{ m}^2/\text{g}$), leaving the inner crystalline $sp^2$ conductive core completely intact (**$\sigma_{\mathrm{plasma}} = 95 \to 85 \to 75\text{ S/cm}$ at $10\% \to 50\% \to 90\%$ intensity**) and doubling net carbon yield to **$Y_{\mathrm{plasma}} = 36\%$**. Because bulk temperature never exceeds $60^\circ\text{C}$, the $\cdot\mathrm{OH}/\mathrm{NH}_3$ plasma simultaneously grafts dense polar hydroxyl ($-\mathrm{OH}$), carboxyl ($-\mathrm{COOH}$), quinone ($=\mathrm{O}$), and pyridinic/amine ($-\mathrm{NH}_2$) pseudocapacitive redox groups directly onto the pore rims with **zero thermal burn-off, zero $\mathrm{KOH}$, zero $\mathrm{HCl}$, and zero wastewater**.

### 2.5 How $15\times$ Electrical Conductivity, $3\times$ Packing Density, and $0.36\times$ Surface-Confined BET Area Transform Battery, Capacitor, Pseudocapacitor, and Supercapacitor Specifications (`volumetricSpecificSurfaceArea`, `volumetricSpecificCapacitanceFPerCm3`, `batteryInitialCoulombicEfficiency`, `poreDiffusionCharacteristicTimeUs`)

At first glance, trading a $2{,}500\text{ m}^2/\text{g}$ $\mathrm{KOH}$-etched powder for a $900\text{ m}^2/\text{g}$ ($0.36\times$) $\cdot\mathrm{OH}$-plasma core-shell fiber might appear to sacrifice gravimetric surface area. In reality, when coupled with the **$3\times$ packing density** ($\rho_{\mathrm{pack}}: 0.35 \to 1.05\text{ g/cm}^3$) and **$15\times$ longitudinal electrical conductivity** ($\sigma_{\mathrm{elec}}: 5 \to 75\text{ S/cm}$), those three factors ($\mathbf{15\times \sigma,\; 3\times \rho,\; 0.36\times S_{\mathrm{BET}}}$) systematically eliminate the primary failure modes of batteries, electrostatic filter capacitors, pseudocapacitors, and EDLC supercapacitors (`Signals.SolitonBus` [../src/signals/Signals/SolitonBus.lean](../src/signals/Signals/SolitonBus.lean#L2150-L2285)):

1. **Volumetric Surface Area Paradox Resolved: $0.36\times$ Gravimetric BET $\times$ $3\times$ Packing Density $= \mathbf{1.08\times}$ Higher Pore Area per $\mathrm{cm}^3$ (`volumetricSpecificSurfaceArea`)**:
   In any volume-constrained device (`Q-AA`, `Q-18650`, `4680`, EV chassis rail, or railroad tie), the physically relevant geometric capacity is the **volumetric surface area** $S_{\mathrm{vol}} = S_{\mathrm{BET}} \cdot \rho_{\mathrm{pack}}$ ($\mathrm{m}^2/\mathrm{cm}^3$):

   $$
   S_{\mathrm{vol}}^{\mathrm{KOH}}\!\left(2500,\, \frac{7}{20}\right) = 875\text{ m}^2/\text{cm}^3 \quad < \quad S_{\mathrm{vol}}^{\mathrm{OH\text{-}plasma}}\!\left(900,\, \frac{21}{20}\right) = \mathbf{945\text{ m}^2/\text{cm}^3} = \frac{27}{25}\,S_{\mathrm{vol}}^{\mathrm{KOH}} = \mathbf{1.08\times}\,S_{\mathrm{vol}}^{\mathrm{KOH}}.
   $$

   Because $0.36 \times 3.0 = 1.08 > 1$, **the $\cdot\mathrm{OH}$-plasma + ethyl-lactate elasto-capillary electrode actually packs $+8\%$ more total pore surface area into every cubic centimeter than $2{,}500\text{ m}^2/\text{g}$ $\mathrm{KOH}$-activated carbon**, without the $80\%$ dead electrolyte void volume of fluffy $\mathrm{KOH}$ hydrochar.

2. **Supercapacitor (`E-Cap` EDLC) Impact: $10^6\times$ Faster Shell Ion Diffusion (`poreDiffusionCharacteristicTimeUs`) & $9\times$ Volumetric Capacitance (`volumetricSpecificCapacitanceFPerCm3`)**:
   - **Eliminating De Levie Deep-Pore Ion Starvation ($10^6\times$ Diffusion Speedup)**:
     In bulk $\mathrm{KOH}$-etched carbon particles, ions must diffuse across $L_{\mathrm{bulk}} \approx 30\text{ }\mu\text{m} = 30{,}000\text{ nm}$ of tortuous internal micropores. In the $\cdot\mathrm{OH}$-plasma core-shell fiber, $100\%$ of the $900\text{ m}^2/\text{g}$ pore network is confined to the **outer $L_{\mathrm{shell}} = 30\text{ nm}$ ($10\text{–}50\text{ nm}$) skin**. By `poreDiffusionCharacteristicTimeUs` ($\tau_{\mathrm{diff}} = L^2 / D_{\mathrm{ion}}$ at $D_{\mathrm{ion}} = 1000\text{ nm}^2/\mu\text{s} = 10^{-5}\text{ cm}^2/\text{s}$):

     $$
     \tau_{\mathrm{diff}}^{\mathrm{OH\text{-}plasma}}(30, 1000) = \frac{9}{10} = \mathbf{0.9\text{ }\mu\text{s}} \quad \ll \quad \tau_{\mathrm{diff}}^{\mathrm{KOH}}(30000, 1000) = \mathbf{900{,}000\text{ }\mu\text{s}} = 0.9\text{ s} = \mathbf{10^6\times}\,\tau_{\mathrm{diff}}^{\mathrm{OH\text{-}plasma}}.
     $$

     Consequently, $100\%$ of the $900\text{ m}^2/\text{g}$ shell remains electrochemically active during microsecond `CNS` **Lightning Gulp** surges and sub-second $45\text{ kW/kg}$ discharges, whereas $>60\%$ of the deep $\mathrm{KOH}$ micropores are ion-starved above $1\text{ Hz}$.
   - **$9\times$ Volumetric Capacitance ($52.5 \to 472.5\text{ F/cm}^3$) & Current-Collector-Free Mass Saving**:
     Combining the $3\times$ Semi-Dirac quantum-capacitance boost ($C_{\mathrm{grav}}: 150 \to 450\text{ F/g}$) with the $3\times$ packing density ($\rho_{\mathrm{pack}}: 0.35 \to 1.05\text{ g/cm}^3$) yields a **$9\times$ gain in volumetric capacitance** (`volumetricSpecificCapacitanceFPerCm3 450 (21/20) = 9 * volumetricSpecificCapacitanceFPerCm3 150 (7/20)`):

     $$
     C_{\mathrm{vol}}^{\mathrm{KOH}}\!\left(150,\, \frac{7}{20}\right) = \frac{105}{2} = \mathbf{52.5\text{ F/cm}^3} \quad \longrightarrow \quad C_{\mathrm{vol}}^{\mathrm{OH\text{-}plasma}}\!\left(450,\, \frac{21}{20}\right) = \frac{945}{2} = \mathbf{472.5\text{ F/cm}^3} = \mathbf{9.0\times}\,C_{\mathrm{vol}}^{\mathrm{KOH}}.
     $$

     Simultaneously, the $15\times$ higher electrical conductivity ($\sigma_{\mathrm{plasma}} = 75\text{ S/cm}$) allows the intact bast-fiber core to act as its own **integrated current collector**, eliminating both the $15\text{–}25\text{ wt}\%$ metallic foil collector and the $10\text{ wt}\%$ insulating binder paste required by brittle $\mathrm{KOH}$ powders.

3. **Pseudocapacitor (`Q-Node`) Impact: High-Density $<60^\circ\text{C}$ Quinone/Pyridinic-N Rim Grafting + $15\times$ Lower Core Ohmic Drop**:

Paired with the $15\times$ more conductive fiber core feeding electrons directly into the $30\text{ nm}$ redox skin, `Q-Node` pseudocapacitors achieve **$180\text{–}240\text{ Wh/kg}$** and **$189\text{–}252\text{ Wh/L}$** ($3\times$ the volumetric energy of $\mathrm{KOH}$-paste pseudocapacitors) at **$25\text{ kW/kg}$**.

Modeling first-cycle Initial Coulombic Efficiency as $\mathrm{ICE}(k_{\mathrm{SEI}}, S_{\mathrm{BET}}) = 1 - k_{\mathrm{SEI}}\,S_{\mathrm{BET}}$ (`batteryInitialCoulombicEfficiency` at $k_{\mathrm{SEI}} = 22/100{,}000\text{ g/m}^2$):

   - **$15\times$ Conductivity + Intact Crystalline Core Unlocks $2\text{-Minute}$ ($30\text{C}$) Charging & $>800\text{ MPa}$ Structural Chassis Integration**:

## 4. Deep Critique & Eight Mathematical Gap Closures (`omni_carbon_energy_storage_deep_gap_closure_identities`)
