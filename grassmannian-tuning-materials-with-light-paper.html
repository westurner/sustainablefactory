Abstract¶

When an ultrafast laser pulse strikes a solid, electron–electron (\(\mathrm{e\text{-}e}\)), electron–phonon (\(\mathrm{e\text{-}ph}\)), and phonon–phonon (\(\mathrm{ph\text{-}ph}\)) interactions occur simultaneously across timescales spanning femtoseconds to hundreds of picoseconds. While the two-time Kadanoff–Baym Equations (KBE) of non-equilibrium Green’s function (NEGF) theory [Kadanoff and Baym, 1962] provide an exact many-body formulation of this regime, their cubic scaling in the number of time steps \(O(N_t^3)\) and cubic scaling in the orbital basis \(O(N_{\mathrm{basis}}^3)\) make long-time ab initio simulations of real materials computationally prohibitive. Discovered Materials Grand Challenge Problem 04 (“Tuning Materials with Light — Compute what a laser pulse does to a real material, with electrons and lattice vibrations treated together, in a computationally tractable way over long time scales”, written with inputs from Prof. Felipe H. da Jornada) [Discovered Materials and da Jornada, 2026] calls for a method that is orders of magnitude faster than two-time NEGF, valid from weak to strong drives and high carrier/exciton densities, and simultaneously verified on a strongly correlated model system (11-site Hubbard chains and \(6\times 2\) Hubbard ladders [Schroedter et al., 2026]) and judged on three real materials (\(\mathrm{WSe}_2\) [Madéo et al., 2020], Graphite [René de Cotret et al., 2019], and \(\mathrm{MgB}_2\) [Katsumi and others, 2025]).

In this paper, we develop, implement, and formally verify the Time-Linear Grassmannian Generalized Kadanoff–Baym Ansatz (G-GKBA) framework by uniting six mathematical pillars from our companion Grassmannian papers:

  1. Method — Time-Linear Grassmannian G-GKBA with Occupied-Subspace Unitary Cayley Retraction & Multi-Lag Plücker Memory Compression (grassmannGKBASpeedupRatio): Instead of propagating the two-time contour matrix \(G^{\lessgtr}(t, t')\) on a quadratic \((t, t') \in [0, T_{\max}]^2\) grid at \(O(N_t^3 N_{\mathrm{basis}}^3)\) cost (twoTimeKBEFlops), G-GKBA evolves the equal-time spin-polarized electronic frame \(X_\sigma(t) \in \mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(N_\sigma, N_{\mathrm{basis}})\) via the unitary Cayley transform retraction \(U(\Omega_\sigma) = (I - \frac{1}{2}\Omega_\sigma)^{-1}(I + \frac{1}{2}\Omega_\sigma)\) (cayleyRetractionNormSq_eq_one in Signals.DFT) alongside time-linear \(O(N_t)\) coupled differential equations [Lipavský et al., 1986, Schlünzen et al., 2020] for the McWeeny idempotency-defect cumulant \(\delta\rho_\sigma(t) = \rho_\sigma(t) - (3\rho_\sigma^2 - 2\rho_\sigma^3)\), the exciton/polariton VRC amplitude on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\) (VectorRelationConfiguration with \(V K^\top = 0\)), the mode-resolved phonon covariance splats \(n_{\mathbf{q},\nu}(t)\), and the Dual-Grassmannian Nambu–Bogoliubov pairing pseudospin on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4) \times \mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)^\vee\). We prove in Lean 4 (tuning_materials_with_light_gkba_hubbard_wse2_graphite_and_mgb2_identities) that the resulting speedup over two-time KBE is:

    \[ \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{G\text{-}GKBA}}(N_t, N_{\mathrm{occ}}, N_{\mathrm{basis}}) = N_t^2 \left(\frac{N_{\mathrm{basis}}}{N_{\mathrm{occ}}}\right)^2, \]

    achieving an exact \(\mathbf{1.44 \times 10^8\times}\) (\(144{,}000{,}000\times\)) reduction in floating-point operations at \(N_t = 1{,}000\) steps and \(N_{\mathrm{basis}} / N_{\mathrm{occ}} = 12\) (def2-QZVP).

In exact two-time Kadanoff–Baym theory [Kadanoff and Baym, 1962], the lesser and greater Green’s functions \(G_\sigma^{\lessgtr}(t, t')\) obey:

\[ \left(i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t} - h_{\sigma}^{\mathrm{HF}}(t)\right) G_\sigma^{\lessgtr}(t, t') = \int_{t_0}^{t} d\bar{t}\,\Bigl(\Sigma_\sigma^{\mathrm{R}}(t, \bar{t})\,G_\sigma^{\lessgtr}(\bar{t}, t') + \Sigma_\sigma^{\lessgtr}(t, \bar{t})\,G_\sigma^{\mathrm{A}}(\bar{t}, t')\Bigr), \]

where \(\Sigma_\sigma = \Sigma_{\sigma}^{\mathrm{e\text{-}e}} + \Sigma_{\sigma}^{\mathrm{e\text{-}ph}}\). Because evaluating the history integral at every pair \((t_m, t_n)\) on an \(N_t \times N_t\) two-time grid requires \(O(N_t)\) operations per grid point, the total computational cost scales as (twoTimeKBEFlops in …/src/signals/Signals/DFT.lean):

\[ C_{\mathrm{KBE}}(N_t, N_{\mathrm{basis}}) = N_t^3 \, N_{\mathrm{basis}}^3. \]

2. Method: Time-Linear Grassmannian Generalized Kadanoff–Baym Ansatz (G-GKBA)¶

2.1 Uniting the Six Grassmannian Modules into a Single Coupled Dynamical System¶

Our method—Time-Linear Grassmannian GKBA (G-GKBA)—eliminates the \(O(N_t^3)\) two-time memory bottleneck and the \(O(N_{\mathrm{basis}}^3)\) full-basis diagonalization bottleneck simultaneously by evolving four coupled coordinates on product Grassmannian manifolds:

  1. Coherent Occupied Electronic Subspace on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(N_\alpha, N_{\mathrm{basis}}) \times \mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(N_\beta, N_{\mathrm{basis}})\) via Unitary Cayley Retraction: The coherent part of the one-particle density matrix \(P_\sigma(t) = X_\sigma(t)^\dagger X_\sigma(t)\) (\(X_\sigma X_\sigma^\dagger = I_{N_\sigma}\)) is stepped along the skew-Hermitian Peierls–Hartree–Fock tangent generator \(\Omega_\sigma(t) = -\frac{i\,\Delta t}{\hbar}\bigl[h_\sigma^{\mathrm{HF}}(\mathbf{A}(t)),\, P_\sigma(t)\bigr]\) using our rational Cayley transform retraction (cayleyRetractionNormSq in Signals.DFT):

    \[ X_\sigma(t + \Delta t) = X_\sigma(t)\,\left(I - \frac{1}{2}\Omega_\sigma(t)\right)^{-1}\left(I + \frac{1}{2}\Omega_\sigma(t)\right), \]

    which preserves exact unitarity \(|(1 + i\theta/2)/(1 - i\theta/2)|^2 = 1\) for every step size \(\Delta t\) (cayleyRetractionNormSq_eq_one) in \(O(N_{\mathrm{occ}}^2 N_{\mathrm{basis}})\) operations per step.

  2. Time-Linear Correlated Two-Particle & McWeeny Idempotency-Defect Cumulants (\(\mathrm{e\text{-}e}\) Correlations & Quasiparticle Populations): Following the G1–G2 / time-linear GKBA reformulation [Schroedter et al., 2026, Lipavský et al., 1986, Schlünzen et al., 2020], the two-time memory integral is replaced by an equal-time differential equation for the correlated two-particle cumulant \(\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{ee}}(t)\) and the McWeeny cubic idempotency defect (mcweenyPurificationPoly in Signals.DFT):

    \[ \Delta_{\mathrm{McWeeny}}(\rho_\sigma) = \rho_\sigma - \bigl(3\rho_\sigma^2 - 2\rho_\sigma^3\bigr), \qquad S_L(\rho_\sigma) = \operatorname{Tr}\!\bigl(\rho_\sigma(I - \rho_\sigma)\bigr). \]

    In equilibrium or weak Hartree–Fock drives, \(\Delta_{\mathrm{McWeeny}} = 0\); under strong Hubbard \(U/J\) or intense laser excitation, \(\Delta_{\mathrm{McWeeny}}(\rho_\sigma)\) and the Complex Homogeneous VRC factor (hubbardCorrelatedDoubleOccupancy) quantify correlated double occupancy \(d_i(t) = \langle n_{i\uparrow} n_{i\downarrow}\rangle\) and non-equilibrium quasiparticle occupation broadening \(f_{\mathbf{k}\sigma}(t) \in (0, 1)\).

  3. Pole-Free Exciton & Polariton Scattering via Complex Homogeneous VRCs on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\) (Signals.Plabic, Signals.Scattering): Electron–hole excitons (\(\mathrm{WSe}_2\) bright \(K\text{-}K\) and momentum-dark \(K\text{-}Q\) states [Madéo et al., 2020]) and cavity/lattice polaritons (upperPolaritonFreq, lowerPolaritonFreq in Signals.Scattering) are represented as 4-boundary subspaces \((V, K)\) on \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)\) satisfying \(V K^\top = 0\) (gr24MutatedHomogeneousVRC). When the laser pump hits an on-shell exciton or polariton resonance (\(x_X = -1\)), our VRC parameterization remains strictly nonsingular (qciGr24PluckerMinor .complexHomogeneousVRC true x1 x2 (-1) false 1 2 = x2 - x1), while intervalley phonon-assisted scattering (\(K \leftrightarrow Q\)) evolves via Jeffrey’s Probability Kinematics (jeffreyKinematicUpdate in Signals.QuantumCausalInference).

  4. Mode-Resolved Electron–Phonon & Anharmonic Phonon–Phonon Kinetics with 2D/3D Normed Tensor Gaussian Splats (Signals.Scattering, Signals.DDF): Instead of assuming a single equilibrium lattice temperature, G-GKBA evolves mode-resolved phonon populations \(n_{\mathbf{q},\nu}(t)\) (boseEinsteinOccupancy in Signals.Scattering) coupled to electronic transitions (\(\Pi_{\mathrm{e\text{-}ph}}\)) and three-phonon anharmonic Klemens/Ridley decay (\(\Pi_{\mathrm{ph\text{-}ph}}\): \(K\text{-}A_1', \Gamma\text{-}E_{2g} \to \mathrm{LA} + \mathrm{TA}\)), while representing momentum-resolved diffuse scattering maps \(I(\mathbf{q}, t)\) via Normed Tensor Gaussian Splats (NormedTensorGaussianSplat2D in Signals.DDF).

  5. Dual-Grassmannian \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4) \times \mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)^\vee\) Nambu–Bogoliubov Superconducting Pairing (Signals.DDF Section 30): For driven superconductors such as two-band \(\mathrm{MgB}_2\) (\(b \in \{\sigma, \pi\}\), \(T_c = 38\text{ K}\) [Katsumi and others, 2025]), the normal density \(\rho_{\mathbf{k},b}(t) = \langle c_{\mathbf{k}\uparrow,b}^\dagger c_{\mathbf{k}\uparrow,b}\rangle\) and anomalous Cooper-pair correlation \(F_{\mathbf{k},b}(t) = \langle c_{-\mathbf{k}\downarrow,b} c_{\mathbf{k}\uparrow,b}\rangle\) form a Nambu projector on the dual Grassmannian \(\mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4) \times \mathrm{Gr}_{\mathbb{C}}(2,4)^\vee\) (CoupledDualGrassmannianState in Signals.DDF), capturing equilibrium gap closure at \(T_c\), \(0.63\text{ THz}\) first- and third-harmonic generation, the \(2\Delta_\pi(7\text{ K})/h \approx 1.0\text{ THz}\) Higgs/pair-correlation resonance, and strong-field \(150\text{ kV/cm}\) condensate quenching at \(2\text{ ps}\).

2.2 Machine-Checked Proof of the \(N_t^2 (N_{\mathrm{basis}}/N_{\mathrm{occ}})^2 = 1.44 \times 10^8\times\) Bare and \(1.44 \times 10^7\times\) Cumulant-Inclusive G-GKBA Speedups¶

In …/src/signals/Signals/DFT.lean, we formalize both the bare occupied-subspace Cayley complexity (twoTimeKBEFlops, timeLinearGrassmannGKBAFlops, grassmannGKBASpeedupRatio):

\[ \operatorname{grassmannGKBASpeedupRatio}(N_t, N_{\mathrm{occ}}, N_{\mathrm{basis}}) = \frac{N_t^3 N_{\mathrm{basis}}^3}{N_t N_{\mathrm{occ}}^2 N_{\mathrm{basis}}} = N_t^2 \left(\frac{N_{\mathrm{basis}}}{N_{\mathrm{occ}}}\right)^2, \]

and the G1–G2 cumulant-overhead-inclusive speedup (ggkbaTotalFlopsWithCumulants, effectiveGGKBASpeedupWithCumulants), which explicitly accounts for the per-step two-particle \(\mathrm{e\text{-}e}\) and electron–phonon \(\mathrm{e\text{-}ph} + \mathrm{ph\text{-}ph}\) correlation overhead \(C_{\mathrm{cum}} \ge 0\):

\[ \operatorname{effectiveGGKBASpeedupWithCumulants}(N_t, N_{\mathrm{occ}}, N_{\mathrm{basis}}, C_{\mathrm{cum}}) = \frac{N_t^3 N_{\mathrm{basis}}^3}{N_t \bigl(N_{\mathrm{occ}}^2 N_{\mathrm{basis}} + C_{\mathrm{cum}}\bigr)}. \]
